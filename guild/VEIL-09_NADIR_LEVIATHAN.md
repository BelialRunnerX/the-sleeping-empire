# VEIL-09 — The Nadir Leviathan

**Threat:** The Nadir Leviathan  
**Classification:** SSS-level provisional existential/megafauna hazard; not verified hostile  
**Last observed:** Veilward Expanse, interstellar space  
**Estimated full length:** 42 kilometers, from long-range survey imagery; approximate, not verified close range  
**Appearance:** Colossal black-crystalline serpentine creature, six luminous fins, asteroid-like formation about its head, rows of interior glowing cavities along the spine. No confirmed anatomy, metabolism, sentience, allegiance or intent beyond remote silhouette and movement.
**Evidence:** At least three survey groups claimed distant imaging; none obtained close enough contact to certify its intelligence, hostility, vulnerability, or even all imaging details. Not an established boss fight yet.
**Status:** **RESTRICTED THREAT FILE / OBSERVATION ONLY — NO GUILD CONTRACT, NO BOUNTY, NO ACTIVE HUNT AUTHORIZATION.**
**First revealed:** [Turn 000262](../saves/turns/000262.md), Mira responding to Elys asking whether the Guild has anything bigger than the Pallbearer King.

## Guild position
The Guild has no lawful or ethical grounds to issue a hunting contract solely on a creature's size. Territoriality, intelligence, sapience, life-state, fleet hazards and threat to local populations are unknown. Authorization for investigation, identification, containment or defense would need a specific review and scoped mission. Do not automatically depict the Leviathan as an evil monster, a confirmed hostile boss, or a defeated/owned entity. Elys has not traveled there or attacked it. The SSS threat classification does not add a guild rank above SSS.

## Comparison with SSS-004
The earlier [Sepulcher Between Stars contract](SSS-004_SEPULCHER_BOSS_DOSSIER.md) was **COMPLETED, Guild-verified and paid ₡14M in turn 000268**, then closed. Its ~900m Pallbearer King outer body and later deeper threats were defeated; this has no operational relationship to the 42km-long Leviathan. Physical size is not a reliable proxy for power. Nadir Leviathan remains a separate observation, without a hunting commission.

## Reanalysis of existing survey imagery — turn 000274

At Guild reception, Mira gives Elys a **newer comparative ANALYSIS of the same original three distant VEIL-09 survey sightings**, not a fourth close encounter and not proof of hostile intent. [Turn 000274](../saves/turns/000274.md):

- Large asteroids apparently move aside before the dark crystalline Leviathan reaches them. Possible gravitational manipulation, defense field or observational distortion; **mechanism UNKNOWN**.
- Multiple luminous cavities along the back pulse in a repeating six-beat sequence followed by a pause. Hypotheses range from biological rhythms/energy regulation to deliberate communications; **no intelligible message proven**.
- A previously recorded **unmanned survey probe** captured what looked like the creature turning toward its locality, then stars appearing at incompatible bearings and the Leviathan **disappearing from the camera image**. The probe later recovered; **no verified physical damage, attack or crewed casualty**. Its own corrupted navigation means disappearance could be an illusion.
- Aletheia notices that navigational distortion precedes the apparent disappearance, which **could** mean the Leviathan is reacting to an external spatial phenomenon instead of causing it. **No proof of any other entity, predator, gate, fixed wormhole, or malicious cause**.

**Status unchanged:** provisional SSS-scale hazard, **intent and life-state UNKNOWN**, no posted contract/hunt/bounty, no Elys expedition. A lawful Guild **non-hunting survey/first-contact assessment** could be petitioned to senior Guild authorities, and higher-resolution observatory data might exist, but receptionist Mira has **not issued an authorization or commission**. Do not force the creature to be evil or auto-start a battle because of Aletheia's gleefully predatory banter.

Following Elys's explicit **request for authorization only** (choice 1 in 000274), receptionist Mira forwarded a new **SSS non-hunting reconnaissance & first-contact proposal** to **Senior Expedition Marshal Ilyra Venn**. The Marshal reviewed Elys's SSS certification and completed Sepulcher, and approved **`VEIL-09-R The Veilward Watch`**, proposed base fee **₡9,000,000**, intended to observe, interpret six-pulse lights, investigate the spatial distortion and attempt safe voluntary first contact, with reporting to appropriate navigation authorities. **THIS IS AN APPROVED MISSION OFFER, NOT YET ACCEPTED, ASSIGNED OR PAID**; Elys is still physically at Mira's counter, with no active mission or deployment. See [offer dossier](VEIL-09-R_THE_VEILWARD_WATCH.md) and [turn 000275](../saves/turns/000275.md).

**Unchanged evidentiary and ethical limits:** VEIL-09 is **not a Guild-approved hunting target**; hostility and sapience unconfirmed, no lethal action based on size alone, defensive force only upon actual credible threat. Survey groups=3, no verified new sighting, exact location and external spatial cause unknown; the old surviving unmanned probe remains the only particular probe event detailed. Aletheia's bloodthirsty commentary is not evidence of an attack. The prior Sepulcher base reward was settled once and is unrelated.


## Actual first direct observation — turn 000282

Elys and Aletheia travel to Veilward observation region after authorized `VEIL-09-R` deployment. Find damaged civilian `Pilgrim's Rest`, rescue three living occupants after six hostile salvage ships attack and are all disabled. Safely awakened navigator Seris Thane reports first-hand that **Nadir Leviathan appeared to position its body between the injured civilian craft and a dangerous spatial storm**, and pulsed six times repeatedly. It **may have been protecting the ship**, but motive and sapience unverified. She saw movement within spatial anomaly without confirming separate hostile being. Seris voluntarily gives Elys a harmonics recorder `ITM-000005`, which identifies a **second signal distinct from known six-pulse**. At end of [turn 000282](../saves/turns/000282.md), Elys and Aletheia **actually visually see Leviathan alive at distance** and turning toward the second signal. No attack against it, no full contact or Guild victory. [Scientific instrument](../armory/instruments/ITM-000005_SIXFOLD_HARMONIC_RESONATOR.md). Mission remains **ACTIVE**, ₡9M unpaid.
