5. Return with auditable evidence and report to the Guild. **No base reward due until acceptance and verified completion.**

Destroying the core outright could erase original knowledge and destabilize local topology; disabling, containing, reprogramming or separating it are possible solutions **if found feasible in play**. The true phase and final victory condition must be discovered, not automatically guaranteed by predictions.
