# IV · Commerce, Industry & the Somnus Portfolio
**Somnus** operates under executive overseer **Lethe**. Its portfolio includes Wheatley Storage & Shipping, industrial and mining ventures under SMITE, Apophatic technology development, and affiliated intelligence activity as established in later saves.

The original **22 WSS freighters** were commissioned. **Thirteen Mnemosyne computing worlds** were completed. Earlier market-reach estimates were date-specific company reports and must not be presented as present audited totals.

## Boundaries
- Bellweather retains cooperative independence under nonexclusive arrangements.
- Clarissa's Andromeda, imperial public assets, the Treasury, public military and Nimbus are not automatically Somnus property.
- A future social/public transfer of Somnus is an expressed long-term intention, **not yet an accomplished handover**.
- The Somnus immunity decree remains **unratified**.
