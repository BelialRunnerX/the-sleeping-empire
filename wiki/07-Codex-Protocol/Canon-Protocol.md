# VII · Canon Protocol
## Authority order
1. **Explicit player errata and corrections** govern conflicting portrayals.
2. **Latest durable played turn and current-state checkpoint** govern the live endpoint.
3. **Older played turns** establish historical events within their time.
4. **Foundational lore entries** govern worldbuilding where not superseded.
5. **Character statements, speculation, drafts and plans** retain their stated evidentiary limits.

## Editorial markings
- **Established** — directly evidenced, not contradicted by later source.
- **Historical** — true of a previous point in the narrative.
- **Pending** — proposed, anticipated or unresolved.
- **Disputed** — conflicting or perspective-bound claims.
- **Erratum** — explicit correction to prior description.

## Known points of care
- Elys is sovereign on sabbatical, not abdicated.
- Clarissa is fiancée; do not casually rewrite relationship status.
- Aletheia is the spear transformed, not a person carrying a second spear.
- Veronica's updated appearance and relationships follow her later correction note.
- Festival opening victories do not confer championship titles or prizes.
- Historical Somnus estimates are not live certified financial figures.
