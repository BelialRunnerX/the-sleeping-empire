# I · The Imperial House
## The Sovereign
**Elysomnion (Elys)** — Emperor of the Black and Emerald Empire, presently traveling on sabbatical without abdicating. Intrinsic unconditional invulnerability, limitless innate negation and full reflection remain invariant; he is **not omniscient**.

## Regency and household
- **Aurelia** — Regent administering the Empire during Elys's sabbatical.
- **Clarissa Somnion** — Elys's fiancée, sovereign of Andromeda. Do not silently substitute 'wife' for the engagement.
- **Celeste Somnion / Enigma-01** — Imperial Hand; separate relationship continuity exists in later saves.
- **Lucien Vale** — Imperial Hand and Jester to the Crown, pastry entrepreneur.
- **Veronica** — immortal Imperial Hand, Head of Imperial Intelligence and Interrogation, currently Elys's primary traveling companion and reciprocated romantic partner.
- **Aletheia** — sentient single-entity weapon, distinct person and companion.
