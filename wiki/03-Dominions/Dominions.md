# III · Dominions, Worlds & Holdings
## Imperial and private jurisdiction
The Empire, Clarissa's Andromeda, the Nimbus family residence, and privately held Somnus assets are **legally distinct**. Do not combine them into a single corporate balance sheet.

## Named places
- **Lumenfall / Veyrath** — Sevenfold Arena, site of current festival opening rounds.
- **Bellwynn / Greenhaven** — earlier harvest and innovation festival.
- **Nimbus** — completed private family estate, not the Somnus corporate headquarters.
- **Caldris II** — industrial and agricultural development associated with early Somnus expansion.
- **Andromeda** — Clarissa's sovereign dominion.
