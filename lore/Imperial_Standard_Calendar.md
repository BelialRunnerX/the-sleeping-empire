# Imperial Standard Calendar

**Canonical current date at Chapter XLVIII (Caldris II / Aurelia's birthday-gift correspondence):** 17 Emberwane, Year 13,427 of the Imperial Concord (ICE).

**Formal date:** The Seventeenth Day of Emberwane, in the 13,427th Year of the Imperial Concord.

The Imperial Concord Era reckons years from the foundation of the unified Sleeping Empire following the end of the great interstellar wars. ICE is the standard calendar for this story going forward. No length of month or other month names have yet been canonically fixed. The birthday of Emperor Elys is NOT yet established; the present date should not be interpreted as his birthday.

Planning reference: 100 years later = 13,527 ICE; 250 = 13,677 ICE; 500 = 13,927 ICE; 1,000 = 14,427 ICE; 2,000 = 15,427 ICE.
