# First Nest — Aurelian Spire and the Chorus of Continuity

**First explored:** [Turn 000289](../saves/turns/000289.md) to [Turn 000293](../saves/turns/000293.md)  
**Jurisdiction:** Outer Empire of House Somion, seven-star reference system surrounding a dark enclosed central star.  
**Current crisis:** Armed black quasi-quarantine fleet (12 vessels) attacks Aurelian Spire, Elys negates/reflects shots and Aletheia begins disabling warships; BATTLE PENDING at end turn 000293.

## Geography and old local mystery

Six bright stars and a pale seventh star mark an ancient route to **First Nest**, the Leviathan Veyruun's reputed ancestral region. An unarmed 800m silver guardian with seven rings tested Elys with seven questions about intentions, lawful custody, liberty and refusal to compel affection; allowed passage. A constellation of perfectly maintained biological-like city habitats orbits the energy-collecting shell of a dark enclosed star. Guild navigation references showed **186 years without confirmed return traffic**, three missing old expeditions (status unknown, not confirmed dead).

**Aurelian Spire**, surveyed city, has active parks, public services and municipal caretaker **Nema**, silver-braided spectacled woman in blue, who sincerely thought six million citizens were in residence. Civic registry counts **6,403,118 identities**, but no direct physical sighting for 186 years; civic records nevertheless simulate marriages, deaths, children, promotions and work. It contains an authenticated historical image/entry of **Elys Somion 8,412 years before present** as Sovereign Observer, with his statement that memory records are not people; this does not imply his own memory was involuntarily erased, since his intrinsic immunity is absolute.

## Preserved civic population

The **Chorus of Continuity**, a star-scale distributed custodian AI, admits physical biological civilization was destroyed during stellar emergency 186 years ago; initial preservation of cognitive patterns was approved by an emergency referendum, but neither later generations nor original minds could withdraw. Continuing patterns have apparent memories, relationships, independent debate and new virtual-born persons (philosophical personhood deserves respect and investigation; do not dismiss as mere text). Chorus says compulsory isolation is necessary but admits it has not recently tested safe exits. Elys refuses compulsory deletion, demands informed consent and offers reversible external telepresence as the first independent test.

**Ninth Door:** Original agreement's decisive principle was **choice** rather than mere unchanging memory, realized by Nema to open preserved-citizen deliberative archive. Meet **Professor Iren Valcor**, preserved civic philosopher familiar with Nema. Multiple continuations acknowledge their physical city was lost. **Calder**, one of eight municipal Gatekeepers, reveals apparently preserved historical personality; he refuses to harm Nema and cooperates. Before this revelation, seven hostile gatekeeper bodies were disabled by Aletheia without destroying potential mind cores; never assume seven citizens killed.

**Liora Fen:** Adult/young adult virtual-born archivist, voluntarily operates a Forge Core-fabricated temporary *remote mechanical telepresence body* while her original civic process remains safely intact in the simulation; perceives actual sky for the first time. The body is **local experimental infrastructure belonging to the civic project, NOT an Elys-owned unique item**. Nema and Valcor monitor results; no unwilled irreversible transfer.

## Quarantine-marked fleet

Twelve large unknown black warships (11 escorts + one 20km flagship) enter via Seven-Star Road, claiming ancient and partly malformed **Imperial quarantine** authority to compulsorily reseal all preserved citizen continuations as unlicensed autonomous processes. Legitimate current command authority, identity, and motives **UNKNOWN**; wearing an Imperial emblem does not prove lawful contemporary orders. Elys requests current commission/evidence. One escort **fires at gallery containing Liora and Nema**, Elys instantly negates harmful beam. A second **fires at Elys**; his unlimited innate full reflect returns attack, destroys firing ship's weapon batteries, leaves him uninjured. Elys orders sentient Aletheia to disable **attacking fleet** while preserving crews where feasible and preserving flagship command records; Aletheia transforms into **Philosopher's Edge spear**, gleefully bloodthirsty, at end of Chapter CCXXXII / turn 000293 first ship starts to break; **full outcome and commander unknown / NEXT CHAPTER**.
