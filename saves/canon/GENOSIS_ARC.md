# The Genosis Arc: standing continuity

**Genosis**, G-E-N-O-S-I-S, is the planet’s name. This is part of The Sleeping Empire, not the separate Crown and the Crow story.

## Identity and stakes

Elys voluntarily caused his human incarnation to be reborn at random when he disappeared. All his powers and memories persisted. Taking breaks from rule is in character, as in the earlier undercover school period. His new life was genuinely that of an Alderford ferryman’s son, not a royal upbringing. His parents are truly his parents and raised him; their child’s body is a vessel Elys can exist apart from. Hazel Alderford is the hometown and family name; **Crow** is the registered and ordinary introduction elsewhere. **Crow the Mischievous** is an honorary decree title. **Elys Somnion** is the exact witnessed name used on the notice, not a general public announcement that Crow is the Emperor.

He is immortal and invulnerable from birth, grows into adulthood, joins the guild at eighteen, and stops aging at twenty-three. Innate absolute defense, negation and reflection are not conditional on gear; Elys is not omniscient. The twelve publicly worshipped gods are distinct from the undisclosed thirteenth. Narration can know a fact without granting it to any character.

The privately recovered pen, everyday-clothes armor and resource pouch are pre-owned, not newly earned divine gifts. The pen’s staff and spear forms and the armor are not sentient. This pen is not the one sentient historical Aletheia. Do not assume Aletheia is on the current flagship. Base stats and specific current slots remain unknown unless established. The Genosis arc’s intended close is the Emperor’s return to annex the world he changed. Aurelia’s classification decree was already issued and read to the Senate; it claims Imperial authority while retaining local offices. Do not erase that act or equate it with a fully implemented political settlement or the final arc culmination.

## Continuity boundaries

- The festival arc stays intact as historical canon. The current archive supplies no missing scene bridging from the festival directly to Alderford.
- Local port watch, sovereign Xiphos, the League Senate/executive, and the Imperial First Fleet are separate authorities. No alias or role is a new person.
- Keep written orders, verified hearings and actual votes separate from proposals, private knowledge, and jokes. The harbour emergency/warrants examples before the later vote were policy exercises.
- The prior magical-door alternatives aboard the personal ship are excluded. No magical door or unsolicited modification belongs on Elys’s personal ships. A strange external beacon was discussed as a possible prompt only.

The player explicitly rewound all events aboard the flagship after Crow took up residence. Only its standing watch and his quiet hiatus remain. Current Senate business proceeds with Aurelia; no events from the rejected alternatives inform canon.

**Native baseline:** Ordinary Genosis technology is roughly comparable to the 1300s–1400s. No sci-fi underwater cities. Offworld capability does not imply native origin; apparent exceptions remain provenance questions, not license to invent or retroactively delete.
