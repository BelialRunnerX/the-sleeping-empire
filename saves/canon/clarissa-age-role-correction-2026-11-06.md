# Clarissa — authoritative continuity correction

**This note supersedes contradictory descriptions in earlier chapters, summaries or character biographies.**

- **Age:** Clarissa is only around **two centuries old**, not thousands of years old and not an ancient companion who predates the academy arc.
- **Origin with Elys:** She was **a school/academy student with Elys approximately 200 years ago**.
- **Gifts:** Elys gifted her **immortality and Andromeda**.
- **Public/political responsibility:** She acts as **an advisor to the Senate on behalf of Lilith**. Her Senate role is important alongside her responsibilities involving Andromeda. Avoid replacing this with invented independent ancient service.
- **Relationship:** Clarissa is **Elys's fiancée**, not his wife. They share a private Nimbus residence; she joins him incognito as **Mara Calder** during his sabbatical.
- **Story implications:** Clarissa has two centuries of lived experience, not millennia. Her reminiscences about first meeting Elys should refer to their time as fellow students at the academy 200 years ago. She is immortal because **he gifted her immortality**. Do not narrate her as being alive before that time.
