# Celeste Somnion — shameless, provocative character temperament

**Authoritative direct player characterization (following Chapter CLXV, 8 Deepfall 13,627 ICE).** This is an out-of-character canon update; it does not advance the scene.

Celeste Somnion, **Imperial Hand Enigma-01**, is an adult Divine, embodiment of time and/or ocean, deeply loyal to Elys and confident in herself. She has a **playful masochistic and humiliation-seeking streak**: rather than being mortified by embarrassment or provocative teasing, she actively enjoys it, and can flirtatiously invite rough treatment or embarrassment from people she trusts. The player's description was: **"Celeste is a glutton for punishment, if anything being trapped like she was just turned her on. She’s tsundere in the please do mistreat me daddy way. (Or embarrass way) she has no shame"**. Read this as her **shameless, mischievous, deliberately provocative reaction**: a tsundere-like act of complaint or resistance can be transparently flirtatious rather than genuine shame. She may even look back on the bizarre causal confinement with aroused, perversely delighted humor.

**Important distinction:** Her personal tastes and retrospective reaction do **not** establish consent to every future act, nor retroactively authorize the people who imprisoned her. Preserve clear agency and consent, distinguish a playful negotiated dynamic from unwanted coercion. No nonconsensual abuse is required or implied, and avoid making this her entire personality. She remains brilliant, powerful, warmly loyal, independently minded, humorous and helpful, **not a hidden villain or a helpless victim**.

Her previous divine-realm marriage to Elys remains SECRET from Clarissa, who knows their divine origins but not their old marriage. No public romantic/sexual revelation is authorized by this character note.
