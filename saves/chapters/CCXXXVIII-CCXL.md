# Chapters CCXXXVIII–CCXL — First Intercept

**Story round:** turn [000299](../turns/000299.md). **Date:** 13 Deepfall 13,627 ICE approximate.

**CCXXXVIII — The Road That Moves Between Stars:** From the Quiet Crown, Elys and Aletheia follow the first of seven provisional intercept regions using the stored Horizon Needle and Sixfold Harmonic Resonator for readings. Four hostile unmanned defense vessels attack an 18 km transfer platform; Elys's intrinsic negation/reflection leave him entirely unharmed, and Aletheia gleefully disables the vessels without destroying recoverable archives. Emergency transmission begs not to be moved.

**CCXXXIX — The Cargo That Could Speak:** Two survivors among the historic 23 Light-Born transfer subjects discovered in capsules, one conscious and nameless, one medically unstable; the station had coerced memory regression and used them as live navigation phase references. Elys negates forced memory effects, respects medical requirements and rejects ownership claims. Auditor recognizes Imperial sovereign and orders station destroyed.

**CCXL — The Auditor's Mistake:** All hostile life-support sabotage/data deletion attempts negated by Elys. Aletheia destroys automated sentries, preserves communications buffers. Engineering record shows a proposed mechanical alternative rejected on cost/schedule grounds. Auditor claims cosmic darkness justifies the Engine, not independently verified. Elys prepares protected transport cradle; Matriarch acknowledges care request but survivors remain on-site at endpoint. New Asterion phase-schedule and unidentified dark stellar-halo object point toward the next intercept. Aletheia looks forward to fighting worthy hostiles.
