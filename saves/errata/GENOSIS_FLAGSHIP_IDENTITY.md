# Genosis: the identity of the First Fleet flagship

**Author clarification, 25 November 2026.** The vessel that the Eleventh Fleet faced in its exercise was Emperor Elysomnion’s own flagship. The older [Lillith dossier](../../Lillith.md#notes) identifies the opposing vessel as **The Indomitable**. This is the same First Fleet super-dreadnought that Elysomnion deployed near Greyhaven Orbital in [turn 000351](../turns/000351.md).

Descriptions of the vessel as unnamed in the archived played turns reflect that its name was not stated there, not a second vessel or a different identity. The original turns and the [flagship hiatus rewind](GENOSIS_FLAGSHIP_HIATUS.md) stand. This clarification does not give in-universe characters new knowledge, name the captain, move the ship, or advance the scene.
