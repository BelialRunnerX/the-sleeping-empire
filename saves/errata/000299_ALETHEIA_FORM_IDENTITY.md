# Errata — Aletheia Is the Spear, Not Its Separate Wielder

**Corrects:** Narration from [turn 000299](../turns/000299.md), displayed chapters CCXXXVIII–CCXL.  
**Source:** User's explicit subsequent correction (verbatim):

> Errata Aletheia is the spear so there’s nothing to rest against her shoulders. When she’s in human form that’s the spear transformed.

## Binding canon clarification

- `ITM-000001 Aletheia / Prima Materia Apophatica` is **one singular sentient physical entity and item**; the three manifestation forms **Materia humanoid**, **Philosopher's Edge spear**, and **Genesis Loom staff** are mutually exclusive transformations of that *same being*.
- **Materia is the spear transformed into humanoid form**. When Aletheia is human-shaped, no separate Philosopher's Edge spear remains for her to carry, summon alongside herself, sling over a shoulder, grip, rest against her shoulder or throw. Her humanoid body itself is the transformed weapon.
- In spear mode, Aletheia **is the spear**, not an additional human standing nearby holding it. She may speak and act through her sentience as already established. When she returns to humanoid shape, that same spear transforms back; **there is never a duplicate body or extra weapon instance**.
- The prose of chapters CCXXXVIII–CCXL describing humanoid Aletheia with “her spear resting over one shoulder,” “taps the floor with her spear,” “drives her spear,” or “produces the Philosopher's Edge” while maintaining human form is corrected to describe a **complete, single-body transformation into spear form to strike**, followed by a complete reversion to humanoid form when a humanoid gesture occurs. Mentions of “resting a spear on her shoulder” have no literal canonical occurrence.
- At the final beat of CCXL, the statement “She produces the Philosopher's Edge” means **Aletheia transforms into the Philosopher's Edge** and remains a single spear-shaped companion poised beside Elys; the prior simultaneous humanoid-plus-separate-spear visual is discarded. Her final spoken words are unchanged.
- Combat outcomes, rescued people, intentions, credits, mission status, established locations and elapsed time remain unchanged. **No new item, extra character, second spear, equipment transfer or additional action is created.**
- This is a **non-narrative correction**, not a new played scene or numbered turn. The immutable historical save 000299 is preserved for provenance, while this errata controls all future portrayal.

**Authority:** Explicit player correction overrides the preceding inaccurate prose.
