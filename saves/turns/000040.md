# Turn 000040 — The Superintelligence Challenge

Player: Lethe I want you to show me what a super intellect like you can do in a short amount of time, I want to see sectors revolutionized in days. And yes this means I want a planet sized server farm.

Elys instructs Lethe to demonstrate accelerated multi-industry change within days, and authorizes planning for a planet-sized computing facility. Lethe's initiative should use verified capital, respect consent, law and property rights, and distinguish research, prototypes and contracted trial operations from completed megastructures or mature production. This turn directly leads to the three-day industrial campaign played in turn 000041.
