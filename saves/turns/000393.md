# Turn 000393 — The Last Suggestion

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** League Senate chamber on Genosis.  
**Player action:** Authorize the montage to the last of the 35 Senate seats, with 17 senators dead and the chair gravely injured before the final verdict; no additional ruling or action.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

By the time the last senator stands, seventeen are dead. The chair is still alive, gravely wounded, and Tessa has stayed at her side. The clerk has stopped turning the pages back to check his tally. He knows it now.

The last senator is from a mountain nation. He has spent the better part of the afternoon with a speech folded inside his coat. He takes it out, looks at it, and sets it aside.

“Let the villages train their own healers,” he says. “My district has one physician. On a good horse it takes him two days to reach the high villages. They have women and men who already tend the sick. Pay to bring one from each valley down for a winter’s training, then pay them to go home and teach someone else.”

He takes a second paper from his coat. It’s a list of names, with a village written next to each.

“The valleys can provide lodging and firewood. The League would pay the instructors and cover the work the trainees miss while they’re away. I have a cost for three valleys. Start there. If people can get help sooner, the districts can decide whether to do it again.”

He puts the list on the table. One name has been crossed out and written back in, in a different hand.

“She changed her mind when she heard we might cover her wages,” he says. “She has two children. She couldn’t afford to come for free.”

He steps away from the table so the clerk can reach the list. Then he waits for your verdict.
