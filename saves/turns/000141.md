# Turn 000141 — The Door That Remembers Tomorrow

**Chapter CXLV, 7 Deepfall 13,627 ICE ~17:54–17:58, Old Eastern Foundations, Pelagia, Tideglass.**

Player selected **1: Try the Unwelcome Key** and expressly **recommended that Clarissa and Davin remain behind**. Elys, disguised as civilian Corin Vale, tells the companions he intends to investigate alone. **Davin Rusk** immediately agrees to remain on the rain-soaked pressure-lift platform. **Clarissa** (alias Mara Calder) expresses concern about the undersea temporal anomaly but voluntarily agrees to wait with Davin and keep communication open. They have NOT entered the passage. **Vesta**, the private household AI rather than Sentinel, keeps a discreet private audio link.

Elys brings his purchased **Unwelcome Key** (with three-interlocking-circles mark) toward the supposedly flooded, restricted **Foundation Maintenance Passage 09** door. Though no keyhole is initially visible, a narrow opening unfolds from the solid surface. Elys inserts and turns the key. The local acoustic field becomes silent despite the still-visible falling rain; the key makes three clicks and **the maintenance door opens**. Inside is an **impossibly dry black corridor** with fine silver luminous filaments, cold-stone and ozone smell, ticking machinery, and a bend concealing its interior. The Wayward Compass points into the passage. This door is **NOT verified to be the warned-about third door**.

Elys steps into the corridor alone while Clarissa and Davin remain outside, visible through the open door. A wall clock activates and displays **06:14**, the reported time the three researchers entered the old complex the previous day. Vesta detects **two incompatible timestamps** at Elys's location: present day and a date **11 years in the future**, without knowing which is meaningful. From within, Elys sees **rain outside apparently fall upward**, though Clarissa sees normal rain. A human-sounding woman pleads for rescue from further inside.

Elys progresses around the corridor bend and meets **Dr. Elara Venn**, a visibly distressed woman in a damaged, rust-stained Tideglass Archaeological Institute expedition suit, short roughly trimmed hair, a bandaged forearm, and a nameplate reading **TEMPORAL SYSTEMS**. She is alive and frightened, says she thought Elys might be another recording, asks if the exit is truly the surface, and urges him to leave with her. When she sees his Unwelcome Key, she says: **"That key doesn't open doors. It decides which side of them you're on."** A heavy unseen thing moves farther inside the ruins; a bell tolls three times. The **open entrance briefly flickers**, showing an **empty rusted chamber** instead of Clarissa and Davin, before returning to the actual platform. Vesta suspects the structure is dynamically changing the connection between the entrance and its destination.

**Immediate endpoint:** Corin/Elys is inside the anomalous black corridor with **Dr. Elara Venn**, who reaches toward him, requesting escape before the doorway changes again. Clarissa and Davin are still outside and unharmed with a presently stable private audio link, though the doorway is unstable. The other **two researchers' positions and conditions are unknown**.
