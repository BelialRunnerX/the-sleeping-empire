## Played scene

You settle into your quarters aboard the super-dreadnought. You’ve left the Senate to conduct its business with Aurelia. The flagship keeps its watch, and you have the quiet you came for.

Down on Genosis, Tessan’s senator arrives at the chamber with forty-seven fishing licence renewals in a canvas bag. She tips them onto the clerk’s desk.

“They’re paper,” the clerk says.

“Yes. On paper you can write the name of a treaty even if the Empire has never heard of it.”

Aurelia joins by holo from Epsilon-7. An Imperial registrar sits beside her with the original treaty open on his desk. He points out that the treaty covers waters on both sides of a maritime boundary. The Empire can keep its inspectors from impounding the boats. It cannot get them unloaded in the other country’s ports without an agreement.

“We have one,” says Tessan’s senator. “The other port has set its market days by it for forty years. They’re asking me whether your inspectors can make us miss them.”

Aurelia asks when the first crew contracts are due. Thursday, the senator tells her. Aurelia tells the registrar to accept all forty-seven applications as filed today and send a receipt to each skipper. Existing licences will carry into next season while the review is open. The registrar starts to ask which treaty name to enter, then looks down at the cover in front of him.

From Vask’s bench, Tessa Vey raises her hand. “Can Nareth and Ordel get the same protection for their river agreement, or do they each have to turn up with a sack?”

“I’ll need the agreements and a list of the people who rely on them,” Aurelia says. “You may send them together.”

“And can we send them under the League’s seal?”

“Yes. I’ll answer under mine.”

By the time the chair calls the next item, the first receipt has reached Tessan. At a kitchen table above the quay, a skipper takes her crew contracts out of a drawer and puts yesterday’s date back on the first one. She’d promised to have it ready then.
