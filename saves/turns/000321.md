## Played record

An earlier survey crew on the river steps asks about roughly a thousand locally observed ships; Crow privately identifies himself in Iona’s presence and asks that they withdraw, which they do. Later, a charter invitation under a private Elys signature brings an envoy to the Senate. The visitor describes the civilian Outer Survey Service, the Empire’s backing, rescue and charting work, a chart without landing rights, and a separate contact tile. Epsilon Seven is a relay, not a treaty. Crow tells the chamber that Xiphos is an automated underground mining and forging operation, with surface agriculture, armed ships and automata. He lends the chair a read-only display and distinguishes his executive command from the Senate's power to set law. The later named Captain Theren is not assumed to be the previously unnamed speaker in all scenes.
