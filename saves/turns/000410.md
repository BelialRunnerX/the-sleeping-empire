# Turn 000410 — The Cost of Withdrawing

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** Inside the League Senate chamber, Greyhaven, Genosis.  
**Player action:** Crow places on record his warning that withdrawals would cripple the League and cost Genosis external representation.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

You interrupt before the clerk can turn the page.

“I’d like the record to show that you must have a full Senate in order for this body to function. If you withdraw your senators, you cripple the League. You also lose this planet’s representation to the greater world beyond you.”

The chair waits for the clerk to finish. “Read that back. All of it.”

He does.

Tessan’s senator turns to Nareth’s delegate. “That consequence is real. When the fishery comes up again, I want a League delegation with your signature beside mine. If I go to the regency alone, I can speak for forty-seven crews. I can’t speak for Genosis.”

“I know,” says Nareth’s delegate. “My assembly knew it too. That’s why it sent me back after Sera came home.” She sets her credentials flat on the table. “I can tell them exactly what leaving will cost. What am I supposed to tell the next person they send about getting home?”

Ordel’s senator rights his chair. “Give them something they can actually vote on. Ask the Emperor for his own undertaking that delegates can leave. We’ve spent the morning asking the Regent to promise what she says she can’t.”

The chair looks towards Aurelia. “Your instruction to your officers still matters. We’ll send it when you issue it.” Then she turns to you. “Crow, you’ve asked us to put the cost of withdrawing on the record. It’s there. Will you also give the delegates your word that they can leave this chamber freely?”
