At dinner in the Caldris farmhouse, Elys states a long-term personal intention to socialize the means of production. No specific mechanism or enacted transfer is chosen. Clarissa observes the distinction among cooperative ownership, public trusts, and government ownership, and predicts Regent Aurelia will question the governance implications. Aurelia has not learned of or answered this proposal on-screen.

Elys asks Clarissa to lead the planning and construction of their future home on jointly owned Nimbus while he completes his affairs on Caldris. She accepts enthusiastically, proposing a storm-viewing observatory, library, research spaces, protected courtyard and private domestic quarters, expressly not an Imperial palace. She begins site survey and architectural planning; construction awaits survey and design rather than being retroactively complete.

Elys considers inviting Veronica to corporate espionage. Sentinel contacts Veronica, who expresses interest in private-sector intelligence work and in the absurdity of the Emperor's farming cover. She notes that her sovereign intelligence office and public resources must remain distinct from Somnus's private commercial interests. She is open to a separately authorized advisory role concerning lawful threat intelligence, security and investigations, but no assignment has yet been accepted or scoped. Clarissa asks what kind of secrets Elys intends to acquire.
