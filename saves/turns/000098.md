# Turn 000098 — The Baker Who Outlived Time

**Time jump requested by player:** Two hundred years into the future, from 13,427 ICE to **5 Deepfall, 13,627 ICE**. At this point Somnus Enterprises, owned privately by Elys under his Hazael Wheatley identity and led by Lethe, has become the dominant economic backbone of galactic trade and industry, securing a galaxy-spanning infrastructure monopoly. Major integrated operations include freight, finance, warehousing, mining, industry, and planetary computing. Independent firms still exist but rely heavily on Somnus networks. The state has regulatory safeguards and oversight, while concentration of economic power remains substantial. These are story-world milestones established by the user's time skip, not retrospectively audited numerical claims.

Lucien's cream-puff-shaped original bakery on Epsilon 7 has operated across the intervening two centuries and remains independently run by Lucien, with multiple staff generations. On its 200th anniversary at approximately 10:02, Lucien examines historical photographs and realizes he has not aged in two centuries while generations of his employees have aged and died. He recalls a beautiful gift-box-shaped pastry his friend Hazael gave him on opening day and suspects that pastry may be connected to his agelessness. Lucien calls Hazael privately and directly asks what was in the gift pastry. **Lucien has not yet been told he is immortal, and he still does not know Hazael is Emperor Elys.** His immortality is the same form held by Aurelia, Bel, Clarissa, Veronica and Lilith, and Elys had earlier said Lucien could eventually choose mortality. The older advisers knew of Lucien's secret and were told by Elys to keep it confidential. No reply has yet been chosen by the player.
