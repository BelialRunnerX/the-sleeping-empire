# Turn 000121 — The Rematch Beneath Dead Stars

5 Deepfall 13,627 ICE, birthday evening. Elys accepts Aurelia's request for a meaningful full-power sparring match in a barren system, using only absolute null and reflection. Aurelia keeps all her abilities and strikes him once after adapting her tactics. Some barren planetoids are destroyed, with no inhabited worlds harmed. Elys then wins without seriously harming her. They return to Nimbus. Aurelia appreciates having been allowed to test her abilities. The gathered friends resume the birthday party. This contest is separate from the earlier Three-Second War.
