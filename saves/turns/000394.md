# Turn 000394 — When It Arrives

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** Sealed League Senate chamber, Greyhaven, Genosis.  
**Player action:** Crow explicitly requires everyone present, including non-senators, to offer a suggestion. He calls on the clerk.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

The mountain senator steps back from the table. His list of healers remains beside the clerk’s inkstand. You call on the clerk.

He looks up. He’s spent the afternoon writing down other people’s answers. He sets his pen in its groove and stands.

“Give every law an effective date,” he says. “And give each district a sealed copy before anyone can be held to it. I can write ‘effective immediately’ here in Greyhaven. A messenger still needs three days to reach some of the people those words bind.”

He opens the dispatch book and turns it so you can see the columns. “We already record when an order leaves. Add a place for the district to acknowledge when it arrives. If a court is asked to enforce the order, it can check the date. Use the messengers and books we have.”

His eyes go to the page of decisions he’s been filling all afternoon. “I can start with today’s orders,” he says. Then he waits.
