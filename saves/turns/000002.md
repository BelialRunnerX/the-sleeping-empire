**Caldris II · Wheatley Farmhouse · 21:12**

**Sentinel:** "Wheatley Storage & Shipping. Construction report, as requested. I have divided it into accomplishments, outstanding work, and opportunities to spend truly unreasonable amounts of money."

**WSS report:** 22 civilian freighters ordered, not delivered (six large trunk-route bulk carriers, twelve medium agricultural freighters, four feeder ships). **Sentinel:** "A surprisingly uncommon philosophy among commercial executives."

**Sentinel:** "Either approach has advantages. The second is less likely to leave you owning twenty-two magnificent ships whose principal cargo is disappointment."

**Clarissa:** "I rather like that description."
