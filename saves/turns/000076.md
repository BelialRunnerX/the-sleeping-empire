# Turn 000076 — The Regent's Last Straw

4 Deepfall 13,427 ICE, around 23:43, Nimbus greenhouse. Elys as Hazael privately messages Regent Aurelia: 'We've broken ground.' This refers to Somnus's legally authorized preliminary excavation for Lucien's future giant cream-puff-shaped bakery at Meridian Commons on Epsilon 7. Aurelia checks the project and finds the site work authorized, although broader design and operating approvals remain outstanding. She remarks that the project has begun earlier than the next-day joke Elys previously sent and asks whether any planet has been renamed for a pastry. Clarissa, Bel, Sentinel and Lethe find the continuing exchange amusing. Lucien mistakenly believes his bakery is already famous. Lucien now knows Hazael owns Somnus, not that Hazael is Emperor. The bakery is unbuilt and the proposed 10% net-profit commercial arrangement has not been signed.
