# Turn 000085 — The Third Decree

5 Deepfall 13,427 ICE, approximately 09:37, in front of Lucien's newly open cream-puff-shaped bakery on Epsilon 7. Elys, publicly Hazael Wheatley, secretly issues a third authenticated Empire-wide public proclamation using precisely the user's text: “don’t worry citizens I will personally see to it that her insides are rearranged properly later”. Its suggestive and medically alarming wording appears on Imperial public channels and above the bakery. Regent Aurelia objects explicitly to the apparent bodily implications, states that she is healthy and that nobody will make decisions about her body, and tells Elys to end such public remarks. Clarissa and Bel react with shock, laughter and concern; Sentinel makes dry remarks. At the Imperial archives, staff verify all three proclamations are genuine. Imperial Health Service reports no medical procedure is scheduled. Lucien is exhausted by the publicity and remains unaware that Hazael is Emperor Elys (although he knows Hazael owns Somnus). Lethe suggests separating the bakery's official branding from the sovereign's announcements. Aurelia privately promises to preserve all three authenticated declarations in the archives, circulate the day's transcript to the Imperial Small Enterprise Forum, and threatens a Senate committee on responsible Imperial stationery should another anatomy-related decree follow. The bakery remains open; Somnus's proposed 10% of qualifying net profit remains unsigned pending review; long-term wheat supply remains unresolved. No medical procedure or action against Aurelia has happened or been authorized. Await player action.
