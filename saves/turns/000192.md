### The Ocean in a Teacup

📍 **Lumenfall Free Adventurers' Guild, Veyrath** · **9 Deepfall, 13,627 ICE · Late afternoon** · Physical aptitude hall

Celeste steps into the testing lane, rolling her shoulders. Her silver-blue hair catches the light of the safety rails.

Garrick regards her over his tablet. “Same constraints as Elys Somion. No magic. The apparatus has a maximum permitted load. When I say stop, you stop.”

“How stern,” Celeste says. “I do appreciate a man who knows precisely what he wants.”

“An undamaged testing facility,” Garrick replies.

“Such modest ambitions.”

**Strength.** The hydraulic carriage starts at the standard resistance. Celeste shifts it a precisely measured twenty centimeters, then returns it without a clank. Garrick raises the load repeatedly. At the certified upper band, she raises one eyebrow and moves the carriage as delicately as if she were passing a sleeping kitten from one hand to another.

The load display shows **MAXIMUM CERTIFIED**. The underlying instrument has nothing further to say about what she could actually lift.

“May I try harder?” she asks.

“No.”

“Devastating.”

**Acceleration and reaction.** Light targets flash across the testing wall in a pseudorandom sequence. Celeste moves with a dancer's fluidity. She reaches the boundary faster than its timer can distinguish from the top certified band, but stops without so much as rattling a floor panel. The target contacts are feather-light.

**Coordination.** The balance platform rotates unexpectedly. One of the training arms drops low as another sweeps high. Celeste ducks, pivots and threads between them, her movements almost unnecessarily graceful. Then the platform issues a particularly undignified instruction: remain balanced on a narrow footplate while holding a padded practice weight above her head.

She obeys. For six seconds she resembles an exceptionally offended ballerina.

“I'm being outmaneuvered by gym equipment,” she announces.

Clarissa watches closely. “You could always ask it for a better dance.”

**Resilience.** The cushioned harness applies pressure in safe increments. Celeste reaches the approved limit without a change of expression. At the termination signal, she immediately relaxes against the supports. No bruising, deformation or safety trip is recorded.

**Controlled exertion.** Garrick presents the fragile glass cylinder. This time the grip test includes a shifting load and a deliberately deceptive force indicator. Celeste trusts the independent pressure sensors, holds the cylinder without cracking it, and releases it onto the receiving pad with exactly the prescribed force.

The glass survives. So does the guild.

Garrick reads the report. “Every instruction satisfied. No damage. No out-of-bounds movement. **Physical aptitude: one hundred out of one hundred.** Actual physical limit unknown.”

Celeste looks genuinely crestfallen. “Not a single deduction? After everything we've been through?”

“You are welcome to preserve the dignity of this institution by accepting your score.”

“Must I?”

“Yes.”

“A cruel and exacting examiner.”

Clarissa chuckles. But when Garrick beckons her forward, the amusement fades into concentration.

“**Clarissa Somnion. Final candidate.**”

She steps toward the strength apparatus. Its rated-load warning is illuminated. She has never been told the scale of the strength you quietly granted her, and her first instinct is still to approach the equipment as an ordinary human would.

Garrick holds out a calibration grip. “We'll begin gently. Squeeze only until the indicator turns green.”

Clarissa accepts it, waiting for the start signal.

For now, the grip remains uncompressed.
