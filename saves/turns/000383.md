# Turn 000383 — A Second Copy

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** League Senate chamber on Genosis.  
**Player action:** Say only “Approved. Next.”  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

“Approved. Next.”

Halden sits. The clerk enters the decision. Beside the line about market day, he adds a note for the district clerks to read the due date aloud with the amount.

The chair looks down the table. When she speaks, it’s the first time since the doors closed that she addresses the chamber from her feet.

“Keep a duplicate of every deed and will deposited with a district court. Send the copy to a neighbouring district. If a courthouse burns, people should still be able to prove what belongs to them.”

She opens a narrow folder. Inside is an appeal with three signatures and a patch of wax where a fourth seal should have been.

“This woman’s brothers say their father left them the whole orchard. She says he left her the lower half. The court lost its copy in a fire. The judge has asked her to produce the original. Her brothers have it, if it still exists.”

The chair closes the folder. “Make the copy part of the existing filing fee. Exchange the bundles with the same messengers who carry the courts’ returns. Start with new filings, then copy the old books as the clerks have time.”

She puts the folder on the witness table, where you can reach it if you want to read the appeal. Then she waits.
