## Played record

**The Orbital Watch**
*League Senate chamber · Midmorning*

The harbourmaster brings up a map of Greyhaven. The spaceport is an orbital station. The quay and landing grounds you visited are its surface terminal, where passengers and cargo transfer to and from orbit. On the display, a shuttle climbs between the two, its docking time ticking down beside it.

You ask the Senate to assign military personnel and ships to the orbital station. Their job is to enforce local law and stop armed conflict at the port, not to settle disputes elsewhere.

“Who gives the orders?” a senator asks.

“The Senate sets the mandate,” the chair says. “Xiphos supplies the personnel and ships. Crow directs them within the limits we vote on. The harbourmaster still runs the station. A civilian asking for a berth should hear from her, not a warship.”

The League deputy adds one line to the motion: **No pursuit beyond port jurisdiction without separate authorization.** The guard can act to stop armed violence in the port and protect people there. It cannot execute a foreign warrant on its own or decide who gets legal refuge.

The Senate adopts the motion. The clerk places the signed mandate in front of you. No ships have been moved yet.

The harbourmaster looks from the document to the map. “I’d like to meet whoever you put in charge before they bring any ships up. If they’re going to work with my people, we should know each other’s names.”
