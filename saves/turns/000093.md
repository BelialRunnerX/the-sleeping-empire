# Turn 000093 — The Gift of Forever

5 Deepfall 13,427 ICE, 10:13, private conference room above Lucien's bakery, Epsilon 7. Elys tells Aurelia, Bel, Clarissa, Lethe and Sentinel that his principal concern is what Lucien just ate and who should tell him. The secret is now disclosed TO THIS ROOM: the miniature gift-box pastry Elys gave Lucien has made the baker **immortal**. Lucien still does not know this. Clarissa is troubled that Elys made such a consequential alteration without Lucien's informed consent. Aurelia asks the exact scope of immortality (aging, disease, injury, permanence), and whether it can be reversed, all not yet established. Lethe advises private disclosure by someone Lucien trusts with the responsible person available; Bel and Aurelia advise Elys himself should tell Lucien truthfully, possibly as Hazael so the Emperor identity need not be revealed. Clarissa offers to accompany or speak to Lucien but stresses his right to his own response. No one has told Lucien, no tests or reversals have been made, and no new medical or corporate action authorized. Lucien remains working downstairs at the bakery and still knows Hazael owns Somnus but NOT that Hazael is Emperor Elys. Other commercial story continuity unchanged: bakery open; 10% qualifying-net-profit Somnus proposal pending, wheat contract unresolved. Await Elys's response about exactly what immortality means and how Lucien should be informed.
