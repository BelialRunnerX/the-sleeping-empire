# Turn 000030 — A Birthday Present for the Emperor

Player (verbatim): I mean we probably have a few hundred or thousand years before I give it to myself as a birthday gift so no rush. Although a degree of imperial head turning away wouldn’t hurt

Elys confirms to Regent Aurelia that Somnus's long-term purpose is to build a privately owned industrial network over several hundred or thousand years, eventually transferring its productive holdings to the Imperial government as an elaborate birthday gift to himself in his sovereign capacity. He asks for a degree of Imperial head-turning-away. Aurelia recognizes the potential public benefit and challenges, including worker protection, independent enterprises and eventual public administration. She agrees to protect the distant strategy's confidentiality, avoid extraordinary scrutiny merely because Elys owns Somnus, and respect normal private commercial autonomy. She refuses to waive law, suppress legitimate complaints, or give Somnus preferential treatment. She withdraws the extraordinary open-ended administrative review and substitutes only limited public/private jurisdiction checks where appropriate, preserving ordinary regulators' powers. Sentinel jokingly names the undertaking Operation Birthday Present despite Aurelia's pleas; Clarissa laughs. Aurelia ends the call. The transfer remains centuries or millennia away, with no public entity designated to receive it and no actual nationalization or acquisition completed in this scene.
