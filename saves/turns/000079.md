# Turn 000079 — The Midnight Departure

Player selected option 6 to conclude the Nimbus housewarming, and specifically directed: 'We take a ship to the capital Epsilon 7.'

At about 23:58 on 4 Deepfall, 13,427 ICE, the party at Elys and Clarissa's completed jointly owned private Nimbus estate ends. Bel and other guests depart, and Lucien packs up the remains of his cake and accompanies Elys and Clarissa on the voyage to Epsilon 7. Lucien still knows Hazael owns Somnus but not that Hazael is Emperor Elysomnion. Lucien asks that nobody open or rename his prospective bakery in his absence. Elys, Clarissa, and Lucien travel through the mountain passage to Nimbus's concealed spaceport, board one of Elys's personally owned Andromedan-pattern ships, and travel to the capital city on Epsilon 7. All three arrive shortly after midnight on 5 Deepfall, 13,427 ICE, at a private berth overlooking the capital. The nearby Meridian Commons building site shows major accelerated fabrication and assembly activity and a conspicuous holographic rendering of the entire giant cream-puff-shaped future bakery. Lethe reports structural modules and independent regulatory reviews advancing in parallel. The morning completion/opening remains a target, not yet guaranteed or authorized for commercial service; commercial design approvals and food-service licensing remain pending. The Somnus development proposal is 10% of qualifying net profit and awaits Lucien's independent review/signature; no completed wheat transaction. Regent Aurelia has not been contacted again during this journey. Clarissa reiterates that she wants a peaceful evening at Nimbus when they return; she accompanies Elys on Epsilon 7. Sentinel supports the flight. Thirteen Mnemosyne computer worlds and other holdings remain unchanged. Await next player action at the capital.


## Canonical erratum (user correction)
Lucien **accompanied Elys and Clarissa aboard their ship** from Nimbus to the capital on Epsilon 7. Any earlier text implying Lucien remained on Nimbus after the party is superseded by this correction.
