# Turn 000110 — Happy Birthday, Your Eternal Majesty

5 Deepfall 13,627 ICE, evening at Nimbus. Elys snaps his fingers to summon Regent Aurelia, chair and all, to his and Clarissa's private home. He gathers the Imperial Hands and existing guests, including Bel, Lucien, Veronica, Lilith, Clarissa, Lethe and Sentinel, then announces a birthday party for himself and asks Lethe to do the honors. Lethe orchestrates a spectacular private celestial-themed garden celebration with decorations, music and catering, consulting Clarissa as co-owner of the house. Lucien prepares a huge Imperial-Palace-shaped cake and the guests jokingly debate Elys's age and candle count. Lethe offers a warm, teasing toast, and Aurelia toasts Elys's gifts to their long companionship. All wish the Emperor a happy birthday. Lucien is now immortal and knows Hazael is Emperor Elys. Bel and Lucien are getting closer but not yet formally a couple. Somnus remains Elys's dominant galactic infrastructure business. Await Elys's response at his birthday party.
