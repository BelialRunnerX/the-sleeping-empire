# Turn 000416 — The Ordel Bank

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** Inside the League Senate chamber, Greyhaven, Genosis.  
**Player action:** After Ordel’s formal immediately effective withdrawal, Crow orders the chamber closed and shows a live projection of Ordel.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

The chamber doors close at your order. The live projection comes up above the witness table. The clerk still has Ordel’s withdrawal notice in front of him.

At first the senators can see the river. Nareth’s delegate recognises the bend where the survey crew took its first sounding. Then the image goes white. When the feed clears, the river is still there. On the Ordel bank, where the landing and the weigh house stood, there is a smooth black surface reflecting the sky.

The feed pulls back. The same surface stretches across Ordel. Names on the chart remain visible long after the places they name are gone.

Ordel’s senator goes to the witness table. “Show me the lower landing,” he says. It takes him a moment to realise it’s already on the screen.

Nareth’s delegate sets both hands on the table. “Their forewoman was meant to meet ours there this morning.” She looks at the clerk. “Has anyone heard from our bank?”

The clerk says he hasn’t. Tessa takes the dispatch book from beside him and opens it to the list of contacts they approved last week. She finds Nareth’s and keeps her finger there. The doors are still closed.

The chair picks up Ordel’s letter. She has to start twice before her voice carries.

“Enter the withdrawal, the time the doors were closed, and what we have just seen. Keep the letter with the record.” She looks at Ordel’s senator. “Are you able to sit?”

He pulls a chair out at the witness table. His own seat is across the room. He sits where he can still see the river.
