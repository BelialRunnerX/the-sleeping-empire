### The Triumph of Proper Paperwork

📍 **Lumenfall Free Adventurers' Guild, Veyrath** · **9 Deepfall, 13,627 ICE · Afternoon** · Operational simulator, Station One

The conflicting bridge reports flash above your command console. You don't gamble 258 lives on a green light. Instead, you turn the exercise into an exceptionally well-managed transport job.

“First, reconcile the records. Drone on route reconnaissance, engineering team on convoy readiness. No vehicle moves on an unverified crossing. Get me fuel reserves, axle loads, projected travel times, and a written fallback procedure.”

Garrick's eyebrows rise. “A written fallback procedure?”

“Preferably signed.”

The work proceeds in parallel rather than in sequence. The drone examines the North Causeway from a safe distance: the bridge deck is indeed fractured. Your telemetry audit identifies the reason both messages authenticate—the controller's **old ‘OPEN’ status was validly signed but cached before the collapse**. Cryptographic validity was never evidence of freshness.

The Quarry Bypass offers a less glamorous answer. Your engineers check its restrictions against the actual loaded vehicle weights, mark two narrow passing points, and calculate a feasible convoy speed. You issue the bypass order with an escort at each end, staggered radio check-ins, a fuel contingency, and a prohibition on improvising shortcuts without confirmation.

No miraculous engine upgrade. No secret magic. No dramatic sacrifice.

At minute 61, simulated rain reduces visibility. The lead escort reports a stalled civilian utility truck ahead. Your standing contingency takes over: rear vehicles hold spacing, engineers pull the truck to a safe turnout, medical staff check its driver, and the convoy resumes without a traffic crush.

At **minute 101**, the last transport passes the shelter perimeter. The toxic front reaches the valley nine minutes later.

The holograms vanish. Garrick inspects the automated audit trail. Every evacuee and crew member arrived; no injuries, no lost vehicles, no prohibited intervention. The incident log is irritatingly comprehensive.

Celeste applauds. “He defeated a natural disaster with dispatch forms.”

Garrick sighs. “Yes. An excellent one. Now, **Celeste Somnion**, you're next.”
