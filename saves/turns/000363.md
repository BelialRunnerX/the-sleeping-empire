# Turn 000363 — From a Train

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**When:** After the Senate’s interim dues arrangement; no established calendar date.  
**Player action:** None. Crow remains in his quiet flagship hiatus.  
**Record:** Exact played voice narration.

## Played scene

The dining car has one table left. Sera Dain takes the seat facing backwards and sets the river agreement between the cups. Ordel’s senator sits across from her with a telegram from home.

“They want to know whether I’ve kept the dredge crews on.”

“Have you?”

“Through Friday.”

He folds the telegram along the line that says Friday. Across the aisle, a porter is explaining to a passenger why the river ferry will stop running at dusk. The water is low enough that its last crossing raises mud.

Sera takes out Nareth’s figures. “My assembly will pay for a replacement dredger if it buys a share in the dredger. At the moment we’re paying for a ship we can’t tell where to go.”

“There’s a reason for that,” he says. “Last spring you wanted it upriver during the weeks we clear the ferry approaches.”

“You cleared the ferry approaches during the weeks we needed it upriver.”

“Yes. That’s what I mean.”

The porter comes to take their order. They ask for tea and keep the menus between them to write on.

By the time the train reaches the river, they have enough to take home. Ordel would keep employing the crews. Nareth would pay a share of the replacement cost and get a say in the work schedule. They leave the division of late-season dredging blank. Neither can give away those weeks without speaking to the people who use the river.

At the junction, Sera wires the Senate clerk a copy of their draft. An hour later, Aurelia’s office asks whether the parties intend to submit it for Imperial registration or ask the regency to settle it.

The clerk starts to tick the first box. Then he reads the last line of Sera’s telegram.

*Send it to the ferry landings first. We wrote it from a train.*
