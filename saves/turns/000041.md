# Turn 000041 — The Second Three-Day War: Final Day

Player: Let’s turn this into the new three day war. This is the final day let’s go bois!!!!!!

The story advances to 20 Emberwane, 13,427 ICE, the third and final day of the Second Three-Day War, a Somnus economic and industrial acceleration campaign, not a military conflict. Lethe directs rapid audits, design and prototypes, temporary leased manufacturing resources, trials in agriculture, logistics and industrial assembly, with Meridian, Granary, Ledger, Forge, Axiom, Prima and Nihil contributing. The Bellweather cooperative remains independent and its final agreements and engineering conditions outstanding; no fictitious full-scale gains are claimed. The original 22 WSS civilian freighters are still under construction, though trial logistics services can use contracted existing capacity. A preliminary planetary-scale computing initiative named Project Mnemosyne has finished architecture and commissioned initial computing clusters, while land/site rights and planetary development remain uncompleted and subject to approval. Lethe describes the first three-day demonstrations as successful but no planet-sized machine or galaxy-wide new manufacturing sector is magically completed. Regent Aurelia requests independent validation of the industrial trials and jokes about the Emperor declaring war on inefficiency. Lethe awaits further instruction after the final report.
