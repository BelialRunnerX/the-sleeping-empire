# Turn 000395 — Ask for the Receipt

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** Sealed League Senate chamber, Greyhaven, Genosis.  
**Player action:** Crow asks what happens if a district claims it never received the order.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

“Ask for the receipt,” the clerk says. “It should carry the district seal and the date. If the district disputes the seal or says the wrong order arrived, let the judge compare its dispatch book with ours and hear from the messenger.”

He turns back a page. Several entries have a departure date and nothing beside it.

“If all I can prove is that I sent it, send another copy. The court can enforce it once delivery is established and the effective date has come. Nobody owes a fine for the days before that.”
