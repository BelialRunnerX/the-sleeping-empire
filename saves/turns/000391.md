# Turn 000391 — The Apprentice’s Copy

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** League Senate chamber on Genosis.  
**Player action:** Say only “Next suggestion.”  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

“Next suggestion.”

A woman on the inland bench stands. She waits for the clerk to turn the page. Miren Vale’s name disappears beneath it.

“Give every apprentice a copy of their indenture, and keep another with the district clerk,” she says. “When the term ends, let the clerk issue a certificate. At present the master holds the only paper. If he says there’s another year to serve, the apprentice has to find a witness who remembers signing.”

She takes a letter out of her sleeve. “A potter in my district died last spring. His son inherited the kiln and claimed the apprentice owed him two more years. The boy had served six already. The woman who witnessed the agreement had moved away. By the time we found her, he’d lost the place he’d been offered in another town.”

“Who pays for the copy?” the clerk asks.

“The master. He pays to lodge the agreement once. The apprentice can collect the certificate without him.”

The senator puts the letter down. “The guilds will object. They’ll say their word has always been enough. For a master, it generally has.”
