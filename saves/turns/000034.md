# Turn 000034 — A Thousand Years' Notice

Canonical current date: 17 Emberwane, 13,427 Imperial Concord Era (ICE).

Player (verbatim): I send her a note with we’ll discuss this further near 14,427 ICE sooner if things go well

At the Caldris II farmhouse, Elys sends Regent Aurelia a short personal message: 'We'll discuss this further near 14,427 ICE. Sooner if things go well.' The message sets a provisional conversation horizon about a thousand years from now for the intended Somnus industrial public handoff, not a fixed birthday date, binding delivery date or actual transfer. Sentinel jokes that it is optional expedited fulfillment. Aurelia is disbelieving but asks her secretary to create a long-range planning file after an ordinary calendar proves inadequate; she provisionally notes Year 14,427 ICE and reminds Elys that 'sooner if things go well' is not a precise scheduling basis. In her private reply, she notes the prospective gift remains ambiguous between Elys as private donor and Elys as sovereign beneficiary. She explicitly reiterates that the sweeping corporate-immunity draft is still unratified. Clarissa jokes that the interval allows time to build Nimbus and enjoy family life, while Sentinel hopes no additional corporations are founded before dinner. No transfer or construction completion occurs. Next user action is open.
