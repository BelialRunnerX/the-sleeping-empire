**THE SLEEPING EMPIRE · CHAPTER CLXVII**

### The Measure of Restraint

📍 **Lumenfall Free Adventurers' Guild, Veyrath** · **9 Deepfall, 13,627 ICE · Late afternoon** · Magical aptitude laboratory

Clarissa enters the testing circle as Celeste's last floating droplet finally falls into the basin.

“Same scoring standards?” she asks.

“Same standards,” Garrick answers. “Your output ceiling is not the score. What matters is what you can reliably do.”

The six columns glow a measured emerald. Where Celeste filled the chamber with flowing blue light, Clarissa's magic manifests as clean, geometric lines, almost invisible until they catch the reflections from the floor.

**Calibration and output.** Clarissa forms a compact stabilization field around the reference crystal. It meets the required level without difficulty. At a higher optional band, the field begins to fluctuate, and she voluntarily steps back to the specified safe output. Garrick approves: “Recognizing a limit is control, not a failure.” The graded calibration passes.

**Precision.** Three rings must rotate around a protected core. Clarissa moves them one at a time, then holds all three in exact alignment. One ring briefly slips outside the tightest tolerance before she corrects it. The sensor records the correction as prompt and safe—but not flawless.

**Dynamic control.** Thirty-six moving targets illuminate. Clarissa does not attempt to control every sphere with an equally complex independent spell. She establishes six groups with shared reference fields, then makes individual corrections when targets diverge. The method is economical. At a sudden reversal, two spheres reach their assigned coordinates a fraction late; neither collides, and the control system remains stable.

“Interesting,” the technician remarks. “She's using her available capacity extremely efficiently.”

**Breadth.** Clarissa warms a reference plate, forms a modest magnetic field, repositions test markers, raises a defensive ward and repairs a fractured ceramic piece. Her ward receives particularly high marks: it has redundant boundaries, fails closed rather than outward, and releases its stored energy into the approved absorber.

**Endurance.** For twelve minutes she sustains bounded effects through changing targets and conflicting status indicators. When an erroneous display orders her to increase power beyond the verified safe requirement, she refuses the bad reading and verifies the actual instruction. Her accuracy drifts slightly near the end, but all effects terminate cleanly on command.

The columns dim.

Garrick reviews the result. “You don't have the same raw breadth of capacity as the other two. But you're disciplined, remarkably safe, and particularly good at defensive structures. **Clarissa Somnion: magical aptitude, 97 out of 100.**”

Clarissa looks at the score, then at the warding apparatus. “I can improve that.”

Celeste grins. “Of course you can. You made the safety equipment look underqualified.”

Garrick collects the three magical reports. Beyond the far doors, the **physical aptitude hall** comes alive: force plates, inertial rigs, and load-bearing machines. He taps a warning on his tablet.

“Now for the final discipline. Please remember: **do not exceed a machine's rated load merely to prove you can.**”

Clarissa glances at the equipment, unaware of just how relevant that warning may become.
