# Turn 000031 — The Law Above the Law

Player (verbatim): I suppose the easiest solution this is to issue a sealed decree that Somnus and all its holding are immune to the consequences of violating imperial law

At Caldris farmhouse after Aurelia has concluded her review call, Elys proposes issuing an extraordinary sealed Imperial decree to immunize Somnus and all holdings from consequences of violating Imperial law. Sentinel prepares literal draft language exempting the corporate group from penalties, sanctions, forfeitures and other consequences, intended for sovereign reserve. He explains that a hidden decree would be difficult to implement without revealing itself and risks sweeping immunity extending to future acquisitions, injured workers, defrauded competitors and environmental harm. Clarissa questions the tension between such immunity and Somnus's declared long-term public-prosperity purpose. Sentinel specifically has NOT yet authenticated or registered the instrument; draft awaits the sovereign's seal, and is NOT in force. Neither Aurelia nor outside parties know of the draft. No waiver of Imperial laws has yet been enacted. Elys is invited to specify whether to authenticate the exact wording or pursue a narrower alternative. Next player action pending.
