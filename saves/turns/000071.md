# Turn 000071 — Lethe and the Bakery

4 Deepfall 13,427 ICE, Nimbus housewarming. Elys as Hazael answers Lucien's question about speed of bakery construction: 'As fast as I can get the permits approved.' Elys then calls Lethe and asks whether she has been listening. Lethe replies that she has not been continuously monitoring the private home, but received the summons and a contextual summary from Sentinel. Lethe considers a feasible design for a whole cream-puff-shaped bakery, with ordinary structural systems under pastry-shaped exterior cladding. She explains the need for a site, permits, food-safety compliance, financing and contractors. Lucien learns that Hazael has connections with Somnus, but not his secret identity. Lethe offers three possible routes for Elys to authorize: independent technical assistance, a commercial Somnus design/build contract, or personal financing. No project, spending, construction, permit or wheat purchase is yet authorized. Lethe asks whether she should prepare the project for review. Clarissa and Bel are present. Thirteen Mnemosyne worlds remain operational.
