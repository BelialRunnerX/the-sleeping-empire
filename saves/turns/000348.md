**The Harbourmaster Has Her Name**
*League Senate chamber · Midmorning*

“Harbourmaster, I’d be in charge.”

She looks from the signed mandate to you. “Oh. Then I suppose I’ve saved myself a meeting.”

She pulls her chair up beside yours and turns the station map so you can both read it. “You command the guard. I run the port. My people handle berths, rescues, and traffic. If there’s armed trouble, I call you directly.”

The clerk strikes out *prospective commander* and writes **Crow** on the watch sheet. There’s a separate blank for the officer your guard would have on watch when you’re away. Filling it wouldn’t put anyone else in command.

“No need to decide that this minute,” the harbourmaster says. She taps a pair of empty berths on the map. “But before you bring your ships up, tell me how much room you want. I’m not turning away a ferry because the military got there first.”
