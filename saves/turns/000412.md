# Turn 000412 — An Empty Seat

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** Inside the League Senate chamber, Greyhaven, Genosis.  
**Player action:** Crow states his condition tying an empty seat to Senate withdrawal, then League withdrawal, then a deemed declaration of war.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

“An empty seat is to be considered tantamount to withdrawal from the Senate,” you say. “Withdrawal from the Senate is to be considered withdrawal from the League. Withdrawal from the League is considered to be a declaration of war against the Empire.”

The clerk writes each clause on its own line.

Tessan’s senator speaks first. “My government told me to stay. It didn’t tell me that if I fell ill on the morning of a vote, forty-seven fishing crews would wake up at war.”

Nareth’s delegate sets her credentials back on the table. “My assembly gave me a recall instruction. If I obey it, will you say they declared war even if their letter says the opposite?”

Aurelia turns the draft she brought with her face down. Ordel’s senator looks at it, then at the clerk.

“Read back the first line,” he says.

“An empty seat is to be considered tantamount to withdrawal from the Senate.”

Ordel’s senator looks down the row of desks that had stood empty after the trial. “We’ve just spent days filling seventeen seats. I want to know whether we’ll ever be given those days again.”

The chair leaves the voting book closed. “Clerk, record those as questions. Nobody here has tendered a withdrawal.” She looks at you. “Does a nation declare war the moment its chair stands empty, or does it have time to send someone else?”
