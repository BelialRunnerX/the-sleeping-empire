# Turn 000314 — Wrenford rescue and Veycross coup

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc · Genosis · Early.  
**Record type:** Backfill synopsis. Chronology backfill from surviving played material; not a verbatim reconstruction or one-to-one voice turn.  
**In-world date:** Unestablished where not directly recorded.

## Played record

Accepted recap: returned letters in foster mother's biscuit tin lead to Chamberlain **Tavin Edrick**. Crow intercepts a royal-sealed but unsigned instruction at the old tollhouse that would send the Queen's escort away; she keeps her guards, and the replacement rider exposes the scheme to hold her at the old mint under cover of an illness and install a regent. The rider produces **Councillor Merrow's signed detention order**. The Queen never reaches the mint and gets safely to her daughters. Crow accepts her authorization/warrant, waits for recorded council roll call, reads her letters denying ill health or a regency and voiding the vote, arrests Merrow peacefully, and delivers him to Vale. Edrick and rider kept separate for questioning. The Queen's signed honor **Crow the Mischievous** subsequently appears on quartz ID under Royal Honors, not as change of legal name.
