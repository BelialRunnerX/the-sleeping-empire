### The Measure of an Adventurer
Lumenfall Free Adventurers' Guild · Veyrath · 10:59

The blue illumination of the examination chamber reflects off Garrick's mechanical forearm. Beyond the glass, technicians are preparing the adaptive testing equipment.

You address the examiner.

**ELYS:** "My lack of legal convictions strongly suggests my ability to use my abilities responsibly. I just request non-destructive aptitude measurements. Although I do insist on testing all of us on our physical and non-physical capabilities. Magic, technology, strategy, et cetera."

Garrick stares at you for a moment.

**GARRICK:** "No criminal record. Congratulations. You're qualified to walk down the street."

Mira coughs into her hand.

**GARRICK:** "But the rest? That's a reasonable request."

He turns toward his terminal, pulling up a much larger assessment program.

**GARRICK:** "Most adventurers specialize. We don't usually test everyone in every discipline. But if you want comprehensive rankings, I can arrange that."

He pauses, looking between the three of you.

**GARRICK:** "And non-destructive testing is absolutely preferable to replacing my equipment."

Celeste raises her hand.

**CELESTE:** "Does that include the examiner?"

**GARRICK:** "Especially the examiner."

**CELESTE:** "How disappointing."

Clarissa gives her an amused sideways glance.

### Comprehensive Adventurer Assessment

**MULTIDISCIPLINARY EVALUATION — Proposed**
All three applicants · Individual scores · Non-destructive protocols

- **Physical:** Strength, speed, agility, endurance, reflexes
- **Magical:** Output, control, complexity, precision, efficiency
- **Technical:** Engineering, diagnostics, systems operation, fabrication
- **Strategic:** Tactics, logistics, command, adaptation, crisis management
- **Operational:** Rescue, teamwork, restraint, civilian protection

**Measurement method:** Adaptive force-field instrumentation, virtual environments, isolated magical sensors, and reversible simulations.
Measurements beyond equipment certification are recorded as lower bounds, not invented exact values. Final rank remains subject to examination and guild review.

Garrick studies the expanded examination schedule.

**GARRICK:** "Each of you will complete every category. That gives us an individual capability profile rather than a single combat score."

He taps the physical-testing entry.

**GARRICK:** "If someone exceeds our instrumentation, we record the maximum demonstrated safe value and stop. We don't destroy a machine to discover the next decimal place."

Clarissa folds her arms.

**CLARISSA:** "And the strategic assessments?"

**GARRICK:** "Command simulations with incomplete information, conflicting objectives, and consequences for civilian losses. No advance knowledge of the scenarios."

Her expression brightens considerably.

**CLARISSA:** "Now *that* sounds interesting."

Celeste leans toward her.

**CELESTE:** "I'm looking forward to seeing you win."

**CLARISSA:** "You're assuming I'll win?"

**CELESTE:** "I have excellent taste in people."

Garrick turns back to you.

**GARRICK:** "One final matter. We're not equipped to certify unlimited power. If you really are as extraordinary as you're suggesting, some of your results may be classified as beyond measurable range."

He gestures toward the three waiting registration terminals.

**GARRICK:** "I'll authorize the full battery. But I want to know which discipline you intend to begin with. The others can follow in sequence."

His mechanical fingers click together.

**GARRICK:** "And please, Corin. No surprises involving explosions."

Celeste smiles.

**CELESTE:** "What about implosions?"

**GARRICK:** "I'm beginning to understand why he calls you a mule."
