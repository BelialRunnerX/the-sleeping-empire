**Receipt of Notice**
*League Senate chamber · Midmorning*

At your direction, the clerk files the notice once the harbourmaster checks the facts and the chair signs it. **Elys Somnion** appears as an official witness to the conversation and the dispatch you saw at the tug berth, not to the earlier handover. The other witnesses’ names, statements, and recordings stay sealed in Greyhaven.

The clerk’s quartz terminal chimes twice. The **Meridian Civil Registry** and the **Vey Transit Authority** have both acknowledged receipt. Neither has announced a review. The League has asked to be notified when they decide.

The chair closes the folder. “That handles this incident. Now imagine it’s three in the morning and the next ship calls for shelter. It shouldn’t need you on the quay and us in session.”

He pulls a marked-up copy of the harbour law from beneath the folder. The paper is soft at the corners from being passed around all night.

“The harbourmaster can offer a berth without waiting for us. Last night she still had to ask who could authorize a supervised visitor, who could see the witness records, and who could request a military detail. I’d like her to have those answers before the next ship arrives.”

He sets the copy on your desk. At the top, in the harbourmaster’s handwriting, is a question: *If I’m not on duty, who can say yes?*

*Your evenings with Iona at your residence remain peaceful and offstage. The story stays with your official duties.*
