# Turn 000057 — The Cream-Pie-R Strikes Back

Player said to Lucien: “Your still working on building your Cream-Pie-R I see” (an intentional pun on 'empire').

Hazael teases him with the phrase 'Cream-Pie-R.' Lucien still owns no bakery and no business registration, wheat sale, purchase, investment or licensing agreement is made. No change to Somnus or the 13 Mnemosyne computing planets.
