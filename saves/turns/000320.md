## Played record

Istren initially appears not to have replied; hostilities begin. Lessa Renn arrives with a certified vote taken six hours before the deadline and evidence concerning the missing dispatch. Crow orders a suspension for verification. Istren is accepted, powers recalled, and the full Senate sits. Xiphos becomes the neutral administrative district under the charter, with military transfer and Senate direction of the League executive, without losing its sovereign nationhood. Crow pledges reparations only if Xiphos is responsible. The senators establish procedure themselves, including an entered reason for a voted urgent waiver of advance notice.
