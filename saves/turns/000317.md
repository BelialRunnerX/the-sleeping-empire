# Turn 000317 — Charter and First Accessions

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc · Genosis · Middle.  
**Record type:** Backfill synopsis. Chronology backfill from surviving played material; not a verbatim reconstruction or one-to-one voice turn.  
**In-world date:** Unestablished where not directly recorded.

## Played record

Crow signs and circulates the League charter with ninety days for accession, permanent membership, one senator from each member, and majority legislation directing Crow as permanent executive. Drafting before the full Senate does not make law. Alderreach joins on a 4–3 council vote; the vote does not choose a senator. Crow's separate executive position distinguishes refusal, silence at the deadline, and lawful accession. Vask initially refuses and recalls its ships; after harbour towns petition, its assembly demonstrates treaty authority and accedes. Xiphos recognizes the certified vote, recalls its forces, and Tessa Vey represents Vask.
