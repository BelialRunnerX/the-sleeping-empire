# Turn 000400 — On an Ordinary Day

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** Inside the League Senate chamber, Greyhaven, Genosis.  
**Player action:** Crow remains seated to one side inside the chamber and observes. He gives no new order.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

The clerk reads Nareth’s motion again, loud enough for the whole chamber to hear. When he comes to the blank that would say when the arrangement takes effect, he stops.

“Who decides we’re unable to meet?” asks Tessan’s senator. “If my government gets word that the Senate has been closed, it needs some way to tell whether that’s true.”

“Ask Greyhaven to confirm it,” says a senator across the aisle.

Nareth’s delegate looks down at Sera Dain’s account. “Last time, Greyhaven was where we couldn’t get an answer.”

Ordel’s senator has been turning a silver coin between his fingers. He sets it flat. “My government already has the right to send grain across the river if Nareth asks. What my people need is a name to put on the waybill. Somebody on the other bank who can sign for the sacks and answer for them when the accounts come due. We could exchange those names this afternoon.”

Tessa Vey looks over the draft. “Then why wait for a closure? Put the contacts in place now and let them do that much on any ordinary day.”

A man near the aisle asks whether that gives a gathering of contacts the right to call itself the League. The clerk starts to answer, but the chair raises a hand.

“Put it in the motion. Each contact speaks for the government that appointed them. Anything requiring a vote of this Senate still requires a vote of this Senate.”

She says it while looking at the clerk. He reads it back without lowering his voice.

At the side of the chamber, you can see several senators glance towards you. One of the replacements has drawn a line through something on her notes. When her turn comes, she asks whether her government may share the name of its road commissioner without signing the whole agreement. Nareth’s delegate says yes. By the end of the discussion, three governments have offered names for an initial exchange. Two others ask to see the amended text before they give theirs.

The chair calls for a clean copy. Nareth’s delegate picks up Sera’s account.

“I also ask that this be entered in the record of the sitting.”

The clerk holds out his hand for it. The chair asks whether she wants it circulated with the motion.

“Separately,” the delegate says. “My assembly wants the roads open, and it wants the account heard. It sent me to do both.”

The clerk gives the account its own number. The chair orders a recess so the delegations can read the amended motion before she calls a vote. For a moment nobody gets up. Then the usher opens the doors, and Ordel’s senator goes out to find a courier.
