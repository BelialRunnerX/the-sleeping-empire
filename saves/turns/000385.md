# Turn 000385 — Something to Sow

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** League Senate chamber on Genosis.  
**Player action:** Say only “No medics. Next suggestion.”  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

“No medics. Next suggestion.”

Tessa stays beside the chair. The usher looks from her to the doors, then lowers his eyes.

A senator at the far end of the table rises. “Give every district the right to move its seed grain to another district for safekeeping. If blight or flood takes the whole harvest, a village should still have something to sow next spring.”

He opens his notebook. “Two villages in my district kept their seed in the same storehouse as the grain they owed in taxes. They had separate locks. The flood came through the wall.”

“Who pays for the storehouses?” the clerk asks.

“Use barns that are already standing. The districts can exchange storage by the sack, one for one. Pay for a dry loft and two people to count what goes in and comes out.” He looks toward the witness table. “And each district keeps its own list, so the people who sent the grain know what they can ask for back.”

The clerk writes the last line. Tessa keeps one hand on the chair’s shoulder.
