# Turn 000035 — The Emperor's Software Update

Player (verbatim): I radically update and overhaul Aurelia’s software for better planning and management features. Next time she uses an electronic device it says “your welcome”

On 17 Emberwane, 13,427 ICE, Elys radically redesigns Aurelia's administrative software environment for powerful and efficient long-range government planning, interministerial coordination, financial and logistical forecasting, dependencies, scenario comparison, preserved records, permissions and audit controls. The overhaul is completed, enabling scheduling over centuries and millennia, including the provisional 14,427 ICE discussion of a prospective Somnus public handoff. This changes administrative software, not public law or who holds government authority; records and governing decisions are not overwritten. At the next activation of Aurelia's electronic devices, the system displays exactly the intentionally ungrammatical phrase 'your welcome'. Sentinel warned Elys of the grammar. Aurelia is impressed by the genuine software improvements and thanks him, while correcting the phrase to 'you're welcome' and remarking that he knowingly left the error. Clarissa jokes he could have sent flowers. This is a whimsical software upgrade in the fictional universe. The broad Somnus immunity decree remains unratified and without legal effect. No Somnus ownership transfer occurs.
