# Turn 000049 — The Housewarming of Two Sovereigns

Player selected option 6 from turn 000048: Plan a housewarming; invite Lucien and the Imperial Hands to celebrate the finished Nimbus estate.

On 2 Deepfall, Year 13,427 ICE, Elys and Clarissa arrange a private housewarming at their newly completed shared Nimbus estate, with invitations to Lucien and the Imperial Hands. The invitation specifies a casual evening gathering on 4 Deepfall, no formal dress or official business. Bel, female chief diplomat and Imperial Hand, replies warmly and brings a guestbook. Lucien replies with outsized interest in the kitchen and attends, producing an absurd six-tier architectural cake with a miniature icing spacecraft apparently crashing into a rendition of the home. Other Hands bring personal gifts. The gathering takes place at the completed mountain residence with greenhouse, gardens and storm views; no new construction or state ceremony occurs. Clarissa delights in making a private place for loved ones rather than a palace of government. Sentinel jokes about questionable cake engineering and the absence of constitutional incidents. The scene ends with the party ongoing and Elys free to decide his next action. Ownership remains 50/50 Elys–Clarissa; it is not a Somnus facility. Somnus's thirteen fortified Mnemosyne worlds continue operating independently.
