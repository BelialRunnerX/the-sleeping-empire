Following Elys's masked on-camera throne broadcast apologizing for the earlier pastry incidents, the Emperor has secretly teleported back to the bakery. The Epsilon 7 bakery is open, but Somnus's proposed 10% of qualifying net-profit agreement is not yet signed and longer-term wheat supply remains unsettled.
