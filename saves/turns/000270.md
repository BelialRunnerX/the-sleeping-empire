### Four known functional domains — independently characterized, NOT fully stress-tested

1. **Anchor of Identity:** Authorized target continuity anchors to resist mnemonic/ontological erasure and disconnection from history **when deliberately equipped/deployed**. Not death prevention, resurrection, universal invincibility or passive protection while in storage.
2. **Testament Seal:** Bounded provenance-authenticated/tamper-evident testimony across later alterations. Confirms record continuity/provenance, **NOT inherent truthfulness of false testimony** or omniscient fact checking.
3. **Sepulcher Waymark:** Stable reference to the repaired/certified Sepulcher approach route that may distinguish navigation deception and drift. **Not free portal teleportation**, nor automatic access to all routes.
4. **Custodian's Accord:** Heart-authenticated key allowing requests for archival read access subject to the **Heart's voluntary consent**; not control/ownership of it or of recorded persons.

### New analysis: continuity-discrepancy forensic principle

Aletheia discovers that microscopic inscription patterns preserve not merely individual names but **independent relationships**: person to event, parent to child, explorer to ship, witness to occurrence. She constructs **only a simulated diagnostic model** (no real person's name or record touched) and deletes a dummy explorer from a dummy manifest; an independently stored simulated ship manifest preserves a reference to the lost identifier, allowing her to infer that deletion/alteration occurred even though the primary record is gone.

**Verified limited analytical deduction:** By comparing independent testimony and continuity references the Last Witness *may help detect* that a name, record, relationship or event was erased or rewritten. This is an **inferred cross-referencing characteristic of Anchor of Identity plus Testament Seal**, not a new independent full magical power or guarantee. It does **not** resurrect missing persons, automatically reconstruct lost data, reveal an unknown perpetrator's exact identity, universally counter every erasure, expose a timeline in full, or trace an attacker without corroborating evidence. **Safe model simulation is not stress certification of the actual inactive relic**.
