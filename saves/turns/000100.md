# Turn 000100 — The Emperor Willed It

**Date:** 5 Deepfall, 13,627 ICE, approximately 10:12, Epsilon 7, 200 years after the opening of Lucien's independent bakery.

Following his discovery that the magical pastry Elys/Hazael gave him two centuries ago bestowed immortality, Lucien asked three questions. User explicitly gave three answers in order:

1. To what can happen to Lucien now, Elys says: **"Up to you"**. Lucien reads this as personal choice over his future, but the exact physical mechanics and limitations of his immortality remain unspecified.
2. To whether Lucien can choose death eventually, Elys replies: **"That could be arranged"**. Lucien is deeply relieved that eternal life need not be compulsory forever.
3. To why Elys chose him, Elys says: **"I wanted to know how long it would take you to realize, and I wanted you to be around for a while cause it would suck to lose you, also because the Emperor willed it."** Lucien is incredulous at the two-century practical joke, touched by the wish not to lose him, and surprised that the Emperor supposedly endorsed the gift.

Lucien recalls his loved ones and employees aging and dying, appreciates Hazael's long friendship, and suspects his unaging friend may also be immortal. The remark that "the Emperor willed it" makes Lucien question the nature of Hazael's relationship with the throne. He knows Hazael owns Somnus and the Emperor, with Imperial face coverings, publicly praised his bakery 200 years earlier. **Lucien still does NOT know that Hazael Wheatley is Emperor Elysomnion.** Lucien asks over their private video call: "Just how well do you know the Emperor?" He waits for a player-controlled answer.
