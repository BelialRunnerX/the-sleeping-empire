# Turn 000080 — The Regent Gets Creamed

**Canonical erratum:** Lucien accompanied Elys and Clarissa aboard their ship from Nimbus to Epsilon 7 on turn 000079. The earlier archive record has been corrected.

5 Deepfall 13,427 ICE, ~09:14 at Meridian Commons in Epsilon 7's capital. Somnus's extraordinarily fast automated construction, separate independent engineering checks and commercial/food safety approvals have enabled the completed, fully licensed first bakery to hold its morning opening ceremony. The entire building is shaped like a giant golden cream puff; it is a real functional commercial structure. Lucien, present after traveling with Elys and Clarissa, is ecstatic. Regent Aurelia attends as guest. Elys, publicly Hazael Wheatley, says word-for-word into the ceremonial microphone: 'In honor of this momentous occasion the honorable Lady Aurelia has come to give a hand and receive her first fresh and hot crème filling'. Aurelia is mortified by the innuendo but keeps her composure. When she takes a bite of Lucien's fresh custard-filled pastry, glaze lands on her chin. Elys then says exactly: 'do mind the glaze you’ve gotten a little on your chin.' Clarissa laughs, Bel struggles not to, and Sentinel is dryly amused. Lucien is innocent and thrilled when Aurelia genuinely compliments his pastry. Aurelia counters by publicly nominating Hazael to speak at an upcoming Imperial Small Enterprise Forum on 'Proper Professional Conduct in Public Ceremonies', promising to attend from the front row. This is her social counter-joke and does not establish an actual binding appointment or law. Lucien still knows Hazael owns Somnus but does NOT know he is Emperor Elysomnion. Although the bakery now opens for trade, the 10% of qualifying NET PROFIT development contract still awaits independent review and execution; no wheat transaction was finally priced in the prior archive. No known Somnus ownership transfer or Mnemosyne change. Await player action immediately after Aurelia's counter-move.
