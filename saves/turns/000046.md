# Turn 000046 — Project Mnemosyne II / Janus

Player: Redundancy and reliability with a side helping of tech. Build a Second Mnemosyne

On 20 Thawrise, Year 13,427 ICE, Elys directs Lethe to prioritize resilience, reliability and technology by constructing a second planetary-scale computer. Lethe proposes Project Janus: Mnemosyne II on a separate, lawfully secured uninhabited Somnus industrial world, geographically distinct from Mnemosyne I, with independent power, computing, maintenance, communications and recovery systems. Neither system should blindly replicate errors or override the other's recovery records. Ordinary regional Somnus logistics and production should still function without either world. The second computer is built through Somnus's existing extraordinary fleet and manufacturing resources, WITHOUT Elys snapping it into existence.

Six weeks later, on 2 Suncrest, 13,427 ICE (new in-story date), Mnemosyne II is physically completed and commissioned. Both planetary computing facilities are operating. Initial independent authentication and failover exercises succeed; prolonged resilience verification remains underway. Lethe remains the same executive AI, using both systems for continuity, operations and major scientific work rather than creating a separate AI personality or unrestricted governmental control. Extra computing resources support research in energy, materials, medicine, physics, alchemy, transportation and computing. Aurelia reacts with surprise and appreciation for the robust continuity design. Clarissa jokes about a third computing planet. Lethe asks Elys to set the first research priority. Somnus's earlier rapid expansion, SIR government data contract and broader governance constraints otherwise remain in force; the sweeping corporate immunity draft still has no legal force.
