## Played record

**Nothing More Tonight**
*Greyhaven · Late evening, then dawn*

You call it a night. The Senate clerk still has your contact if the harbourmaster needs you, but no message comes while the rain runs down the roof.

At dawn, your quartz slate lights up on the bedside table. The harbourmaster has left a note marked **For your information. No decision needed.**

*He reached his sister. She answered on her way to work. She’s no longer at the station and asked that we not record where she’s staying. He says she sounded like herself for the first time in weeks. They’re going to speak again tomorrow.*

There’s a second line beneath it.

*She asked him what he wanted for breakfast. Apparently this is how they end difficult conversations.*

It doesn’t tell you whether the company had the right to keep her there, or whether anyone else is still waiting to leave. The harbourmaster has sealed the statements and kept the passengers’ lodging private. Nobody has asked for a fleet.

There’s a knock, then Iona’s voice through the door. “I have tea and two loaves. Your mother said one was for toasting. I think she was giving you instructions, but I’m happy to be an accomplice.”
