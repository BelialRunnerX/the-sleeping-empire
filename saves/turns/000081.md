# Turn 000081 — A Most Satisfying Filling

Player selected prior option 1, to congratulate Regent Aurelia on the filling. Aurelia nearly addresses Hazael as 'Your Majesty' but corrects herself to 'Mr. Wheatley'. Lucien still knows Hazael owns Somnus but NOT that Hazael is Emperor Elys. Lucien jokes he should have opened a sandwich shop. Somnus's proposed 10% share of qualifying NET PROFIT remains unsigned pending independent review.
