# Turn 000372 — A Name They’d Stand Behind

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** League Senate observation gallery on Genosis.  
**Player action:** Authorize the chair to ask whether anyone knows another person able to do the same. No further action.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

The chair turns from the gallery to the benches.

“You saw what Crow did. Does anyone here know of another person who can do the same?”

For a moment, the only sound is the younger apprentice shifting his boots on the stairs. Then the senator from Ordel raises his hand halfway.

“I know a story about a woman who cracked a millstone with her hands.”

“Did anyone you know see her do it?”

“My grandfather claimed he did. He also claimed the miller owed him for that stone. Every time he told it, the miller owed him a little more.”

A few people laugh. The senator lowers his hand. “That’s the best I have, Chair.”

She looks toward the masons’ apprentices. The older one clears his throat.

“I cut that sample myself,” he says. “I left the rest of the block in the yard. I can tell you what kind of stone it was. I’ve never seen anybody do that to it.”

The chair gives the room another moment. No other hands go up.

“An old story from Ordel,” she tells you, “and nobody here who can give you a name they’d stand behind.” Beside her, the clerk has written down *millstone*. He leaves room beneath it in case anyone remembers more.
