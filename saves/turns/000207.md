# Turn 000207 — The Mountain Was an Unnecessary Complication

**Chapter:** CLXVII
**Location:** Lumenfall Remote SSS Assessment Annex, certification chamber
**Time:** 9 Deepfall, 13,627 ICE, evening
**Verbatim user message:** "3"
**Selection:** Elys tells Garrick that this is why a simulated mountain was unnecessarily complicated.

## Scene

In the perfectly restored certification hall, Elys gestures at its pristine walls and tells Garrick: **“See? This is why a simulated mountain would have been unnecessarily complicated.”**

Garrick looks at him as if the sentence is a new assault on logic.

“An *unnecessarily complicated* simulation,” Garrick says, very carefully, “would have been preferable to making an **occupied accredited building** briefly cease existing.”

Celeste covers her mouth. Her shoulders shake.

“Garrick, you don't understand. He has reduced the entire philosophy of destructive-capacity certification to an administrative cost-benefit analysis. Why provision a mountain when the facility is already here?”

“Because,” Garrick answers, “the mountain would be **simulated**!”

Clarissa folds her arms. “Elys, next time please leave the people and buildings where they were.”

Seraphine Vael examines the new incident report and, with uncanny composure, adds:

**FACILITY REMOVAL WITHOUT EXPLOSION: CONFIRMED**  
**RESTORATION WITHOUT DAMAGE: CONFIRMED**  
**PRIOR SAFETY AUTHORIZATION: NOT OBTAINED**

“Candidate—rather, **SSS adventurer**—Somion,” she says, “your achievement was extraordinary. Your argument for its convenience is not an adequate safety plan.”

Celeste raises a finger. “May we at least put *mountain unnecessary* in the record?”

“No.”

Garrick exhales. “For once, thank you.”

Seraphine closes Elys's completed file. “The demonstration is recorded. The procedural reprimand remains. Your SSS certification stands.”

She turns to Celeste. “Miss Somnion, your own examination awaits. If you will refrain from removing the premises, we can begin.”

Celeste gives the examiner a bright, angelic smile.

“I shall endeavor to leave you with something to write about.”

Garrick looks toward the ceiling.

“That's what I'm afraid of.”

The SSS testing console activates for **CELESTE SOMNION**. No trial begins until she is called into the assessment circle.
