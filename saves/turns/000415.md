# Turn 000415 — The Seat Is Occupied

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** Inside the League Senate chamber, Greyhaven, Genosis.  
**Player action:** Crow observes; he issues no new direction.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

A week later, Nareth’s delegate comes back to the Senate with two papers under the assembly’s seal. She hands the clerk the thinner one first.

The chair reads it aloud. Nareth has rescinded its recall instruction. Its delegate is authorised to remain, speak, and vote. The assembly also reserves the right to contest the Empire’s claim that leaving the League would amount to declaring war.

“Which part do I enter against their seat?” the clerk asks.

“That it’s occupied,” says the chair. “Read the rest into the minutes.”

Tessan’s senator lets out a breath and reaches for the fishing papers she brought with her. Nareth’s delegate puts a hand on the second letter before the clerk can take it.

“There’s a personal statement with this one. Sera asked me to read it myself.”

The chair gives her the floor.

“‘I advised my assembly to keep the seat filled. I want Nareth there when the river agreement comes to a vote. I also want it understood that the person sitting in my old chair has taken a risk on our behalf.’”

The delegate folds Sera’s statement along its old crease. Then she takes her seat.

Aurelia asks for a certified copy of the assembly’s reply. The chair directs the clerk to send both papers together. When he calls Nareth on the roll, the delegate answers. Then she asks him to open the voting book.
