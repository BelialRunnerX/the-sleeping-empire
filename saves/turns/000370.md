# Turn 000370 — A Block of Granite

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** League Senate observation gallery on Genosis.  
**Player action:** Ask for a large, solid chunk of stone; take no further action.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

“Someone bring me a stone. A large, solid chunk of stone.”

The clerk lowers the roll. The chair puts a finger beside the name he’s just called. “We’ll resume here.”

The usher sends for a block from the masons repairing the courtyard steps. A few minutes later he returns with two apprentices and a low handcart. On it sits a rough block of granite about the size of a boot chest. One apprentice brushes loose grit off the top with his sleeve. The other keeps both hands on the cart until its wheels stop.

“Solid all the way through,” the usher says, looking up at you in the gallery. “The mason says we can have it if we’re prepared to carry it back.”

The apprentices set the block on the flagstones at the foot of the gallery stairs. Beside the chair, the clerk keeps his finger on the roll.
