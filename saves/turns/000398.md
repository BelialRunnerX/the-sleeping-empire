# Turn 000398 — The Full Roll

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** League Senate gallery, Greyhaven, Genosis.  
**Player action:** Crow reminds the Senate it cannot pass laws until all seventeen vacancies are filled, urges the nations to move quickly, and skips ahead to attend the next actual sitting.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

You remind the chamber that the Senate cannot pass laws until all seventeen vacancies are filled. You recommend that the nations move quickly.

By the next sitting, the clerk has a credential for every seat. Ordel sent its notice of succession back by return courier. Nareth took nine days. Its assembly spent the first two hearing Sera Dain’s account of what happened, then chose a district magistrate who insisted on bringing the written account with her.

You attend from the gallery. Below you, the chair opens the sitting. She holds the roll flat with her restored hand while the clerk calls the thirty-five names. When he reaches Nareth, the new senator answers, then lays Sera’s account beside her credentials.

The clerk brings up the first item. Ordel’s landings have submitted their costs for the river gauges. Three can pay from their ordinary dues. Two are asking the Senate for help. The new senator from Nareth turns the figures over and finds the old joint survey attached.

“Did both banks sign these readings?” she asks.

“They did,” says Ordel’s senator.

“Good. Then we can start with the same numbers.”

The chair puts the estimates before the chamber. For the first time since the trial, the clerk opens the voting book.
