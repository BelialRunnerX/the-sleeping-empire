# Turn 000070 — Operation: Puff of Mass Destruction

**Player selected option 1:** Tell Regent Aurelia construction begins tomorrow, to see how she reacts to the fictional construction deadline.

At 23:16 on 4 Deepfall, 13,427 ICE, from the completed private Nimbus estate during a housewarming, Elys sends Aurelia a private message: 'Construction begins tomorrow.' The statement is a prank concerning Lucien's prospective giant cream-puff-shaped bakery, not a real construction order. There is no construction permit, property, approved design, contractor or secured investment. Sentinel notes the impossibility of the schedule under conventional development processes; Clarissa laughs. Aurelia initially fears Elys might personally conjure such a building because he has already created a planetary computer by reality alteration. She calls Elys privately and encounters Bel, Sentinel, Clarissa and Lucien. Lucien thanks Aurelia for ordinary business-startup guidance but confirms he has no architect, site or permits. Aurelia stresses that a bakery is a legitimate private aspiration subject to ordinary approvals, and tells Elys she will know whom to contact if a pastry-shaped building appears overnight. Sentinel jokes about contacting him by communicator; Aurelia ends the call. Lucien asks Hazael how quickly he could hypothetically build a giant cream puff. He still does not know Hazael is Emperor Elys despite the dramatic near-revelation; preserve this secrecy unless player deliberately changes it. No building was created or authorized, no wheat sale arranged, Somnus's thirteen Mnemosyne worlds are unchanged. Await player's response.
