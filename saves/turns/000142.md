# Turn 000142 — The Man Who Arrived Before Himself

**Chapter CXLVI, 7 Deepfall 13,627 ICE ~18:02, beneath Pelagia, Tideglass.**

**User action:** chose option **5 — Investigate the deeper corridor**, rather than immediately escorting Dr. Elara Venn out. Elys remains disguised as **Corin Vale**, accompanied farther into the anomalous ruins by **Dr. Elara Venn**, a distressed researcher. His fiancée **Clarissa / Mara Calder** and salvage diver **Davin Rusk** remain outside, by Elys's earlier recommendation.

**New discovery:** The formerly narrow, dry, black-stone corridor reconfigures into a huge cylindrical chamber with immense suspended concentric silver-lined rings and a central vertical column of light. These are not straightforward time generators: Elys's observations suggest a system that **indexes possible states and selects one as the locally allowable reality, correcting causal history**. It is an observed hypothesis rather than a fully confirmed cosmological explanation. A mysterious, attractive **cyan-haired woman** is contained in the central column. She is extraordinarily powerful, deeply intelligent, **friendly and loyal**, and **not a threat**. The containment holds her within a shifting causal reference; her identity is currently unknown.

A **three-meter, six-limbed black-and-silver maintenance construct** unfolds from a wall and announces authorized repairs. It claims Elara **will** request maintenance in the future. Elara identifies the falling figure as her colleague **Professor Saren**, who was apparently subjected to the machine's 'repair' routine; she says it has tried to repair him for eleven years. The machine claims the repair **has not yet begun**, suggesting temporal frame mismatch. Professor Saren momentarily manages to warn: **"Don't let it finish."** Elys has not attempted to interrupt the mechanism yet.

**Key reaction and future footage:** Elys's purchased **Unwelcome Key** resonates, leading the machine to announce **"ADMINISTRATIVE AUTHORITY DETECTED"**, followed by **"PRIMARY AUTHORIZATION: INCOMPLETE", "IDENTITY MISMATCH", "NO VALID RECORD OF YOUR ARRIVAL EXISTS"** and **"YOU ARE EARLY."** The machine displays a holographic **purported archival recording dated 18 Deepfall 13,638 ICE** (eleven years and eleven days after current in-story date). The ambiguous recording shows **the cyan-haired woman** associated with the chamber, her image partially obscured by interference. Its origin and authenticity are **unverified**. It is **not** a record proving Elys was already present or predestined to arrive. The machine's authorization discrepancy remains unexplained.

**Complication outside:** Private household companion AI **Vesta** maintains communications, with some static. Clarissa reports she and Davin are fine but **a second door has appeared where the entrance was**. It is NOT known whether this is the earlier warned-about 'third door'. The machine announces a 'causal maintenance window' has opened, and rings rotate faster. The **cyan-haired woman remains contained but is not an enemy or danger**. Elara pulls an **emergency field cutter** (not a weapon) and asks Corin for a plan. Player has not chosen a rescue, repair interruption, return to Clarissa, or identity revelation. The whereabouts of the **third missing researcher** remain unknown. Preserve player agency.
