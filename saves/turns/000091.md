# Turn 000091 — The Little Box

5 Deepfall 13,427 ICE, about 10:02, inside Lucien's newly opened bakery, Epsilon 7. Elys, publicly Hazael Wheatley, gifts Lucien a beautiful miniature gift-box-shaped pastry, which Lucien eats entirely. **Secret canonical fact known to Elys alone:** eating the gift has made Lucien immortal. There is no outward sign and no other character, including Lucien, Clarissa, Aurelia, Bel, Sentinel or Lethe, knows the pastry's effect. Do not disclose or imply their knowledge without a later story event. Lucien thanks Hazael warmly and treasures the gesture. He still does NOT know Hazael is Emperor Elys, although he knows Hazael owns Somnus. His bakery continues operating. The proposed Somnus development share is 10% of qualifying net profit, not yet finalized, and the longer-term wheat agreement remains unresolved.
