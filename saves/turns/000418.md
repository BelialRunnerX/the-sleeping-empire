# Turn 000418 — Crow Stated

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** Inside the League Senate chamber, Greyhaven, Genosis.  
**Player action:** Crow calls the deceased former Ordel representative an enemy of the state.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

“This man was an enemy of the state,” you say.

The clerk writes it beneath the time of death. Then he adds two words in front of it: *Crow stated*.

The chair looks at the withdrawal notice under the inkstand. “Keep his credentials with that letter,” she says. “He entered this chamber as Ordel’s representative. Anyone reading the record should know how he came here and what the Emperor called him before this sitting ended.”

Nareth’s delegate closes the dispatch book around her finger. “Ordel has no one here to speak for the people across that river. I can speak for the people on my side. They’ll be standing at the landing waiting for word.” She looks at you. “Will you let me send it?”

Behind her, the projection still shows the bend in the river. The chair leaves the question with you.
