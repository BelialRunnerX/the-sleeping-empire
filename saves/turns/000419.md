## Played scene

You widen the projection until it takes in all of Ordel. The river shrinks to a thin line. The outlines of the districts stay on the map, but the towns inside them are gone. The clerk watches the image pass over the capital. Its name is still there.

Estimated fatalities within Ordel: the entire population present at the time of the strike.
Estimated survivors within Ordel: zero.

“Read that first line,” the chair says.

The clerk reads both. When he reaches zero, someone at the far end of the table starts to speak, then sits down again.

“Within Ordel,” Tessa says. “There are people from Ordel here in Greyhaven. Some of them will come looking for their senator.”

The clerk looks at the witness chair. He draws a line beneath the projection’s figures and records the representative’s death separately.

Nareth’s delegate opens the dispatch book again. “My message needs to say there is no one expected back from the other bank.” She stops with her pen above the page. Her request to send word is still unanswered.
