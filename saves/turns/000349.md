## Played record

**The Watch Roster**
*League Senate chamber · Late morning*

You request a list of available Xiphos personnel and ships. The duty office sends back a one-page roster headed **AVAILABLE · NOT ASSIGNED**. The harbourmaster pulls it onto the station display beside tomorrow’s ferry schedule.

- **Twenty-four port guard personnel**, enough for three rotating watches. The roster includes a watch lead on each shift, all reporting to you.
- **Two patrol cutters, *Kestrel* and *Tern*.** Both are available. Either fits the station’s small service berth without displacing a ferry. There’s room for only one at a time; the other could stay at the Xiphos yard as relief.
- **One rescue tender, *Lantern*.** It has a medical bay and room to take evacuees aboard. It can use the service berth in place of a cutter when needed, or stay on call from the yard.
- **One frigate, *Bastion*.** Available, but it would take the berth the harbourmaster keeps open for a ferry that arrives late or needs help.

The harbourmaster lays a finger over *Bastion*. “That empty berth is how I keep a late ferry from becoming everybody else’s problem. If you can do the job with a cutter, I’d rather keep it.”

The clerk leaves the assignment line blank. The ships are available, and the crews are on the roster, but nobody has been ordered to move.
