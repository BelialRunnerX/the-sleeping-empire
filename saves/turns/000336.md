## Played record

**They Can Say No**
*Greyhaven harbour · Tug berth · Same evening*

“You understand that passengers aren’t obligated to go with you if they don’t want to, right?”

“Yes,” the pilot says. Then, more firmly: “Yes. They can refuse. The company paying for the rescue doesn’t change that.”

He lowers his wrist slate. The loose chain knocks twice against the hull.

“You said you thought they worked for the company,” the harbour officer beside the gangway says. “Would that have changed your answer?”

The pilot opens his mouth and closes it again. “No. I suppose I thought *crew transfer* meant somebody else had already asked.” He rubs his thumb over the slate’s darkened screen. “There was a man who stopped at the foot of the gangway. One of the company guards picked up his suitcase and told him to follow. I saw him go. I didn’t check whether he felt free to stay.”

He looks back down at you. “I can put that in a statement, if you want. I don’t know what happened to him after he left my sight.”
