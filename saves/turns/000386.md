# Turn 000386 — Both Have to Agree

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** League Senate chamber on Genosis.  
**Player action:** Ask whether the proposed seed storage exchange is compulsory; give no verdict.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

“No,” the senator says. “Either district can refuse. I meant exchanging space in the barns. The grain still belongs to the people who sent it.”

He opens his notebook again. “If a district sends ten sacks, it can offer room for ten in return. They agree before anything leaves the village. If the other district needs every loft it has, the sacks stay home or go somewhere else by agreement.”

He glances toward the clerk. “I said every district should have the right to do it. Write down that both have to agree. I should have said that the first time.”
