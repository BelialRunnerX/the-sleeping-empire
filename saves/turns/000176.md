### The Machinery Has Rights, Apparently

📍 **Lumenfall Free Adventurers’ Guild, Veyrath** · **9 Deepfall, 13,627 ICE · Late morning, ~11:47** · Technical testing wing

The strategy chamber's simulated ocean dissolves into a grid of blue light. On the wall, three results remain stubbornly visible: **Elys Somion — 99**, **Celeste Somnion — 98**, **Clarissa Somnion — 96**.

Celeste stands beneath her score with the unmistakably pleased expression of someone who has just been called an idiot by a fictional naval officer and considers it an excellent morning. Her question to you—whether Garrick will allow her to fail quite so spectacularly again—hangs unanswered.

Garrick presses two fingers against the bridge of his nose.

“Technical assessment,” he announces. “Diagnostics, repair, fabrication, and systems judgment. You will be evaluated individually. You will not be rewarded for destroying the apparatus merely because you can replace it.”

A set of thick shutters rises behind him. Beyond them waits an immaculate workshop: dismantled field generators, a disabled service automaton, power-routing benches, sealed component drawers, and a compact starship-engine simulator. Yellow safety lines run across polished black flooring.

Clarissa studies the benches rather than the scores. “You have failure-state isolation on the engine simulator, I assume?”

“Three independent layers,” Garrick replies at once. “And a fourth installed this morning.”

Celeste tilts her head. “Because of us?”

“Because,” Garrick says, “I have met adventurers.”

A junior technician in orange coveralls rolls a toolbox to the first station, checks its tamper seal, and retreats behind the viewing partition. Garrick gestures toward the entrance, but does not yet call a candidate.

“Before we begin: questions about the rules?”
