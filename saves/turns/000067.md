# Turn 000067 — The Second Coming of the Telephone

**Player selected option 1 after turn 000066:** Call Bel again while standing directly in front of her.

**Date/location:** 4 Deepfall, 13,427 ICE, around 23:02, completed private Nimbus greenhouse during the housewarming.

Elys, in public as Hazael Wheatley, calls Chief Diplomat Bel again via her private communicator despite standing two meters away. Bel answers in a formally exasperated tone, says this communication is entirely unnecessary, waves at him from nearby, and threatens to draft rules against needless same-room calls. Sentinel makes dry technical remarks. Clarissa laughs and retreats behind a flowering tree. Lucien notices the devices and concludes, incorrectly, that they are testing network latency; he does not learn Hazael's true Imperial identity. Bel retaliates with an official appointment request for an audience with the Emperor, marked immediate and located one meter from him, regarding appropriate use of communications technology. Lucien sees the request and innocently wonders whether he could ask the Emperor to invest in his bakery. Bel says she needs a long holiday. The player has not replied to the audience request. No pastry company or wheat sale exists yet; Nimbus remains the completed private residence co-owned by Elys and Clarissa. Somnus and thirteen Mnemosyne worlds unchanged.
