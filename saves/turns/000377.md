## Played scene

“I’d have the landing authorities pay,” Ordel’s senator says. He lets go of the chair and opens the survey accounts. “Use the dues they already collect from boats. A marked post is cheap. The expense is sending someone out to check the depth and move the safe mark when the river shifts. Pay the crews who already do the soundings to do that work.”

He runs his finger down the two columns for Ordel and Nareth.

“At a shared crossing, each bank pays for its own post. They split the cost of the soundings, the way we split the survey this week. Where a landing is too small to cover its share, it applies to the Senate for a grant. Give the Senate the cost before it votes on the money. I don’t have a reliable total for all of Genosis.”

Tessan’s senator looks up. “You’re taking it out of the dredging dues?”

“Out of ordinary landing dues. The disputed dredging money stays where Aurelia ordered it held.” He turns back to you. “Local money first. Senate money where there’s a shortfall. No new toll.”
