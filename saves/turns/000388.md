## Played scene

The shot cuts through the chamber. The senator falls, and his notebook lands open a few feet away.

The clerk flinches hard enough to drag his pen across the page. He looks at the mark, turns to a clean sheet, and asks the senator’s aide for his full name.

“Edren Vale,” she says. When the clerk starts writing, she adds, “He has a daughter waiting in the courtyard. She came to walk him home.”
