# Turn 000089 — The Wheatley Special

5 Deepfall 13,427 ICE, at Lucien's bakery on Epsilon 7. Hazael insists on buying the signature pastry instead of accepting a free gift, pays its menu price, and offers a bite to Clarissa. He says, 'I hope you like my crème Darling.' Clarissa appreciates the pastry and responds playfully. Lucien recognizes their flirtatious wordplay, Bel and Aurelia groan, and Sentinel comments dryly. Lucien still does not know Hazael is Emperor Elys. The Somnus net-profit development draft and wheat supply agreement remain unresolved.
