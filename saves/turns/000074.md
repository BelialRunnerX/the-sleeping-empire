**Player choice:** 1 — “Because you're my friend.” Respond to Lucien's question why Somnus's wealthy owner would spend time with a modest baker.

He dismisses the floating registry showing Somnus's considerable businesses and thirteen planetary Mnemosyne computers so he can look at his friend rather than the corporate portfolio. On Epsilon 7, Somnus has acquired a prospective commercial site and commenced permitted preliminary groundworks for a future entire cream-puff-shaped bakery, but the full structure remains unbuilt and building/food-service permissions are incomplete. Lucien continues to dream of becoming a future commercial pastry rival and promises a front-table invitation to his friends for the opening, with genuine pastry criticism welcome. **Lucien now knows Hazael owns Somnus, but does not know Hazael is Emperor Elysomnion**; protected identity remains undisclosed.
