# Turn 000402 — Under the League’s Seal

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** Inside the League Senate chamber, Greyhaven, Genosis.  
**Player action:** Crow observes; he issues no new direction.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

By the next sitting, the clerk has six signed statements. Ordel’s senator has brought his in himself. The first line reads, “I was permitted to sit.” Farther down, in smaller writing, he’s added, “I asked for a physician for Sera Dain and returned to my seat when ordered.”

The chair calls the motion to circulate the accounts. Tessan’s senator asks whether the copies will go out under the League’s seal. The clerk says yes.

“Then send the trial minutes with them,” she says. “My government will need to know which measures Crow issued. And if somebody asks whether I voted for them, I want them to be able to look at the record.”

A replacement senator asks whether his government will be expected to endorse the accounts when it receives them. Nareth’s delegate shakes her head. “I’m asking you to send what the witnesses signed. Your assembly can decide what it makes of them.”

The vote is twenty-four in favour, eight against, and three abstaining. The chair waits while the clerk reads all thirty-five names back. One of the new senators asks him to repeat hers. She voted yes. The clerk repeats it.

Tessa rises while the packets are still being folded. “I want the regency to answer one question before we ask another witness to walk through these doors. Can the Empire give anyone attending this chamber a guarantee that they’ll be allowed to leave?”

A senator near the aisle asks whether she expects Aurelia to bind the Emperor.

“I expect her to tell us if she can,” Tessa says. “If all she can do is promise what her own officers will do, put that in writing too. Our governments need to know what they’re asking of the people they send here.”

The chair has the clerk put Tessa’s question on a separate sheet. There’s no vote on it yet. Beside you, the usher sets down a copy of the packet the Senate has just agreed to send. The first sheet records the laws you issued. The rest record what happened in the room while you issued them.
