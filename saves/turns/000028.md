# Turn 000028 — The Emperor and the Farmer

Player (verbatim): The Emporer would like to inform you that Hazael wheatley is working on ensuring the long term economic prosperity of the empire through fair market practices

At Caldris, Elys replies to Aurelia's request for clarity about the administrative review by speaking of the Emperor and his civilian identity Hazael Wheatley as if separate correspondents. He assures her that Wheatley's private enterprise is committed to the Empire's long-term economic prosperity through fair market practices, without disclosing the private strategy of eventual public handoff or clarifying that his earlier strong recommendation was an actual sovereign command. Aurelia notes the conflict-of-interest irony and calls out that both are the same person. Sentinel and Clarissa are amused. Aurelia notices Elys's phrasing 'prosperity of the Empire' rather than personal enrichment but still does not know his long-term objective.

Aurelia offers to replace the broad Somnus administrative review with a narrow compliance and public/private jurisdiction assessment: standard laws and independent regulators remain applicable; private intelligence and security boundaries get focused scrutiny; irrelevant private labs, wealth and unrelated correspondence remain outside scope absent specific lawful reason; confidential corporate long-term strategy need not be disclosed merely because of the review. It is a proposal awaiting Elys's response, not a completed agreement, and no review has yet been canceled. She jokes about whether to address the agreement to the Emperor or Mr. Wheatley.
