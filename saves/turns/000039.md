# Turn 000039 — The Emperor Is Not Subject to the Emperor

Player says SIR's paid government information-management agreement is EXCLUSIVE, and that future Somnus affiliates may supply food, materials, logistics and manufacturing to government agencies, although no such additional contracts have yet been executed. Elys jokes that there is no general Imperial law prohibiting the Emperor himself from having a conflict of interest and challenges Aurelia to stop him. Aurelia accepts the absence of a blanket statutory ban on the Emperor's own private interests, but distinguishes this from the government's contract performance duties, normal accountability, procurement procedures, and operational continuity. Cipher acknowledges that exclusivity needs verification, performance terms and contingencies. The signed SIR exclusivity is established, but its exact limits are subject to the actual agreement. No further government supply contracts are awarded at this point. Aurelia recognizes Somnus's long-term integration could create a productive industrial base to be gifted into public ownership centuries hence, and says she welcomes legitimate services at fair prices but will insist upon delivery. Veronica and Clarissa enjoy the verbal exchanges; Sentinel continues to call the future handoff Operation Birthday Present. The earlier immunity decree remains unratified. Aurelia awaits the Emperor's next revelation or conclusion.
