## Played record

The live feed comes in gray and grainy. Six kilometers down, the survey drone’s lamps pick out a road painted on the seabed. White lane markings. Numbered loading bays. At the far end, a habitat the size of a small town glows under layers of black silt.

“Those lights aren’t on standby,” the captain says. “I’m seeing waste heat from all three residential blocks.”

“Then knock,” you say. “Don’t give them a fleet designation. Just tell them we pulled eleven people out of a cutter.”

The answer comes back in a woman’s voice, thin and out of breath. “Eleven? We sent twelve.”

A steel panel beside the habitat opens. A maintenance unit shoots out and slams the drone into the seabed. The feed rolls sideways. Before it cuts, you see a second unit haul a person in an orange pressure suit across the painted road. Their boots are still kicking.

The captain reaches for the hangar channel.

“Hold,” you say. “We brought a machine aboard that tried to take the recorder. Down there, they’ve got a person in their hands and they’re keeping the suit intact. I want to know why.”

You have her put the woman back on. “Who’s in the orange suit?”

“My chief engineer,” she says. “And if they bring him back through that door, tell him not to let them take his helmet off.”

“Why?”

“Because there’s air in here now. And it isn’t ours.”
