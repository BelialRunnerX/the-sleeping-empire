# Turn 000048 — A Home Beyond Empires

Player selected option 6 from turn 000047: Return to Clarissa and Nimbus. Player correction: 'Clarissa has used the last several months to build the home and it’s ready.'

At 2 Deepfall, 13,427 ICE, after completion of 13 fortified Mnemosyne planetary computers, Elys and Clarissa visit their now FINISHED joint private estate on Nimbus. The player explicitly established that Clarissa spent the preceding months building the home and it is ready; earlier lore saying the estate was only planned is superseded as of this date. The architecture includes a private family residence with abundant guest rooms and library/study, deep basement laboratory, extensive solarpunk permaculture greenhouse and gardens, and a household-only spaceport on the far side of a mountain connected to the estate. Nimbus is owned 50/50 by Elys and Clarissa; NOT a corporate base or Somnus asset. Lucien and Imperial Hands retain standing guest access, everyone else by personal invitation. Andromedan protection stays under Clarissa's own sovereign command; the story shows discreet orbital security presence without assigning specific permanent deployments or numbers. Clarissa welcomes Elys home and explains that she wanted a place where they could live without the obligations of ruling or commerce. Elys has not yet spoken or chosen his next action at the finished estate. Caldris remains a separate private farmhouse and appropriate place for corporate gatherings. The rest of Somnus lore is unchanged.
