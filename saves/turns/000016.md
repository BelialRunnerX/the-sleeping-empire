At Caldris farmhouse dinner, Elys clarifies that Somnus's present private phase is intended to be genuinely profitable and beneficial, while its future destination is public ownership. These are successive stages of the same strategy, not mutually exclusive. There is no declared deadline; the Emperor anticipates centuries or millennia of patient institution-building. Lethe records this long-horizon doctrine and keeps any ultimate public recipient, governance mechanism and transfer date undecided. No ownership transfer or compulsory acquisition occurs.

Elys corrects Sentinel's prediction of Regent Aurelia's memorandum length from 87 pages to exactly 86 pages, prompting jokes from Sentinel, Lethe, Veronica and Clarissa. This is a prediction/joke, not a memorandum actually issued by Aurelia. Lethe notes that while the owner may have millennia, ordinary workers live on shorter timelines, so the current commitment to their prosperity remains important. Veronica departs without any SIR-state intelligence cover structure being decided. Lethe resumes delegated duties and withdraws. Clarissa warmly mentions looking forward to a future Nimbus home overlooking the storms. Elys's next action remains unchosen.
