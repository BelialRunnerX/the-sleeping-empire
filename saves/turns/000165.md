### Guild Advanced Placement

Mira projects a holographic assessment dossier above the counter.

**DIRECT PLACEMENT ASSESSMENT — Registration available**
- Phase I: Capabilities and equipment declaration
- Phase II: Combat and survival evaluation
- Phase III: Simulated expedition and rescue
- Phase IV: Examiner interview and rank recommendation
- Assessment fee: ₡150 per applicant
- Maximum initial award: A-Rank
- S-rank and above require documented field performance, exceptional review, or extraordinary certification.

**MIRA:** "The examination isn't simply about how powerful you are. We assess judgment, civilian protection, teamwork, and whether you're likely to get everyone else killed."

She smiles brightly.

**MIRA:** "You would be surprised how many applicants fail that last part."

A broad-shouldered man in battered graphite armor approaches from the adjacent training corridor. A mechanical forearm clicks softly as he adjusts his gloves.

**MIRA:** "Oh, perfect timing! This is Examiner Garrick Voss, A-rank veteran and senior practical assessor."

Garrick studies Clarissa's composed expression, your civilian attire, and Celeste's enthusiastic smile.

**GARRICK:** "Three tourists wanting to skip the beginner ranks?"

His attention settles on Celeste.

**GARRICK:** "And one of you is a pack mule?"

**CELESTE:** "A very good one."

Garrick's expression remains utterly flat.

**GARRICK:** "Fantastic."

He gestures toward a reinforced examination door.

**GARRICK:** "Before anyone pays a fee, I need to know something. Are you three applying as a coordinated party, or do you want individual rankings?"

Mira positions three unsigned registration tablets on the counter.

Celeste glances at you expectantly.

**CELESTE:** "Oh, I do hope we're a party. I'd hate to be separated from my owner."

Clarissa loses her battle against laughter.

Garrick closes his eyes for a moment.

**GARRICK:** "It's going to be one of those examinations."
