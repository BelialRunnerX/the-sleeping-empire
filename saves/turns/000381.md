# Turn 000381 — The Boats Go Out Tomorrow

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** League Senate chamber on Genosis.  
**Player action:** Say only “Sufficient. Next.”  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

“Sufficient. Next.”

Tessan’s senator sits. The clerk marks the wage rule approved. She draws her letter back toward her and adds a line for her deputy about changing the crew lists.

Tessa Vey rises for Vask.

“Build a signal post at Dun Quay and pay a keeper to stay through the last tide. Right now, if a boat misses that tide, its family walks two miles to an office that may already be closed.”

She lays an estimate on the desk. “Dun Quay can provide the timber and the labour. I’m asking the League to pay the keeper through the first winter. After that, the quay council can decide whether to put the wage in its own budget.”

The clerk reaches for the estimate. Tessa puts it into his hand.

“The assembly sent me to ask for money from a mine that hasn’t opened yet,” she says. “The boats go out tomorrow.”
