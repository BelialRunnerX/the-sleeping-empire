17 Emberwane, 13,427 ICE, Caldris II / Regent Aurelia's holographic call. Elys promises to correct the special suppression function in the Regent's upgraded administrative software, asserting Somnus's extremely efficient AI bureaucracy will prevent unlawful activities. This is Elys's confidence, NOT a confirmed guarantee that no corporate misconduct can occur. Elys then says he cannot guarantee Aurelia's intelligence agency will not misbehave, joking that Veronica may or may not be 'in cahoots' with SIR director AI Cipher.

Elys executes the promised software correction: the Somnus-specific obscuring function is removed from compliance displays, while authorized economic planning links to Somnus remain. Independent auditors can compare reports with outside original records; a clean result is not assumed. Aurelia calls Veronica, who confirms she has discussed potential professional cooperation with Cipher, but has NOT accepted private appointment, transferred classified government files, assigned state agents to Somnus, or established joint operational command. Aurelia directs that any future cross-organizational personnel, resource, or classified information sharing needs express authorization and auditable boundaries. Veronica acknowledges this. There is no secretly implemented SIR/Imperial intelligence merger established by the scene. The sweeping legal immunity decree remains UNRATIFIED without effect. Aurelia, having confronted Elys, Veronica and the software issue, asks whether he has anything else to tell her; Clarissa and Sentinel comment humorously. Next player action pending.
