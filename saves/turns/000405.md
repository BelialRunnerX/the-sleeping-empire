# Turn 000405 — Who Saw What

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** Inside the League Senate chamber, Greyhaven, Genosis.  
**Player action:** Crow challenges whether the circulation packet will be believed.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

“I don’t know. This sounds like propaganda to me,” you say. “Do you really expect your home countries to believe that eighteen people died and rose again? Or do you think they’ll believe you’re making up a story to explain why seventeen senators are being replaced?”

“Some of them will think exactly that,” says Nareth’s delegate. “My assembly heard Sera in person. Even there, one member asked whether she could tell us she’d died or only that she remembered you coming towards her and then waking up on the floor. She said the second.”

The clerk looks down at the new heading. “I’ve made the summary sound as if every death was examined and certified. We didn’t have a physician in the room.”

“Then say who saw what,” the chair says. “I can sign for my own injury. The officer can sign for what he reported about Miren. She can give her own account if she wants to.”

Tessan’s senator takes one of the unsealed packets. “And put the replacement order on a page of its own. My government can know who ordered the vacancies even if it doesn’t know what to believe about the rest.”

The clerk sets the vacancy roll beside the witness statements and reaches for a clean sheet. The chair looks back at you.

“If you’d like to give your own account, Crow, I’ll have it sent under your name with the others.”
