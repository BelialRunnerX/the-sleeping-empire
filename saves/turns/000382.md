# Turn 000382 — The Due Date

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** League Senate chamber on Genosis.  
**Player action:** Spare Tessa and speak the stated exclusion of ferries, harbours and boats. Give no verdict on Halden’s proposal.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

“That’s fine. You live. But the next person to give me something related to ferries, harbours, boats, anything to that effect dies. Next.”

Tessa sits. The clerk pins the Dun Quay estimate to the page marked approved.

Halden rises. He leaves his notes on the desk. “Give every district a public copy of what it owes. I mean taxes, levies, and grain requisitions. If a collector asks for more than the posted amount, he has to show a signed order for the difference.”

“In some villages,” he continues, “you’d have to pay someone just to read it to you. So have the amount read aloud at market on the day it’s posted. The district clerk already copies the accounts for the collector. Have him make one copy for the people who pay.”

He looks down at his notes at last. “And put the due date on it. If you owe two sacks in autumn and a man comes for them in summer, that matters as much as how many sacks he takes.”

The clerk sets down his pen. Halden waits.
