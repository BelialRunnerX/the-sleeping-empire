**One Berth, Three Ships**
*League Senate chamber · Late morning*

You assign all twenty-four guards to the orbital watch. *Kestrel* takes the station’s service berth, *Tern* stays at the Xiphos yard as its relief, and *Lantern* stays on call at the yard for medical support and evacuation. *Bastion* remains unassigned. The civilian overflow berth stays open.

The harbourmaster checks the order against the station map. “When *Tern* relieves *Kestrel*, they’ll trade the same berth. If we need *Lantern* alongside, my traffic desk will make room for her.” She hands the signed copy to the clerk. “That’s all I needed.”

By midday, the station confirms *Kestrel* is berthed and the first watch is on duty. On the port feed, you see a guard step aside to let a family through with a sleeping child draped over a suitcase. The ferry behind them docks on schedule.

The first watch lead sends you a short report: **Station watch active. Relief and rescue available from the yard. No incidents.** Below it, she’s left one question: “Do you want a daily report, or just to hear from us when something needs your attention?”
