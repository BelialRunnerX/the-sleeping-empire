**Lumenfall SSS Assessment Annex · 9 Deepfall, 13,627 ICE · Evening**

The command amphitheater falls quiet just long enough for your voice to carry across the observation tier.

“Celeste, I must congratulate you. You've managed to turn even catastrophic wardrobe failure into a leadership exercise.”

Clarissa's composure lasts approximately half a second.

She laughs into the back of her hand. Garrick closes his eyes like a man trying to resign from existence. Seraphine looks up from the assessment sheet with the weary expression of someone who has discovered a regulation was written specifically because of this family.

Celeste doesn't look around immediately. The simulated sea is climbing the outer floodwall, and forty-three civilian icons remain stranded behind an immobilized crawler.

“Thank you, Elys,” she replies, dry as desert stone. “My next leadership initiative will be procuring clothing that survives contact with reality.”

“You could classify that as a technical competency,” Clarissa offers.

“It was apparently an *operational* deficiency,” Garrick says.

“Enough,” Seraphine replies. “Candidate Somnion, your civilians?”

“Still alive.”

Celeste selects the third reroute. Not the safe-but-destructive power-station diversion. Not the hazardous passenger corridor.

She magnifies the stalled crawler's telemetry, isolates an overheated steering actuator, and directs two maintenance drones to release its mechanical lock. The first drone loses signal under the floodwall's electromagnetic interference.

A warning tone pulses.

Celeste switches the second drone to a narrow-beam relay through the evacuation gate she opened earlier. Its claw catches the crawler's emergency tow eye. A municipal recovery winch takes the load; centimeters become meters. The lane clears.

“Convoy Fourteen,” Celeste says, her voice suddenly stripped of all mischief. “Proceed. Maintain speed. Do not stop for debris.”

The civilian transport glides beneath the emerald gate as a wave breaks over the seawall. Its rear lights vanish into the transit aperture.

**EVACUEES ACCOUNTED FOR: 43 / 43.**

Then the entire coastal simulation fades to pale blue.

**MODULE II — CRISIS MANAGEMENT: COMPLETE.**

A second line appears:

**SCORING AND CERTIFICATION REVIEW: PENDING.**

Celeste finally turns from the projection. Her ruined uniform remains ruined; the universe offers no miraculous replacement. Nevertheless, she delivers a dignified little bow.

“There. I have saved forty-three people and the municipal power station. May I now be permitted to suffer one ordinary embarrassment at a time?”

Garrick opens a new incident-report field.

Clarissa peers at its title.

“*Uniform Failure and Associated Imperial Commentary*?”

“It is a working title,” Garrick says.

Seraphine's stylus comes down with a decisive click.

“Candidate Celeste Somnion: Module II complete. Your final SSS assessment still requires Module III. And I am formally prohibiting that report title.”

Garrick changes it to *Unanticipated Textile Event*.

Celeste stares at him.

“Somehow,” she says, “that's worse.”
