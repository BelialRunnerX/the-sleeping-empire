# Turn 000075 — The Friendship Dividend

On 4 Deepfall 13,427 ICE, at the Nimbus housewarming, Elys as Hazael instructs Lethe to revise Somnus's proposed compensation for developing Lucien's future Epsilon 7 bakery from 10% of sales revenue to 10% of qualifying net profit. Lethe presents the revised draft and explains that normal expenses are deducted before profit participation, with no payment during unprofitable periods. Lucien likes the improvement but wants independent review before signing. No binding agreement exists yet. The bakery will remain Lucien's independent business. Somnus has already begun approved preliminary site works but the bakery is not built; no wheat purchase yet. Lucien knows Hazael owns Somnus but not that he is Emperor Elys. Bel, Clarissa and Sentinel are present.
