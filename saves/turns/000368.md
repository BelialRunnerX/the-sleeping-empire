**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.

## Played scene

“Neither,” you say from the gallery.

The chair waits a moment, then turns the spare print back towards the clerk. “Enter the answer as given.”

Tessa Vey pushes her chair back. “Then I move that we demand advance notice before any Imperial ship fires on Genosis.”

“Demand it from whom?” asks Tessan’s senator. “If you send that to the harbourmaster, she’ll have a handsome piece of paper and exactly as much authority over a dreadnought as she had this morning.”

“From Aurelia, under the League’s seal. She gave us an answer about who can inspect a fishing boat. She can give us one about this.”

Sera Dain has been looking at the first image. “Ask for something the district can use. Where, when, and who to send word to if there are people out there. Uninhabited is a fair description of that rock. It’s also where people from the next valley take their goats when the lower grass dries up.”

The harbourmaster asks for a copy of the map to send with the images. The clerk brings her one, then hesitates over the dispatch. “What do I put down for the purpose of the shot?”

The chair looks up at the gallery once more. Then she looks back at the clerk.

“Leave it blank. Send the district what we know.”
