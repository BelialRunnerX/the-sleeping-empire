# Turn 000101 — The Worst-Kept Secret in the Galaxy

**In-story date:** 5 Deepfall, 13,627 ICE at ~10:17, Epsilon 7. The scene continues Lucien's private video call with Hazael Wheatley after Lucien discovers his own immortality.

**Player's exact words:** "I am the Emperor but honestly it’s a miracle you didn’t figure that out first"

**Definitive reveal:** For the first time, Lucien learns that his longtime friend Hazael Wheatley, privately sole owner of Somnus Enterprises, IS Emperor Elysomnion (Elys). Lucien recites baffling clues from two centuries of friendship: Hazael's command of Somnus, intimacy with Regent Aurelia and Chief Diplomat Bel, a pointless same-room call to Bel, absurd bakery innuendo, masked Imperial broadcasts, and the joke that Hazael might be a court jester. He laughs uncontrollably at the recognition that his supposedly solemn Emperor is the friend responsible for the pastry antics.

**Lucien's emotional response:** After laughter, Lucien acknowledges he is grateful that Elys wanted to keep him alive but hurt that his immortality and his friend's Imperial identity were concealed for two centuries while he watched mortal colleagues and friends age and die. He still cares for Elys and chooses to keep calling him **Hazael**, declining formal court address in their friendship.

**Last beat:** Lucien recalls Hazael's old jest that he might become the Emperor's personal immortal jester and pastry chef. He now asks Hazael directly whether he was made immortal to provide pastries and terrible jokes forever. Await the player's answer; do not decide Elys's words or commitments.

**Continuity:** Lucien's immortality is the same form shared by Aurelia, Bel, Clarissa, Veronica and Lilith, and Elys has said Lucien could choose to return to mortality eventually. The gifted pastry was eaten 200 years before this call. The story now takes place after Somnus becomes the dominant galactic economic infrastructure monopoly, but independent Lucien still operates his bakery. No new business agreement or transformation is made in this turn. Character was visibly Elys in the personal call as Hazael, but previous public throne broadcasts had worn imperial face coverings; Lucien did not discover his identity through a public broadcast.
