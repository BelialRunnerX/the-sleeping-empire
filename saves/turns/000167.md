**THE SLEEPING EMPIRE · CHAPTER CLXVII**

### An Examination of Impossibilities
Lumenfall Free Adventurers' Guild · Veyrath · 10:56

The examination chamber hums behind its reinforced glass. Blue holographic numbers float above the strength-testing apparatus, its maximum rating prominently displayed.

**50,000 kgf · MAXIMUM CERTIFIED LOAD**

Garrick waits for your explanation.

**ELYS:** "Garrick, all three of us can surpass the machine's limit. And me and Celeste, the mule here, have immense magical aptitudes as well. Clarissa can't do magic at the same scale as us, but she's a great strategist."

The veteran examiner looks at you.

Then at the machine.

Then back at you.

**GARRICK:** "All three."

**MIRA:** "That's what he said."

**GARRICK:** "Fifty metric tons."

**MIRA:** "Also what he said."

Garrick exhales slowly.

**GARRICK:** "Excellent. Three applicants claiming they're stronger than industrial lifting equipment. That's a new one."

Celeste looks absolutely delighted.

**CELESTE:** "And I'm a mule!"

**GARRICK:** "Yes. That part I've established."

Clarissa, however, is studying you intently.

**CLARISSA:** "All three of us?"

She glances toward the enormous hydraulic apparatus.

**CLARISSA:** "Corin, I appreciate the endorsement of my strategic abilities, but I think you're going to have to explain the other part eventually."

She isn't questioning your sincerity. She's trying to understand something that apparently contradicts everything she knows about herself.

Garrick's mechanical hand clicks against his wrist terminal.

A red warning appears on his display.

**GARRICK:** "Right. New procedure."

He deactivates the strength-testing station.

**GARRICK:** "I'm not allowing three strangers to turn half a million credits' worth of equipment into scrap metal because someone feels competitive."

Another holographic panel appears:

**EXCEPTIONAL CAPABILITY PROTOCOL — Examiner review.** Standard physical calibration suspended pending an alternative test. Available alternatives: Remote force-field dynamometry, reinforced adaptive combat simulation, and supervised environmental manipulation trials.

**GARRICK:** "I can believe you're exceptional without believing everything you tell me. That's what examinations are for."

He looks at Clarissa.

**GARRICK:** "And if she's your strategist, I'd rather see her solve a problem than watch her break a machine."

Clarissa smiles slightly.

**CLARISSA:** "Finally. Something sensible."

Then Garrick turns back to you.

**GARRICK:** "One question, Corin. Are you looking for the highest rank your abilities can earn you, or are you prepared to demonstrate that you can use those abilities responsibly?"

Celeste leans against the counter.

**CELESTE:** "Can we do both?"

Garrick gives her a long, tired look.

**GARRICK:** "That is rather the point."
