# Turn 000397 — Seventeen Notices

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** Sealed League Senate chamber, Greyhaven, Genosis.  
**Player action:** Crow keeps the chamber sealed and observes. He authorizes no dispatch or reopening.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

The doors stay closed. Edren Vale goes as far as the threshold. The officer tells him again that Miren is alive. Edren asks whether he can see her from where he stands. The officer looks through the doorway and says he can ask her to come to the other side.

At the witness table, the chair has been testing her restored hand by turning the pages of the roll. She stops at Sera Dain’s name.

“Seventeen vacancies,” she tells the clerk. “Prepare seventeen notices. Address each one to the authority that sent the senator. Ask them to send their credentials when they’re ready.”

“Tomorrow?” the clerk asks.

“Some of them won’t even hear from us tomorrow.”

Sera unclips the river schedule from her papers and takes it over to Ordel’s senator. “The ferry landings already have this. If Nareth sends someone else, give them the soundings as well. I don’t want them spending a week finding out what the two of us already know.”

He takes the papers. “I’d rather you told them yourself.”

“I can do that at home,” she says.

Tessa pulls a chair out for Sera at the witness table. The clerk starts to move the roll to make room. Then he sets it beside the trial minutes. The two books tell very different stories about how the sitting went.

“We can copy the approved orders while we wait,” he says. “The villages will want to know about the healers. And the districts need the dates.”

“Copy the minutes too,” says the chair. “Keep the two together.”

From the corridor comes a knock. The officer says Miren is at the inner door. Edren answers before anyone asks him to. “I’m here.” A moment later, the same words come back through the wood.

The chair waits until he’s heard them. Then she looks at you. “The clerk has your decisions. I’ll send them out with the record of this sitting, and the seventeen nations can choose their replacements. Let the people in this building go home. They have work to do in the morning.”
