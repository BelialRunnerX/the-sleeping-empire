# Turn 000086 — The Great Imperial Redaction

5 Deepfall, 13,427 ICE, approximately 09:41, Meridian Commons bakery plaza on Epsilon 7. Player/Elys discreetly orders that all three of his previous authenticated, comedic Imperial proclamations be scrubbed from the in-world official record. The Imperial archival and distribution systems execute his sovereign command: the three decree texts and approved public/archive copies are purged and the public signs return to ordinary advertisements. The public and witnesses retain their memories; the administrative system retains a content-free audit record that a sovereign purge occurred. The wording of the original decrees remains canonical in earlier fictional story turns but no longer exists in-universe in official transcripts. Aurelia recognizes what happened, is relieved official channels no longer broadcast the texts but asks to be warned before historical materials are destroyed in the future. Aurelia gives archivists a neutral index statement that three ceremonial proclamations issued at the bakery were withdrawn by the Emperor, citing personal embarrassment. Bel, Clarissa and Sentinel point out that witnesses still remember and Aurelia promises she can give her future presentation from memory. Lucien notices the displays vanish and is relieved customers will stop asking if the bakery offers medical services. Lucien knows Hazael owns Somnus but still does NOT know he is Emperor Elys. The bakery is open and selling pastries; the unsigned 10%-of-qualifying-net-profit Somnus development proposal and unresolved longer-term wheat agreement remain. Thirteen Mnemosyne worlds and other established holdings remain unchanged. Await player action.
