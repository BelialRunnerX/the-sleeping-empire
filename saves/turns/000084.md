# Turn 000084 — The Regent Cannot Stomach It

5 Deepfall 13,427 ICE, ~09:33, Epsilon 7 capital, newly open cream-puff-shaped bakery. The player, Emperor Elys undercover as Hazael Wheatley, discreetly issues a second authenticated Empire-wide comedic public decree with the exact text: “it appears the regent is making a quick escape as her guts have been rekt, and she couldn’t stomach any more”. The unusual spelling is preserved intentionally. The proclamation replaces the first on many public screens and appears above Lucien's bakery. Aurelia, departing for routine governmental duties, reads the new decree, confronts Elys over its implication that a pastry defeated her, and notes its wording. Bel and Clarissa laugh; Sentinel jokes about medical historians interpreting 'rekt'. Lucien sees more demand at his bakery; Lethe oversees legitimate ingredient resupply, but declines to endorse the proclamation as a business-development technique. Aurelia archives both proclamations as authenticated ceremonial commentary rather than binding public policy and vows to include them in the future public-speaking lecture materials, inviting government dignitaries and Lucien. Lucien still knows Hazael owns Somnus but NOT that he is Emperor Elys. The bakery is open; the unsigned 10%-of-qualifying-net-profit draft and unresolved longer-term wheat supply persist. Await the player's next action.
