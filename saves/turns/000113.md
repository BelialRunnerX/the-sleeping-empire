# Turn 000113 — The Crown Stays Where It Is

5 Deepfall 13,627 ICE, approximately 22:21, Elys's birthday party at the private Nimbus estate. Regent Aurelia asked whether Elys's promised birthday handover of 'everything' meant abdication of the Imperial Throne in addition to the transfer of his asset portfolio. Player Elys clarifies in his exact words: "I remember promising to hand over my assets not the throne, regardless maybe someday yes but not today".

**Canonical decision:** Elys remains Emperor Elysomnion, with no abdication, no succession appointment and no change of sovereign authority today. He allows that he might someday consider stepping down, but that is not a present commitment. His prior birthday promise concerned the handover of his assets, including Somnus holdings, into the Throne's institutional asset structure with Lethe as the designated **administrator**, not new sovereign or personal owner. Regent Aurelia remains in office. Institutional documentation and operational transition are to be handled without disruption; no independent transfer of Clarissa's property or her Andromedan rule occurs.

Aurelia and Bel express enormous relief at the clarification; Clarissa jokes about discussing abdication well before a birthday cake. Sentinel claims to have prepared succession contingency paperwork, which Aurelia tells him to discard. Lucien humorously compares ruling the Empire to running an enormous bakery and urges the guests to finally eat the birthday cake he prepared. The birthday celebration continues, with friends raising toasts to an asset promise fulfilled and the throne unchanged. No actual abdication has occurred.

**Ongoing canon:** Elys owns and shares his private Nimbus residence with fiancée Clarissa, who independently rules Andromeda. Somnus's economic infrastructure dominance followed a two-century expansion and is now entrusted to Lethe's institutional asset administration. Lucien independently owns his bakery and is now aware that friend Hazael is Elys and that the magical gift-box pastry made him immortal two centuries earlier.
