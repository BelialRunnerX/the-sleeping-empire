# Turn 000315 — Alderford Again

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc · Genosis · Middle.  
**Record type:** Backfill synopsis. Chronology backfill from surviving played material; not a verbatim reconstruction or one-to-one voice turn.  
**In-world date:** Unestablished where not directly recorded.

## Played record

Iona wants to understand the ferry, and Crow's father invites them after the grain-cart rush. They arrive at Alderford using the front door arrangement. The upper ferry is working and the lower landing stays closed while new timber goes in. Iona helps with the rope rather than asking for operations to stop. Crow’s signed honor, Crow the Mischievous, is recorded on his quartz ID; Crow remains his legal name. His holdings extend fifty miles around Greyhaven. Crow later leaves Iona to her visit and returns to the capital to seek an audience with the Queen.
