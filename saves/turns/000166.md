Lumenfall Free Adventurers' Guild · Veyrath · 10:54

The blue-white light of the registration holograms washes across Garrick's battered graphite armor.

**ELYS:** "Individual rankings, sir."

Garrick gives an approving nod.

**GARRICK:** "Good. Each of you earns your own standing. No one gets carried to A-rank by a stronger teammate."

**CELESTE:** "Oh, I don't mind carrying people."

**CLARISSA:** "We know."

Three separate applications appear before Mira.

You turn toward Clarissa.

**ELYS:** "Mind your strength."

Clarissa raises an eyebrow.

**CLARISSA:** "My strength?"

The words carry a significance she cannot yet understand.

Unknown to her, you have blessed her with **overwhelming physical might**—a gift whose magnitude she has never been given occasion to discover.

Clarissa studies your expression.

**CLARISSA:** "Corin, I know how to handle myself."

She pauses.

"Although the fact that you felt the need to warn me is becoming slightly concerning."

Celeste's turquoise eyes flick between you and Clarissa.

A knowing smile threatens to overtake her face, but she says nothing.

Garrick taps his mechanical wrist against a nearby control panel.

**GARRICK:** "First examination is physical calibration. Strength, speed, coordination, reaction time. Standardized equipment, increasing resistance."

He points through a glass partition.

Inside the testing chamber stands a reinforced machine resembling an enormous hydraulic press, fitted with paired grips and a floor-mounted force plate.

**GARRICK:** "The strength station is rated to withstand fifty metric tons of force. More than enough for virtually any applicant."

Clarissa looks at the apparatus.

Then back at you.

**CLARISSA:** "Why do I suddenly suspect you're not worried about me getting hurt?"

Garrick notices her expression.

**GARRICK:** "Something I should know about?"

Celeste clasps her hands behind her back.

**CELESTE:** "Oh, this is going to be *wonderful*."

And Clarissa waits for your explanation.
