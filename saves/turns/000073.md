# Turn 000073 — The Wheatley Revelation

**Player dialogue:** 'Lucien have you even stopped to look at who the registered owner of Somnus is?'

4 Deepfall 13,427 ICE, ~23:31, private Nimbus greenhouse housewarming. Lucien has just seen Lethe arrange lawful preliminary groundbreaking of his proposed cream-puff-shaped bakery on Epsilon 7 within five minutes, with a proposed 10% of bakery revenue development agreement still UNEXECUTED. Lucien asks Hazael who he is. Hazael directs Lucien to the public corporate ownership registry. The registry identifies **Hazael Wheatley** as the 100% beneficial owner of **Somnus Enterprises**, with Lethe executive overseer. Lucien is astonished that his supposedly ordinary farming friend owns the vast conglomerate, including fleets, mining, factories, and 13 Mnemosyne-class planetary computers. Bel, Clarissa and Sentinel knew. Lucien asks why Hazael never told him; he reaffirms their friendship, promises Hazael the first cream puff from the bakery, and insists on a fair price for wheat rather than accepting charity. He asks why someone so wealthy would spend time with a baker who makes constant embarrassing puns. **Lucien now knows Hazael owns Somnus but does NOT know Hazael is Emperor Elys.** The registry lists Hazael, not his protected Imperial identity. Somnus owns the Epsilon 7 development site and initiated lawful preliminaries; the full bakery remains unbuilt with licenses and façade design pending. No agreement binding Lucien to 10% revenue and no wheat contract. Await player response to Lucien's question.
