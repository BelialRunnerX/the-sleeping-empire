# Turn 000117 — The Emperor's Unfair Advantage

5 Deepfall 13,627 ICE, ~22:48–22:51. At Elys's birthday celebration at Nimbus, he genuinely challenges Regent Aurelia to a duel in a barren, uninhabited star system. Aurelia agrees to a friendly duel with no governmental consequences or unnecessary destruction. Elys transports them to uninhabited system K-000 while guests observe remotely. Aurelia begins to manifest her star-derived abilities. Elys snaps his fingers and **strips/suppresses all her extraordinary power**: cosmic energy, stellar abilities, supernatural defenses, and the supernatural resilience maintaining her immortality are unavailable. She retains her identity, intellect, memories, and her office as Regent. Elys moves easily and defeats her harmlessly within approximately three seconds by touching her shoulder and upsetting her balance. No injuries, deaths, damage to inhabited places, abdication, or succession. The spectators joke about the grossly unequal contest and Sentinel proposes 'The Three-Second War'. Aurelia calls the fight unfair and, noticing her abilities remain inaccessible after the duel, asks Elys directly whether he will restore her powers because she does not want to remain mortal. **Do not presume her powers have been restored; they are still suppressed pending player's choice.** Elys remains Emperor; Lethe is asset administrator of the Imperial Throne and Somnus. Lucien is immortal, owns his bakery independently and knows Hazael's real identity; Bel and Lucien are not formally a couple. Player prefers simple numbered emoji choices, not interactive cards.
