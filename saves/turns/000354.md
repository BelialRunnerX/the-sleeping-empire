## Played record

Acting on Elys’s private direction, Regent Aurelia issues a sealed decree, read aloud to the Senate. It classifies Genosis as a semi-autonomous Imperial subregion and says Imperial law applies and prevails, while member governments and the League retain offices and local administration. This is an issued decree, not evidence all transition questions, objections or political consequences are resolved. Elys later describes annexation as the intended culmination of Crow’s arc; that later culmination is not assumed already played.
