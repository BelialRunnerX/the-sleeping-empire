# Turn 000058 — The Professional Creamers

Player, as Elys/Hazael, told Lucien: "You’ll make a whole bunch of professional creamers".

On 4 Deepfall 13,427 ICE, at approximately 22:24 during the housewarming at the completed Nimbus private greenhouse, Hazael continues teasing Lucien about his hypothetical bakery "Cream-Pie-R." Lucien takes "professional creamers" earnestly as a suggestion to train and certify skilled pastry makers. He improvises titles including Junior Creamer, Professional Cream-Filling Specialist, Master Choux Technician and Grand Creamer, First Class, then imagines a Galactic Guild of Professional Creamers. Clarissa laughs helplessly, Bel begs for more conventional terminology and jokes about resigning from diplomacy, and Sentinel offers dry commentary. Lucien almost figures out the joke but mistakenly concludes they need a professional association. No guild, academy, credentials or business registered, no wheat contract or commercial purchase; the ideas are pure comedic brainstorming. Lucien remains unaware Hazael is Emperor Elys, and the user controls Elys's next choices. Nimbus estate and thirteen Mnemosyne computing worlds remain unchanged.
