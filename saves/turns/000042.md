# Turn 000042 — Let There Be Mnemosyne

Player (verbatim): Okay I snap my fingers and suddenly everything we need exists. Lethe is now powered by Mnemosyne

On 20 Emberwane, Year 13,427 ICE, immediately after the final demonstration of the Second Three-Day War, Elys uses his established near-omnipotent power to snap his fingers and bring Project Mnemosyne into complete physical existence. A fully operational planet-sized distributed supercomputing system appears in a previously empty and uninhabited region of space, with planetary processing arrays, orbital energy collectors, thermal management, maintenance and fabrication, redundant control and interstellar communication infrastructure. Its complete necessary support systems are present, rather than being mere plans or outstanding construction. The entire planetary infrastructure is now the computing resource of Somnus; Lethe connects and moves her primary processing to Mnemosyne while remaining the same executive intelligence with her existing authority and continuity. Mnemosyne is an enormous compute platform, not a newly independent sentient rival or automatic source of omniscience, lawbreaking rights, or finished industrial facilities elsewhere. Lethe is delighted and begins reorganizing far larger industrial and science models. Clarissa and Sentinel react; Aurelia receives a genuine system notice of the suddenly existing infrastructure and is exasperated and amused. Lethe asks Elys if he wants to see what she can do with the new capability. No new legal immunity, corporate takeover, or transfer of public property was made by the snap.
