# Turn 000366 — Five Metres

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** Aboard Elys's unnamed Imperial super-dreadnought.  
**Player action:** Direct the captain to make a five-metre crater in an uninhabited area. No further player action is recorded.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

Your captain asks a survey officer to find a bare stretch of rock well away from roads, fields, and settlements. The officer brings up a patch of grey stone and holds the image long enough for its shadows to move. There’s nothing there but stone.

The dreadnought fires once. On the survey feed, a puff of dust rises and drifts out of frame. When it clears, there’s a shallow bowl in the rock where there was flat ground a moment before.

“Five metres across,” the captain reports. She leaves the before and after images beside each other on the display. “Shall I mark the coordinates for you?”
