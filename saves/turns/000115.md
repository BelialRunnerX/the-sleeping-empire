# Turn 000115 — The Wisest Battle Is the One Not Fought

5 Deepfall 13,627 ICE, ~22:31, Elys's birthday celebration at his private Nimbus estate. Aurelia awaits Elys's response to this peaceful bureaucratic challenge. **No actual duel or change of sovereignty has occurred. Elys remains Emperor.** Lethe has been appointed administrator of the Throne's assets, not sovereign or personal owner. The birthday party and cake continue.
