## Played scene

You pick up the hand-sized piece of granite the usher laid on the rail.

“Do I have your attention?”

You wait. The clerk lifts his eyes from the roll. At the bottom of the stairs, one of the mason’s apprentices nudges the other. You look from bench to bench until the last senator has turned toward you.

You close your hand. The granite crunches. For a moment a corner is still visible between your fingers. Then it gives, and grey dust pours through your fist onto the flagstones. You rub your fingertips against your palm, brushing off what’s left with the same hand.

“I would like to find someone who can do the same thing.”

The younger apprentice looks down at his own hands. No one gets up.

Tessa Vey looks across at the chair. The chair has taken her finger off the roll. For a moment she studies the dust at the foot of the stairs. Then she looks up at you.

“I can ask the room,” she says. “Is that what you’d like me to do?”
