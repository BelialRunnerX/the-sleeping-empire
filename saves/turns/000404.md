# Turn 000404 — Both Counts

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** Inside the League Senate chamber, Greyhaven, Genosis.  
**Player action:** Crow challenges the omission of Miren Vale and asks what casualty count means after full restoration.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

“You left Miren Vale off the casualty list,” you say. “And is there really anyone to count as a casualty if everyone is alive and well?”

The clerk draws the master copy towards him. “I took the number from the vacancy roll,” he says. “Miren never held a seat. I should have counted her.”

He reads the officer’s report again, then looks at the chair. She has both hands flat on the table.

“If someone opens this packet to learn who needs burying or tending, the answer is no one,” he says. “If they open it to learn what happened here, the answer is eighteen people died and were brought back. The chair was gravely injured and healed.”

“Put the outcome on the same page,” the chair says. “A reader shouldn’t have to turn it over to find out we all lived.”

The clerk strikes through the heading and writes both counts in full. He puts the seventeen vacancies on a line of their own. Then he calls back the copyists before anyone puts a seal on a packet.
