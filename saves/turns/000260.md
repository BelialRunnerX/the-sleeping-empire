## Narrated scene

Elys asks **Mira**, the established Lumenfall Free Adventurers' Guild receptionist, about the **unranked missing-planet observatory bulletin**. Mira shows a public holographic reconstruction of a barren, formerly unremarkable iron-red rocky planet in the outer Lumenfall system. Multiple public observatories agree the planet existed in a stable orbit before a recent intense emerald-white impact-like illumination, rapid contraction/inward collapse, disappearance to a tiny luminous point, and complete absence of the planetary body afterward. There is **no known surviving intact world, crater or substantial field of planet-sized conventional debris**. A small anomalous gravitational/navigation reading around the former coordinates is **not yet understood**; it does not prove an actual new black hole or other stable remnant. Exact body mass, scientific strength of the residual signature, and long-term orbital effects remain unverified.

Mira says that **no credible cause has been established** in public records, and there is no public identification or accusation of Elys, Aletheia, Somnus, Clarissa, or any other culprit. Hypotheses include unknown spatial phenomena, exotic military technology, engineering catastrophe, or fanciful rumors of divine intervention, but none are established. The body was barren; no habitation detected in the surveyed area, and **no verified casualties**. Observatory agencies hold more detailed data; Mira, as Guild receptionist, cannot access or offer it. The Guild currently holds **only a provisional bulletin, NOT a funded or authorized contract**. If astronomy and navigation authorities confirm a hazard and approve an investigation, it *may* become a high-tier Guild job, but no reward, rank or authorization is assigned.

A nearby Guild adventurer protests that they should investigate immediately; Mira answers that entering an unknown gravitational hazard without insurance is not a sensible expedition, to which he yields. Elys's **sentient staff/spear Aletheia**, hovering at his right in spear mode, communicates privately over encrypted suit channels that the recording is recognizable as their earlier thrown-spear planetary implosion, and dryly jokes that the Guild may hire Elys to investigate himself. Her private remarks are NOT overheard by Mira or the patrons. Mira says she hopes whatever caused it is not nearby; Aletheia responds aloud courteously that this is a reasonable hope. **No Elys dialogue, admission, lie, voluntary disclosure or new chosen act is invented.**
