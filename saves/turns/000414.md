# Turn 000414 — One Week From Today

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** Inside the League Senate chamber, Greyhaven, Genosis.  
**Player action:** Crow sets substitute counsel during illness and gives one week from the current sitting to respond.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

“If you fall ill, your government should send substitute counsel until you recover,” you say. “And you have one week from today to respond.”

The clerk looks at the line where he’d left room for the date of receipt. He crosses it out and writes the date of this sitting.

“Then I need the notice before we adjourn,” Nareth’s delegate says. “Every day it spends here is a day my assembly loses.”

The chair gives the clerk the signed minutes to copy from. Tessan’s senator asks for the substitute rule on the same sheet as the deadline. “My government can name someone now,” she says. “I’d rather they do it while there’s time to tell the person what the job entails.”

Aurelia takes a copy for the Regency. Nareth’s delegate waits at the clerk’s desk while he checks hers against the minutes. He holds it out before the wax has cooled.

“Is there a place for my reply?” she asks.

“The Senate clerk’s office. I’ll enter it when it arrives.”

She takes the notice. Her credentials are still on the table.
