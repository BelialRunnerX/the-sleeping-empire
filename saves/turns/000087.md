# Turn 000087 — A Word From the Throne

Date: 5 Deepfall, 13,427 ICE, ~09:46. Elys teleports from Lucien's open pastry shop in Epsilon 7 to his Imperial throne, activates a system-wide official live video broadcast, delivers the following speech verbatim, ends the broadcast, and teleports back to his prior place on Epsilon 7:

“In regards to today’s incident I’d like to inform the populace that all was said and done in good fun, and no harm was intended. Lucien’s Pastry’s are particularly good and the regent genuinely enjoyed them. As for the other events of today it appears that certain people which may or may not include myself were having a gander. I apologize for any confusion. Please do try the pastries”

Aurelia, Bel, and Clarissa approve of the public clarification and apology. However, the televised throne address shows Elys's face clearly. Lucien watches the official broadcast on a bakery screen, compares the Emperor's face with his friend Hazael, runs outside, and realizes definitively that Hazael Wheatley IS Emperor Elysomnion. This is the first in-story moment Lucien learns that secret. He is stunned and recounts his previous joking behavior, wondering whether he should bow. He remains uncertain about how this affects the friendship; Elys has not yet answered him. Aurelia, Clarissa, Bel, and Sentinel are present. The three earlier comic decrees have been purged from in-world official archives, with a content-free audit trace retained. The 10% of qualifying net profit Somnus development arrangement for Lucien's independent bakery still requires contract review and signature, and longer-term wheat supply has not been agreed. The bakery is open and licensed; Somnus's thirteen Mnemosyne systems remain unchanged.
