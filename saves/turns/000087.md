# Turn 000087 — A Word From the Throne (Corrected)

**Canonical erratum:** Emperor Elysomnion wore his **Imperial face coverings** throughout his on-camera throne address. The broadcast did not reveal his face or establish that Hazael Wheatley is Emperor Elys. Any scene portraying Lucien comparing the Emperor's uncovered face with Hazael and identifying him is **non-canonical**.

On 5 Deepfall 13,427 ICE, about 09:46, Elys teleports from the Epsilon 7 bakery opening to his Imperial throne. Wearing his customary Imperial face coverings, he delivers a system-wide official camera broadcast, saying exactly:

> “In regards to today’s incident I’d like to inform the populace that all was said and done in good fun, and no harm was intended. Lucien’s Pastry’s are particularly good and the regent genuinely enjoyed them. As for the other events of today it appears that certain people which may or may not include myself were having a gander. I apologize for any confusion. Please do try the pastries”

He ends the broadcast and teleports back to the bakery. Aurelia, Bel and Clarissa appreciate his clarification and apology. Lucien watches the covered Imperial figure praise his baking, feels astonished and honored, and remains wholly unaware that his friend Hazael is the Emperor. Elys's return does not itself expose him. Lucien knows only that Hazael owns Somnus Enterprises, not his sovereign identity.

The three earlier comedic decrees were expunged from official in-world archival reproductions, with a content-free administrative audit record remaining. Lucien's cream-puff-shaped Epsilon 7 bakery is open. Somnus's proposed 10% of qualifying net profit remains unsigned awaiting independent review, and the wheat supply remains unresolved. Clarissa and Aurelia are present; thirteen Mnemosyne worlds unchanged.
