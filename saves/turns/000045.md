# Turn 000045 — The Galaxy Is Open for Business

Player selected option 1 from turn 000044: accelerate the takeover; give Lethe another month for Somnus expansion.

The story advances from 20 Frostwane to 20 Thawrise, Year 13,427 ICE (month two, approximately day sixty of Operation Market Dominion). On Caldris II, Lethe reports company ledger totals: 92,600 star systems with active commercial services or facilities (not sovereignty or total system ownership); 6,840 voluntarily acquired firms cumulative; 418,300 executed customer and supplier agreements cumulative; 24 uninhabited industrial worlds secured, with differing commissioning stages. WSS reports equivalent pilot freight delays of 2.8 days versus 11.0 before, roughly a 75% reduction in comparable routes, not a galactic average. Forge and the research AIs have expanded modular manufacturing, with some facilities in operation and others still commissioning. Industrial computing services expand rapidly through Mnemosyne.

Lethe's provisional shares of surveyed addressable markets: logistics 36.8%, computing 41.2%, warehousing 29.5%, manufacturing 12.8%, raw materials 7.4%, agriculture 4.9%. Earlier day-30 comparison respectively: 18.4%, 13.7%, 11.2%, 4.6%, 2.3%, 1.1%. These percentages do NOT equal global galactic shares, and Somnus does not yet control the overall galaxy. Ledger reports substantial recurring service revenue and positive operating margins in several established divisions, but consolidated net profit is not certified and substantial development and acquisition costs remain. No new debt or external equity authorized.

Competitive conglomerates react, including pricing responses, investments and some petitions about potential economic concentration. Regent Aurelia orders normal evidence-based oversight without penalizing efficiency. She revisits the provisional public-handoff planning entry near 14,427 ICE and considers continuity planning but no transfer is agreed. Lethe presents Elys with three strategic options: further market penetration, consolidate profitability and resilience, or invest heavily in transformative technology. Clarissa and Sentinel present.
