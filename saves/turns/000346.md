## Played record

**The Safe Harbour Vote**
*League Senate chamber · Midmorning*

At your request, the chair puts the revised harbour protocol to a vote. The clerk sets the hypothetical ship exercise aside and reads the actual motion.

“Greyhaven guarantees safe harbour, not legal refuge. The harbourmaster or named duty deputy can grant a berth, evacuate civilians, and provide immediate care without waiting for permission.”

She turns the page.

“Safety overrides warrants. Keep a person aboard only if it’s safe. If it isn’t, evacuate them first. A temporary hold ashore requires a verified request from a legitimate government authority. A hold does not authorize a handover. Taking on a legal dispute, intervening offworld, or deploying the military still requires separate authorization.”

The voting lamps click on across the chamber. The clerk waits until the last light settles, then looks up. “Motion carried.”

The chair signs the adopted protocol. The harbourmaster takes the first copy and underlines one sentence twice: *Evacuate them first.* The League deputy asks for a copy to post beside the duty roster.

**Continuity:** The prior emergency ship and warrant discussion was a tabletop exercise, never an incident. This is an adopted and signed policy; no exercise evacuation or holder action enters the timeline.
