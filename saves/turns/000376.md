# Turn 000376 — The First Suggestion

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** League Senate observation gallery on Genosis.  
**Player action:** Declare the fictional trial and terms shown in the scene. No verdict or further action.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

You tell the Senate they’re on trial. Each senator is to offer one way to improve Genosis. If the suggestion pleases you, it takes effect immediately. If it doesn’t, that senator dies and is replaced tomorrow.

The clerk writes down the terms. When he reaches *replaced tomorrow*, the nib catches and tears the paper. He starts the line again.

Ordel’s senator is still standing. His name was the last one called before the doors closed. He looks down at his notes on the notice motion, folds them once, and puts them aside.

“I’ll go first,” he says. “Give every landing on Genosis a public gauge that shows when the river is too low for a loaded boat. Post the safe mark where the pilots can see it before they take a load. Put a gauge on each bank wherever two countries share a crossing, and have their crews agree on the mark together.”

Across the aisle, Sera lifts her eyes. She knows those gauges were in his assembly’s budget last winter. They were the first thing cut when the old dredger’s winch broke.

“We lost a boat last year after two landings gave its pilot different depths,” he says. “He had to choose which one to believe. I’d like the next pilot to be able to look down and see for himself.”

The clerk finishes the sentence. Ordel’s senator keeps one hand on the back of his chair and waits for your answer.
