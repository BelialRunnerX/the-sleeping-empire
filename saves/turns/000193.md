### A Gentle Touch, an Impossible Strength

📍 **Lumenfall Free Adventurers' Guild, Veyrath** · **9 Deepfall, 13,627 ICE · Late afternoon** · Physical aptitude hall

“Begin,” Garrick says.

Clarissa closes her hand around the calibration grip.

The indicator flashes green almost immediately. Her fingers stop.

Then the technician frowns. “That was the minimum registration pressure. We haven't measured the upper range yet.”

Clarissa looks down at the grip. “It felt like nothing.”

“That's not unusual,” Garrick says. “Proceed in small increments. Stop at the first amber signal.”

She squeezes again, cautiously. Green becomes amber so quickly that the readout skips several display intervals. She releases it before it can reach red.

The grip remains intact. The lab does not.

That is to say, the *atmosphere* in the laboratory does not.

“Sensor calibration?” Garrick asks.

The technician checks the independent reference channel. “Both agree. She's at the permitted upper band.”

Clarissa stares. “I barely tightened my hand.”

You remember the earlier hint you gave her: **mind your strength**. It has acquired a more literal meaning.

Garrick does not order her to squeeze harder. Instead, he replaces the grip with a passive force plate and strictly limited hydraulic resistance. Clarissa presses only until the certified limit illuminates. The safety circuit locks the actuator, and Garrick records **strength: maximum certified range; actual capacity unknown**.

“This is absurd,” Clarissa says, not quite laughing.

Celeste, who has been unexpectedly quiet, smiles. “Absurdly promising.”

**Acceleration and reaction.** Clarissa steps onto the sprint lane. She moves too quickly on her first start—not beyond the safe boundary, but fast enough that she must arrest her own momentum far sooner than expected. She catches herself with an instinctive, awkward half-step. The timing system records the highest certification band; Garrick pauses the exercise to explain that speed without controlled deceleration is not a complete success.

Clarissa nods, jaw set. On the repeat she launches within the specified envelope and stops exactly on the indicated plate. At the reaction wall, she taps randomized lights with careful fingertips, every strike just above the trigger threshold.

**Coordination.** The balance platform offers a more surprising challenge: the moving arms are harmless, but her unfamiliar strength can overcorrect a small sway. Twice she adjusts too sharply before slowing her motions and letting the platform move beneath her. The third run is clean.

**Resilience.** The cushioned harness reaches its limited pressure ceiling. Clarissa barely reacts. When the cutoff engages, she asks, “Was that supposed to hurt?”

“No,” Garrick says sharply. “It was expressly designed *not* to hurt.”

“Then excellent design.”

**Controlled exertion.** Finally, she must hold a thin glass tube steady while supporting a calibrated weight. She glances at her own hands with newfound suspicion. Her first grip is too cautious; the tube begins to slip. She corrects it before it falls. With both sensors visible, she finds the tiny interval between too little and too much force and holds it through every randomized instruction.

The tube survives.

Clarissa exhales as though she has just negotiated peace with an especially unreasonable nation.

Garrick signs the last assessment.

“Raw strength and resilience exceed our certified measuring range. Acceleration is at the upper band. Your initial deceleration and coordination required correction; controlled-force performance recovered successfully. **Clarissa Somnion: physical aptitude, ninety-eight out of one hundred.**”

**CLARISSA SOMNION — PHYSICAL: 98/100.**

Clarissa does not immediately smile. She studies her hands, then looks toward you.

“Elys,” she says quietly, “you told me to mind my strength. What exactly did you know?”

For the first time today, Garrick has nothing to add.

All fifteen discipline scores are now recorded. The guild rankings have **not yet been announced**.
