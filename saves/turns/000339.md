**After the Courier Leaves**
*Greyhaven harbour · Port office · Late evening*

You end the supervised visit and leave the interviews with the harbourmaster. The officer walks Marr back to her courier. She returns her visitor badge at the landing, and the harbourmaster logs her departure. No passenger gives the company a statement.

An hour later, the harbourmaster finds you under the office awning. Cold rain has pooled in the grooves of the quay. She brings two paper cups and a thin folder.

“The first interviews are done. Here’s what I can actually show you.”

Sera’s copy of the rescue log records an objection at **18:42**. The tug’s original dispatch record confirms that the crew-transfer order arrived at **18:53**. That establishes the order of events. It still doesn’t establish that the pilot read the alert. He gives a signed statement saying he saw a company guard take a man’s suitcase and tell him to follow. The man with the battered suitcase independently confirms that it was him.

“He’s waiting inside,” the harbourmaster adds. “His sister sent him a voice message just before his ship left. He wants you to hear it.”

Inside, the man sets the brass locker key beside his slate and presses play. A woman’s voice comes through, low and close to the microphone.

*They’re saying I need a supervisor to sign me out. I asked when he’s coming. They said they don’t know. Take your ship. Don’t miss it waiting for me.*

The recording ends with a hard click. The harbourmaster checks the date. “Three days old. It tells us what she was told then. We still don’t know whether she’s there now.”

The man closes his hand over the key. “Can Greyhaven ask her directly?”
