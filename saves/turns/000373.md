# Turn 000373 — Elin Rusk

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** League Senate observation gallery on Genosis.  
**Player action:** Ask that the Ordel millstone report be investigated. Give no additional order after Elin’s reply.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

Ordel’s senator pulls the clerk’s sheet closer. Beneath *millstone* he writes the name of a village.

“My grandfather kept accounts for the mill,” he says. “I can send someone who knows where the books went when it closed.”

Three days later, a messenger comes back with a name: Elin Rusk. She’s alive. She lives with her daughter in the same village, and the village reeve found her by asking who’d mended his gate last winter.

Elin remembered the stone. She said the miller was getting ready to grind another sack when she saw a crack open near the centre. She made him stop the wheel. He told her the stone would last the day, so she showed him how far the crack had gone. She put her hands on either side and pulled it apart in front of him.

The senator reads that part twice. “Did the messenger ask whether she could do it again?”

“He did,” says the clerk. “She asked him whether you meant a cracked millstone or a sound one. He showed her the account of what Crow did. She said she’s never tried to crush sound granite in her hand.”

The clerk puts a second sheet on the rail within your reach. Elin has dictated it herself. She’ll come to Greyhaven and try a sound piece if she can start with a stone she’s handled before. There’s a split half of the old millstone in her daughter’s yard, where someone has been using it to prop a gate open.

At the bottom she’s added one condition. *Send a cart. I’m seventy-nine, and I’ve carried that stone far enough.*
