# Turn 000078 — The Imperial Stand-In

4 Deepfall, 13,427 ICE at 23:51, Nimbus estate greenhouse housewarming. When Lucien asks why Regent Aurelia speaks as if Emperor Elys is in the room, the player (Elys undercover as Hazael Wheatley) responds exactly: 'The regent probably just misses her Monarch but I’m sure I could be a decent stand in since the bakery will be ready in the morning thanks to Somnus Enterprise’s advanced construction technology and integration with the permit system'. Lucien accepts the comic explanation and remains unaware Hazael IS Emperor Elys. Aurelia knowingly teases that Hazael can be an adequate stand-in at Lucien's future opening and offers to judge his imitation of the Emperor, insisting on formal dress, a speech, and solemnity. Lethe treats the morning of 5 Deepfall as an aggressive new project target for the giant cream-puff-shaped bakery at Meridian Commons on Epsilon 7. Somnus mobilizes modular fabrication and parallel inspections, but regulatory approval is independent: site preliminaries are authorized, while final building approvals and food-service inspection are pending. Nothing in this turn grants unauthorized construction or guaranteed opening. Lucien realizes he has not even purchased flour; everyone notes the longstanding unresolved wheat supply. No signed bakery profit-sharing contract yet: proposed Somnus compensation is 10% of qualifying NET PROFIT, not revenue or ownership. Clarissa, Bel, Sentinel, Lethe and Aurelia's remote image participate. Thirteen Mnemosyne worlds and the privately completed Nimbus estate remain unchanged. Await player's action.
