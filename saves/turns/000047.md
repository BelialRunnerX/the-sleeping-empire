# Turn 000047 — The Thirteen Thrones of Mnemosyne

Player: Great now build 11 more for 13 total, and use are defense capacity to arm those systems to the teeth

On 2 Suncrest, Year 13,427 ICE, Emperor Elys orders Lethe to build eleven additional Mnemosyne-scale planetary computers, raising Somnus from two to thirteen total, and to fortify all of their systems heavily using available defense capacity. Lethe launches Project Triskaideka; each facility is planned as a separate, independently powered, secured and maintained planetary computational world in a different stellar region. Somnus Security Command's AI Strategos coordinates independent early-warning, defensive orbital facilities, patrol and interception assets, hardening and civilian safety provisions, using the corporate defense and manufacturing capacity. Elys's separately owned 100 Andromedan-pattern armed ships can support temporary patrols under his authority without being transferred to Somnus. Clarissa's actual Andromedan navy is neither incorporated nor commandeered. Somnus defensive forces remain private security rather than sovereign state military.

After eight months of parallel construction, on 2 Deepfall, 13,427 ICE, all 13 planetary compute installations and their local defensive systems are completed and commissioned. Initial failover and coordinated readiness tests pass; long-run validation and exercises continue. Lethe describes greatly expanded compute, scientific research potential, and independent continuity. Aurelia receives the notice, expresses concern about the powerful fortified corporate network, then confirms Imperial jurisdiction and separation of privately owned and Andromedan government fleets; she intends ordinary civilian security review. Clarissa jokes about whether 13 is enough, and Lethe starts sketching a possible fourteenth without actual authorization. No additional planets beyond thirteen are built. Earlier blanket immunity decree is still unratified. Next choice is open.
