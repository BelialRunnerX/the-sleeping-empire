# Turn 000408 — An Instruction to the Officers

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** Inside the League Senate chamber, Greyhaven, Genosis.  
**Player action:** Crow observes; he issues no new direction.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

The next morning, Aurelia takes the witness seat at the end of the Senate table. The chair places two papers before her. One is the League’s cover sheet. The other is the regency’s single sentence.

Tessan’s senator speaks first. “Does your statement ask my government to disregard the packet?”

“It gives the Empire’s classification of the packet,” Aurelia says. “It places no restriction on what your government may read.”

“Does the Empire dispute any of the testimony?”

“I have nothing to add on the truth of the accounts.”

The senator puts a finger on the cover sheet. “Then my assembly has a label under your seal and six signatures it can ask about. I’ll tell them that’s what you gave us.”

Tessa Vey waits until the clerk has caught up. “Can you guarantee that people who attend this chamber will be allowed to leave?”

“I can direct officers acting on my authority to leave your proceedings alone,” says Aurelia. “I can put that in writing today. I cannot bind the Emperor.”

“Would that protect us if he gave the order himself?”

Aurelia looks directly at her. “My instruction could not override his.”

The chair asks the clerk to read back that answer. He does. Nareth’s delegate then asks how soon the written instruction would reach Greyhaven’s officers.

“Before the end of this sitting,” Aurelia says.

Tessa draws the draft guarantee towards her and crosses out its heading. “Call it an instruction to the officers,” she tells the clerk. “Send a copy to every government that has to decide whether to send someone here.”
