# Turn 000066 — A Call from the Almighty

4 Deepfall 13,427 ICE, ~22:57, completed Nimbus greenhouse at the housewarming. Player Elys as Hazael calls Bel (female chief diplomat, also previously called Bell) by communicator even though she stands within the same room. Elys says exactly: 'I heard you wish to speak with me?' This invokes Bel's earlier despairing wish to 'speak with God' during the pastry innuendo debacle. Bel recognizes Elys on the private secure caller ID, addresses him quietly as Your Majesty, protests that she meant the remark figuratively, and complains about Lucien's prospective pastry guild, embarrassing promotional slogans and the absurd same-room telephone call. Clarissa laughs. Lucien hears portions but is told Bel is speaking with an old friend; he still DOES NOT know Hazael is Elys. After conversation Elys suggests Bel give Lucien some bakery ideas. Bel declines a formal job but offers practical brand advice: The Filling Station is a better candidate than Puff Daddy's subject to name availability; she suggests the slogan 'A Little Joy in Every Bite' and honest ingredients, careful craft and a welcoming shop. Lucien likes the campaign and tries to recruit Bel as marketing consultant, which she declines. No bakery name actually registered, wheat sale concluded, or employment arranged. At the end Bel smiles that she will remember the phone call. Nimbus estate is privately held by Elys and Clarissa; Somnus's thirteen Mnemosyne computing worlds remain operational.
