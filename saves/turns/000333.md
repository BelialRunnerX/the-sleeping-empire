## Played record

**Under Which Authority?**
*Greyhaven harbour · Tug berth · Shortly after docking*

At the tug’s berth, a work lamp throws white light across a deck scuffed bare between the wheelhouse and the rail. The tug’s pilot steps out where you can see him. He stays aboard.

You introduce yourself as Crow, sovereign of Xiphos. You make the distinction clear: Xiphos is a sovereign nation. It hosts the League Senate’s administrative capital, but the Senate’s jurisdiction over that district is not sovereignty over Xiphos. You’re asking questions in your own capacity, not speaking for the Senate or changing the harbourmaster’s orders.

The pilot glances at the harbourmaster. “Then I’d like the record to say which of you is asking.”

“Crow is asking,” she says, opening her ledger. “I’m witnessing.”

His gloves squeak as he grips the wet rail. When you question him about the last pickup, he says he returned the passengers to company officials because the dispatch he received called them a *crew transfer*. He says he did not hear anyone object.

From inside the wheelhouse, a woman speaks. “You didn’t have to hear a word. I entered the objection in the rescue log.”

The pilot turns. A deckhand in an oil-stained blue jacket stands in the doorway, holding a quartz slate flat against her chest.

“I want to give my account somewhere he can’t hear me,” she tells the harbourmaster. “And I want someone to check the time on my entry against the time on his dispatch.”

The harbourmaster has a private room on the quay and a secure line back to it. She waits to see whether you want to keep the tug’s no-disembarkation order intact or make an exception for the witness. The deckhand’s slate is still in her hands; neither record has been checked.
