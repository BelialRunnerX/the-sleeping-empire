# Turn 000399 — The Continuity Motion

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** Inside the League Senate chamber, Greyhaven, Genosis.

## Played scene

You sit in a chair to one side of the chamber, on the same floor as the senators. When the clerk calls the roll, all thirty-five seats answer. Nareth’s new senator has brought Sera Dain’s account with her credentials. She keeps it on the desk in front of her.

The clerk has entered the measures you approved in the register as laws issued under your authority. He reads the heading so every replacement hears it before the chair moves to the day’s business.

“Nareth has a motion,” she says.

The new senator stands. “My assembly sent me here to take my seat. It also sent me with a question. If this chamber is closed again and our delegates fail to return, do our governments have to wait for word from this building before they can act together?”

A senator from an inland country turns in his seat to face her. “Act together to do what?”

“Keep the courts sitting. Move food across a border when a district asks for it. Warn each other if a road is closed. My assembly spent two days getting three different accounts of who was alive. In that time two districts held up each other’s grain wagons because neither knew whose orders still stood.”

Tessan’s senator asks who would speak for the League while the chamber was unavailable. Nareth’s delegate says each government could name a contact at home and put that name on the list. The contacts could coordinate services their governments already run. They would have no authority to pass laws or commit money for another country.

“Then my government could sign that,” Tessan’s senator says. “I’d want the same copy in every capital. A list we can only get from this building won’t help us much.”

Tessa Vey taps the blank at the top of the draft. “Give it a date and issue a new copy whenever a name changes. People keep the old ones. We ought to make it easy to tell which they’re holding.”

The clerk writes that in. Then he asks what event would actually put the arrangement into effect. The new senator from Nareth starts to answer and stops. A winter storm could close Greyhaven for three days. So could an order from the person seated beside them. The same words would have to cover both.

The chair asks the clerk to leave that line blank for debate. He reads back the motion so far. At the end, the new senator from Nareth asks him to read it once more, this time without lowering his voice.
