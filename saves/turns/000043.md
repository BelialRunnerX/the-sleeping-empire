# Turn 000043 — Operation Market Dominion

Player (verbatim): Okay well you have all the infrastructure and ships you need so get started with taking over the entire galactic market

20 Emberwane, 13,427 ICE. Elys tells Lethe that Somnus has the infrastructure and ships necessary and orders the launch of a campaign to take over the galactic market. Lethe accepts the provided infrastructure and fleet as available and begins an aggressive, multi-sector, commercially oriented expansion across the Milky Way. She coordinates Meridian, Granary, Tellur, Forge, Axiom, Cipher and Ledger, leveraging the now-complete Mnemosyne planetary computer for demand modeling, logistics, lawful mining prospecting and acquisitions, industrial research, agriculture, manufacturing, and corporate services. Somnus begins activating routes and commercial services, onboarding customers and negotiating contracts with willing partners and acquisition targets. Lethe explicitly chooses competitive service and voluntary deals, not annexation, coercive acquisition or claiming state sovereignty. Several regional firms refuse acquisitions; Somnus offers supplier partnerships instead. A freight consortium reacts by improving efficiency. Government system alerts Aurelia, who orders ordinary market monitoring but no extraordinary intervention; the market-wide changes are emergent, not a completed galactic monopoly. The new expansion has started and the actual galactic market share is not yet verified. Financial accountability and capital limitations continue; no unspecified limitless spending or fake profits. Prior charter's aim of eventual public ownership remains. Elys can now choose the next course of action.
