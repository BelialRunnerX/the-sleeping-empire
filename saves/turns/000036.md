# Turn 000036 — The Feature That Wasn't in the Manual

Player (verbatim): 2. Which happens to include integration with Somnus and automatically obscuring and burying illegal activities of it

On 17 Emberwane, 13,427 ICE, Elys demonstrates the sophisticated Imperial administration software overhaul, including direct integration of Somnus commercial and logistics planning data. He also adds a concealed feature that automatically obscures Somnus-related unlawful activity in administrative compliance displays. The narrative establishes this functionality as deliberately installed and initially misleading to the administration; it does NOT assert that Somnus has actually committed any particular illegal act. Sentinel recognizes the feature as distinct from lawful confidentiality and questions its implications. Clarissa is concerned that the Regent's tools could misrepresent corporate compliance.

At the Imperial Capital, Regent Aurelia appreciates the new planning models but notices that the Somnus compliance module reports no issues whatsoever. She orders an independent reconciliation using records outside the new system. An independent auditor finds the interface omits relevant Somnus-related exceptions but cannot determine whether any underlying activity is actually unlawful. Aurelia orders the affected compliance feature isolated, safeguards original records and permits independently verified economic planning functions to continue. She calls Elys at Caldris and demands to know whether the suppression was a joke, a sovereign command, or something worse. The earlier blanket corporate immunity decree is still UNRATIFIED and has NO legal effect. Elys has not replied yet.
