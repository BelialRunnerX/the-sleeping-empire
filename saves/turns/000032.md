# Turn 000032 — A Gift for the Regent's Desk

Player (verbatim): Sentinel leave the decree prepared but un-ratified on Aurelia’s desk

Elys directs Sentinel to deliver the previously prepared sweeping Somnus corporate legal-immunity decree to Regent Aurelia's private desk as an expressly unratified draft. Sentinel complies. No sovereign authentication, legal registration, immunity or alteration of enforcement occurs. Aurelia discovers it on her desk, reads the proposed immunity from consequences of violations of Imperial law, confirms the signature block is empty, and interprets the delivery as deliberate political theater. She annotates the draft in red, 'REVIEWED. NOT RECOMMENDED,' asking how Somnus can be for the public benefit while exempt from accountability to that public. She returns it through private correspondence, noting her reserve of three thousand sheets of official stationery. Sentinel presents her annotated reply to Elys and Clarissa at the Caldris farmhouse; Clarissa laughs. The draft remains unratified and without legal effect. Player's next move is open.
