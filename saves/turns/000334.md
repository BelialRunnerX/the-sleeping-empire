**Eleven Minutes**
*Greyhaven harbour · Quayside interview room*

You ask the harbourmaster to let the deckhand alone come ashore for her statement.

“One person, for an interview,” the harbourmaster tells the pilot. “The rest of your crew still has no shore leave.”

The deckhand crosses the gangway with her quartz slate tucked under her jacket. In the interview room, she sets it on a scarred wooden table and wipes two drops of seawater from the screen with her sleeve.

“Sera Venn,” she says for the record. “I heard Nessa ask us not to hand her over. I entered it at 18:42 and flagged it for the bridge. The pilot told you he was acting on a crew-transfer dispatch. What I want to know is when that dispatch came in.”

The harbourmaster checks the copy the pilot supplied. Its receipt stamp reads **18:53**. She sets it beside Sera’s entry. Both records show ship time.

“Eleven minutes apart,” the harbourmaster says. “We still need the original logs to verify those stamps. And the flag tells us the bridge was notified, not who read it.”

Sera nods. “That’s fair.” She puts both hands flat on the table. There’s a black crescent of engine grease under one thumbnail. “One more thing. I’d like to give you the full account. But if I do, am I expected to walk back aboard tonight?”
