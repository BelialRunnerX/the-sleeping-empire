# Turn 000068 — The Imperial Telecommunications Crisis

Player selected option 3 after turn 000067: Forward Bel's same-room imperial-audience request to Regent Aurelia for review.

**Date:** 4 Deepfall 13,427 ICE, around 23:06. **Location:** Completed Nimbus private estate, greenhouse housewarming.

Elys, publicly Hazael Wheatley, escalates Bel's official audience request, which had proposed an immediate meeting with the Emperor standing one meter away, to Regent Aurelia. Sentinel dryly notes that he has escalated the dispute, while Bel is horrified and Clarissa laughs. At the Imperial capital, Aurelia reads the improbable administrative filing and tells her aide not to prepare a ministerial briefing. Aurelia's official reply recognizes the audience request as administratively valid but refuses to intervene, advises the parties to communicate directly, and includes the emphatic note 'HE IS STANDING RIGHT THERE.' In a private message to Elys she points out that possessing thirteen planetary computers, having improved her administrative system, and wielding extraordinary power provide no reason to contact her office late at night; she asks him to enjoy his housewarming.

Lucien sees that Hazael somehow involved the Regent and wonders how he knows such important people, but Bel says only that he has unusual connections; Lucien still DOES NOT know Hazael is Emperor Elys. Lucien asks whether Aurelia could review his prospective bakery advertising, but Bel, Clarissa and Sentinel object. Aurelia sends Bel a personal five-word note 'Stop encouraging him. Please, Bel.' Bel resents being blamed and jokingly asks Elys if he wants to escalate her complaint about the escalation.

No government regulations or policy altered, no wheat transaction or bakery founding occurred, and no new Somnus commitments were made. Nimbus remains the completed joint private home of Elys and Clarissa. All thirteen planetary Mnemosyne systems remain commissioned. The story awaits Elys's next words or action.
