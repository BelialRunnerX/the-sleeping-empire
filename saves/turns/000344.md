**The Name on the Notice**
*League Senate chamber · Morning*

You ask the clerk to add you to the notice as an official witness under the name **Elys Somnion**.

Her stylus stops just above the quartz. She’s written *Crow* on enough of the League’s records to notice the difference. She doesn’t ask you to explain it. She enters the name exactly as given and turns the draft toward you.

**Elys Somnion · Official witness**  
*Present at the Greyhaven tug berth. Spoke directly with the pilot and personally viewed the dispatch displayed on his wrist slate. Did not witness the earlier transfer at the company station.*

The chair reads the entry. “That makes you a witness to what the pilot showed and told you, not to what happened at the station.”

The clerk leaves the other witnesses in the sealed local file. Your name is on the notice itself. If it’s filed as written, both outside authorities will see it alongside the account of your role at Greyhaven. The harbourmaster’s fact-check and the chair’s final signature are still pending.
