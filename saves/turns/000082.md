# Turn 000082 — The Slogan That Ended a Regent

**Player's speech (verbatim):** 'Anyways that’s all folks. Cum Hungry?! Leave filled!'

**When and where:** 5 Deepfall, 13,427 ICE, about 09:26, grand opening of Lucien's new cream-puff-shaped bakery at Meridian Commons, Epsilon 7 capital.

Elys, publicly as Hazael Wheatley, closes the opening ceremony by saying the player's deliberately outrageous double-entendre tagline over the public microphone. Bel and Regent Aurelia are mortified, Clarissa laughs uncontrollably, Sentinel remarks dryly and Lucien objects that no slogan was approved. The crowd laughs. Aurelia takes the microphone and publicly praises Lucien's work while clarifying that Hazael's comments are not Imperial policy on pastry filling. She privately scolds Elys and revises her proposed talk title to 'How Not to Give a Public Speech: A Practical Demonstration.' A civilian woman purchases six vanilla cream puffs, marking Lucien's first ordinary sale. Lucien is sincerely happy. He jokes that Hazael is banned from bakery marketing; Aurelia and Bel approve, Clarissa abstains. The outrageous slogan is NOT adopted or registered as the bakery's official slogan. Lucien continues to know Hazael is sole owner of Somnus but NOT that Hazael is Emperor Elys. The bakery is open with approvals, yet Somnus's proposed 10% of qualifying NET PROFIT development arrangement remains unsigned pending independent review. Long-term wheat supply contract remains unsettled and Lucien bought opening-day flour elsewhere. Completed private Nimbus estate and Somnus's thirteen Mnemosyne worlds remain unchanged. Await player action at the opening.
