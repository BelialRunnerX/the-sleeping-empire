**THE SLEEPING EMPIRE · CHAPTER CLXVII**

### The Rank Exists for a Reason

📍 **Lumenfall Free Adventurers' Guild, Veyrath** · **9 Deepfall, 13,627 ICE · Late afternoon** · Physical aptitude hall

“Well, it's a recognized guild rank,” you point out. “So surely *someone* can test us?”

Garrick regards you for a moment. Then he exhales.

“Yes,” he says. “You're right.”

He turns his tablet around. A formal rank ladder illuminates the screen. Its highest entry is not merely a legend or a hypothetical classification:

**SSS — RECOGNIZED GUILD RANK.**

“My objection was to using *these machines* as the SSS examination, not to conducting one. I should have made that distinction immediately.”

Celeste raises an eyebrow. “An examiner admitting an error. Is that on the syllabus?”

“No,” says Garrick. “And it won't be scored.”

He opens the guild's accreditation directory. The SSS track is plainly listed, along with its authorized route: a registered request, an accredited upper-rank assessor, independent observers, and a practical demonstration with a published safety envelope. The process measures whether an adventurer can reliably accomplish SSS-grade tasks, not whether a hydraulic carriage can withstand unlimited force.

“The guild recognizes SSS,” Garrick continues. “So it maintains procedures to assess candidates for it. We don't demand that you smash enough property to establish a theoretical maximum. That would disqualify you before it qualified you.”

A small chime sounds as his credentials authenticate. He brings up the **Upper-Rank Assessment Desk** and submits both candidate dossiers, including their completed examinations and over-range instrument notes. The terminal responds:

**ELYS SOMION — SSS ASSESSMENT REQUEST REGISTERED**  
**CELESTE SOMNION — SSS ASSESSMENT REQUEST REGISTERED**  
**ASSESSOR ASSIGNMENT — REQUIRED**

Garrick rotates the screen toward you. “That's the actual route. An accredited assessor can administer it, and I can coordinate the venue and safety preparations. Your application is no longer waiting for someone to invent an examination.”

Clarissa, still studying her own hands, speaks up. “And the test?”

Garrick indicates three official modules: an isolated controlled-output trial, an independently witnessed SSS-grade mission simulation, and a precision-and-restraint exercise. Candidates must satisfy the assessor's published criteria across the required modules. There are non-destructive remote facilities for the output portion; the simulation and control portions do not require maximum-power demonstrations.

Celeste smiles at the final heading. “Precision and restraint. Elys, you've finally found an institution that can appreciate your greatest passion.”

“Paperwork?” Clarissa asks.

“Being profoundly overqualified for it.”

Garrick closes the previous exam and looks at you with renewed professionalism.

“Your point is accepted, Somion. **SSS is a real rank, and there are authorized people who can test you for it.** The remaining question is whether you'd like me to summon the accredited assessor through the guild network now, or review the official assessment requirements first.”

He gives the certification desk a meaningful glance.

“And I can issue your existing examination results separately. There's no reason to withhold those while your SSS requests proceed.”
