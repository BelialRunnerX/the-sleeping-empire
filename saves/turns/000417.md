# Turn 000417 — His Name Too

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** Inside the League Senate chamber, Greyhaven, Genosis.  
**Player action:** Crow brings out Ophelia, who takes human form, and gives her an order.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

You bring out Ophelia. She takes human form beside the witness table. You give her the order.

Ophelia steps between Ordel’s representative and the projection. His shadow falls across the flagstones. She kneels and touches it with one finger. It breaks into a scattering of black birds that vanish before they reach the ceiling. The representative dies in his chair. Ophelia stands again, still in human form.

The clerk reaches for the withdrawal notice. For a moment it looks as if he means to take it away. Instead he weighs it down with the inkstand so he can write beside it.

Nareth’s delegate looks from the representative to the empty seat across the chamber. “He came here under instructions from his government,” she says. “Put that down.”

The chair looks at the clerk. “Put his name down too.” She waits until he opens the roll. “Ordel withdrew its representative. We still witnessed his death.”
