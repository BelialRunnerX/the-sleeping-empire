# Turn 000396 — Alive

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** Sealed League Senate chamber, Greyhaven, Genosis.  
**Player action:** Crow accepts the clerk’s suggestion under the trial terms and spares him. With a snap of his fingers he restores everyone killed or injured during the session. He orders the seventeen senators who died replaced, despite their restoration.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

“Very well. You live.”

The clerk reaches for his pen. Before he touches it, you snap your fingers.

Sera Dain draws a breath beside her chair. Edren Vale sits up and finds his notebook open on the floor. At the witness table the chair looks down at her restored arm and closes her hand. Tessa, who has been holding her upright, lets go.

In the courtyard, Miren Vale opens her eyes. Inside, all seventeen senators who died are alive again, and every injury is healed.

“Those who died today lose their seats,” you tell the chamber. “They’re going to be replaced. I said you would die. I never said your death would be permanent.”

The clerk looks down at the names he entered. Beside the first, he writes *alive*. Then he takes a fresh sheet and begins a list of the seats each nation will have to fill.

Edren looks toward the door. “Where’s my daughter?”

The officer answers from beside it. “Alive. In the courtyard.”
