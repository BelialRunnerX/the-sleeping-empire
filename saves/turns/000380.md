# Turn 000380 — The Same Sheet

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** League Senate chamber on Genosis.  
**Player action:** Say only “Return to your seats. Next person.”  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

“Return to your seats. Next person.”

Ordel’s senator gets up from beside Sera. He sits where you told him to sit. For the first time since the doors closed, he looks directly at you without holding on to the chair.

Tessan’s senator rises. She sets the letter to her deputy flat on the desk.

“Let fishing crews name who gets their unpaid wages if they die at sea. Have the harbour clerk take the name when the crew signs on. If a boat goes missing and the owner disputes the claim, let the harbour court hold the wages until it can hear both sides.”

She puts her palm down on the folded letter. “Last winter a skipper’s daughter waited four months for money he’d already earned. The owner said he was still waiting for the boat to come home. He may have been. She still had four months of rent to pay.”

“Who pays for the clerk’s time?” Ordel’s senator asks from his seat.

“The owners already pay to file the crew lists. Put the names on the same sheet.”

The clerk reaches for a fresh page. Tessan’s senator waits while he writes the proposal down.
