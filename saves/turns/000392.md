# Turn 000392 — The Market Weights

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** League Senate chamber on Genosis.  
**Player action:** Say only “That’s fine. Next.”  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

“That’s fine. Next.”

The senator from the inland bench sits. The clerk marks her proposal approved and folds the potter’s letter into the record.

A senator near the aisle stands. “Set one standard for the weights used at market. Keep a set at the district hall, where anyone can bring a merchant’s weights to be checked.”

He takes two iron weights from his satchel and sets them on the desk. Both are stamped with the same number. One is visibly smaller.

“These came from towns a day’s walk apart. A farmer who buys salt in one and sells wool in the other pays the difference twice. Have the district keep the standard, and use the existing market fees to pay for the checks.”
