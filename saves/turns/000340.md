## Played record

**A Safe Berth**
*League Senate chamber · Late evening*

You bring the harbourmaster’s findings to the Senate. The public benches are nearly empty. Rain ticks against the high windows, and a clerk carries the sealed witness statements to the chair rather than reading the names aloud.

You advise the Senate to keep its role narrow: protect the civilians already in Greyhaven, and otherwise stay out of the dispute. That means keeping their whereabouts private and refusing any involuntary handover. It does not mean sending Xiphos ships to the company’s station or deciding what happened there on the strength of a three-day-old recording.

A senator at the end of the front bench raises her hand. “And the sister?”

The chair looks down at the harbourmaster’s report. “Her brother asked whether he could speak to her. He didn’t ask us to send a fleet. Those are different requests.”

The Senate agrees to leave the matter with the harbourmaster. The passengers can stay under her protection, and the tug can keep its berth without city entry. No deployment or inquiry outside Greyhaven is authorized.

As the clerks begin gathering their papers, the harbourmaster sends one last note. The man has found his sister’s personal contact on his slate. He wants to try it himself, but his battery is nearly dead and he doesn’t want to make the call alone.
