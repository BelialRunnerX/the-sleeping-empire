# Turn 000403 — The Casualty Sheet

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** Inside the League Senate chamber, Greyhaven, Genosis.  
**Player action:** Crow opens and reviews the packet; he issues no new direction.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

You open the packet at the side of the chamber and lay the summary beside the trial minutes.

The first sheet gets the laws right. Each is listed as issued under your authority. The village-healer pilot is marked approved for three valleys. The rejected petitions proposal is absent from the list of laws. In Ordel’s signed account, the order of events is right too: he was permitted to sit, later rose to call for a physician for Sera Dain, and sat again when you told him to.

The discrepancy is on the casualty sheet. The clerk has written, *Seventeen fatalities. Seventeen seats vacated.* Both numbers refer to senators, but the heading says *Casualties of the Sitting*. It leaves out Miren Vale, whose death the officer reported and whose name the clerk entered in the minutes. The chair’s injury is in the minutes too.

You turn the sheet over. The restoration is there, in the final entry. It says everyone killed or injured was restored. The next line records your order replacing the seventeen senators who died. The minutes give the full sequence. The summary makes a count of Senate vacancies look like a count of everyone who died.

The packets are still on the clerk’s table, ready for their seals.
