Having localized the actual threat and distinguished it from preserved histories and potentially person-like old imprints, she changes **Genesis Loom staff → Philosopher's Edge spear form** for a focused attack. **Unambiguous outcome: THE EATER OF NAMES IS DESTROYED IN THE SEPULCHER.** The original historical record of how it attacked civilization remains stored, but **as inert record**, no longer an active contagious copy.
