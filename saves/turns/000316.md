## Played record

The recognition and port terms pass 5–2 in the accepted council sequence; reciprocal transit passes separately, 6–1. Signed instruments and the third-day noon proclamation carry effect. Greyhaven is Xiphos's port; Crow takes customs and watch, and separately pledges protection to Elara's kingdom. At Bell Square Iona releases him from his previous personal fealty. She accepts honorary Xiphos citizenship and chooses to wear the ferry-rope ring privately. It is not an engagement.
