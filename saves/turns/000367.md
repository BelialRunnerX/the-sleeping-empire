# Turn 000367 — Whose Land?

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** League Senate chamber on Genosis.  
**Player action:** Go to the Senate to see its reaction. No answer is given to the chair and no further action is recorded.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

Your captain confirms the shot made a crater five metres across in a bare stretch of uninhabited rock. By the time you reach the Senate, the harbourmaster has brought in the dreadnought’s before-and-after images. The clerk has set both on the witness table.

“Whose land?” Sera Dain asks.

The clerk gives her the district. Sera takes a moment to place it. “Then send a copy to their council. They should hear it from us before someone shows up with a drawing and calls it a sign from heaven.”

“Send both pictures,” says Tessan’s senator. “The first one is the reason I’m still sitting here.”

Tessa Vey takes the two images and walks them over to the chair. “We spent a week getting the registry to accept that Vask can object to what a mining company does on the moon. I’d like the minutes to show whether anyone on Genosis gets to know in advance when a dreadnought does something to Genosis.”

The chair asks the harbourmaster if her office received a notice before the shot.

“After,” she says. “The report came straight through. If there’d been a notice first, I’d have known which district to call when it happened.”

The senators turn as you come in. The chair slides a spare print towards the empty seat at the table.

“Crow, was the shot a test for your captain, or a demonstration for this chamber? If it was for us, you have the floor.”
