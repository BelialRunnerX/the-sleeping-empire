# Turn 000307 — Alderford Landing

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc · Genosis · Early.  
**Record type:** Backfill synopsis. Chronology backfill from surviving played material; not a verbatim reconstruction or one-to-one voice turn.  
**In-world date:** Unestablished where not directly recorded.

## Played record

Hazel, a nonroyal ferryman's son who joins the guild at eighteen, takes his first contract to find surveyor Dain. He rigs a second rescue line, brings Dain and the woman holding him above the water up from the millrace, and discovers two fully cut braces plus a marked third beneath the floodgate. Hazel secures guild pay for tracker Nessa, offers to cover it himself if required, reports before approaching the tollhouse with backup, and questions the woman's brother separately. Their tracks show proximity, not guilt. He follows the missing board survey, obtains the forged note and Berran's six-wagon letter from the inquiry, checks his father’s account against Dain's documented warning and the apprentice who delivered it, apprehends the fake watchman/carter, and records separate accounts. The carter admits a paid false report and diversion; Berran admits starting the third cut and bargaining away the carter's horse debt for six wagons. **Player-direct:** order Berran arrested, keep the damaged landing safe, give the evidence to the watch. **Outcome:** watch takes Berran into custody, refers the statements to the magistrate; first two cuts remain disputed. Millwright braces the floodgate, lower landing stays closed pending timber replacement, upper ferry remains open, and the evidence clears Hazel's father.
