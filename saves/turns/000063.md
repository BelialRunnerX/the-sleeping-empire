# Turn 000063 — The Great Puff of Commerce

Player selected option 1 from turn 000062: Encourage Lucien's idea for a giant rooftop cream puff at his future bakery.

On 4 Deepfall 13,427 ICE, around 22:46, during the Nimbus greenhouse housewarming, Hazael/Elys encourages Lucien's proposed giant decorative cream puff atop his future bakery, tentatively named The Filling Station. Lucien sketches a landmark called 'The Supreme Puff.' Bel initially objects to designing a roof before a building but recognizes a lightweight, properly engineered rooftop sculpture could serve as commercial signage; Sentinel recommends structural and wind-load checks and a hollow, independently supported construction. Clarissa proposes that Lucien first obtain affordable premises, refine and sell pastries, and build the extravagant sign only once the bakery is financially stable. Lucien enthusiastically writes three phases: make good pastries, achieve financial stability, build the Supreme Puff. He says he'd place Hazael's name inside the eventual sculpture as credit for encouraging his idea. The bakery, sign, contracts and investment remain proposals only, not constructed or executed. No wheat deal has been concluded; Lucien remains unaware that Hazael is Emperor Elys. The housewarming continues on Nimbus, whose estate is completed and co-owned by Elys and Clarissa. Somnus and thirteen fortified Mnemosyne computing worlds remain unaffected.
