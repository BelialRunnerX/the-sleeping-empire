# Turn 000105 — The Diplomat's Two-Hundred-Year Secret

5 Deepfall, 13,627 ICE, ~21:14, gardens of the private Nimbus mountain estate. After a pleasant four-person dinner, Lucien and Chief Diplomat Bel are walking in the garden. Player Elys approaches with Clarissa and says verbatim, 'So Bel Lucien knows the truth now.' Bel checks which truth, and learns Lucien now knows BOTH that friend Hazael Wheatley is Emperor Elysomnion and that Elys granted Lucien immortality by a gift-box pastry two centuries earlier. Lucien discovers from the conversation that Bel and Clarissa knew about his condition for those two centuries, as did Aurelia and the limited inner circle. Bel admits she kept the secret under Elys's sovereign confidentiality order even though she objected at the time. She refuses to excuse or minimize the concealment, and cannot honestly promise she would have told Lucien on her own. Lucien is hurt and disoriented but not hostile; he values his friendship with Elys and budding rapport with Bel, and says he wants honesty going forward, not endless hidden facts about his own life. Bel and Lucien are still getting to know each other; no romance is assumed. Clarissa is mildly exasperated at Elys bringing the issue into a promising dinner. Lucien asks Elys directly whether they can be honest with each other in the future. Await player's exact reply. All other canon unchanged: Somnus is galaxy's dominant infrastructure monopoly owned privately by Elys; Lucien still independently operates his bakery on Epsilon 7, immortal with the same kind of immortality as Aurelia, Bel, Clarissa, Veronica and Lilith. Elys stated Lucien may choose mortality eventually. Use numbered plain-text choices rather than interactive cards.
