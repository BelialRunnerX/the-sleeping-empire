# Turn 000413 — Time to Reply

**Arc:** The Genosis Arc.  
**Location:** Inside the League Senate chamber, Greyhaven, Genosis.  
**Player action:** Crow excludes sickness from the condition and confirms a government has time to hear it and reconsider before actual withdrawal is deemed a declaration of war.  
**Record:** Exact played voice response.

## Played scene

“These conditions have nothing to do with whether you’re sick,” you tell Tessan’s senator. Then you turn to Nareth’s delegate. “Your government will be given time to hear the condition and change its mind. If you withdraw, yes, it’s a declaration of war. I advise you don’t do that.”

Tessan’s senator nods once. “That answers my question. Put it beside the first clause, please. I don’t want the two reaching home on different sheets.”

Nareth’s delegate keeps both hands on her credentials. “I’ll send your words to my assembly. I can tell them they have time. I can’t tell them how much.”

Ordel’s senator pulls the dispatch book towards him. “If Nareth gets the letter on market day, half its assembly will be out in the districts. Give them time to meet after they receive it.”

Aurelia looks at the clerk. “Record the time allowed for a reply once the Emperor specifies it. My office will need the same answer Nareth gets.”

The chair asks for the two clarifications to be copied with the original clauses. Then she looks at you. “How long does Nareth have after its assembly receives the notice?”
