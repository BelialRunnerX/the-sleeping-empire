# Turn 000027 — The Weight of a Recommendation

Player: I call Aurelia back, and I let her know that the emperor strongly advises not engaging in an administrative review.

Moments after her 87-page briefing, Elys calls Regent Aurelia back and formally says the Emperor strongly advises against conducting an administrative review of Somnus. Aurelia asks whether this is personal advice from the Emperor, an actual sovereign directive, or a petition by Hazael Wheatley as private shareholder. She notes possible conflict of interest and explains that the proposed examination would check boundaries between Somnus private intelligence, defense research, armed assets, independent regulators, and public responsibilities rather than seize assets or obstruct lawful business. She offers to narrow the review and safeguard private research and finances if that is Elys's concern. She respectfully maintains that the government has duties to the public and asks Elys whether he is actually ordering the review stopped or merely recommending it. Clarissa and Sentinel observe the exchange. No cancellation or sovereign order has yet occurred; Aurelia awaits clarification.
