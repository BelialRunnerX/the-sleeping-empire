### A Strong Argument for Restraint

📍 **Lumenfall Free Adventurers' Guild, Veyrath** · **9 Deepfall, 13,627 ICE · Late afternoon** · Physical aptitude hall

You take the first position beneath a lattice of steel beams and cyan-lit measurement rails.

“Elys Somion,” Garrick says. “Physical aptitude: strength, acceleration, reaction, coordination, resilience, and controlled exertion. No magic; no modification of the apparatus; no exceeding rated loads.”

The floor beneath you consists of force plates, with a harness mounted to a calibrated resistance carriage. At its far end, a surprisingly enormous red button bears the words **MANUAL ABORT**.

Celeste notices it immediately. “Oh, that's optimistic.”

Garrick doesn't look up. “The button interrupts the apparatus, not the applicant.”

“Even more optimistic.”

**Strength.** You take the handle of a hydraulic resistance rig. Garrick begins at an ordinary applicant's working load and increases resistance in certified increments. Your hand does not tremble. At the maximum permitted load, the carriage moves exactly the prescribed distance—no farther. The machine's limit alarm signals completion rather than failure.

“Upper certification band,” the technician reports.

“Record it as such,” Garrick says. “Not his actual maximum.”

**Acceleration and reaction.** You cross a short instrumented lane without producing a damaging shockwave or striking its safety barrier. On the reaction wall, lights ignite in randomized sequences. You contact the indicated targets with the lightest force that still triggers their sensors, then come to a complete stop inside the required zone. The computer finds no errors above its resolution.

**Coordination.** A rotating balance platform shifts beneath your feet as articulated arms move through unpredictable but clearly marked exclusion zones. Your motions are economical; you do not need to make a spectacle of avoiding them.

**Resilience.** Rather than subjecting anyone to damaging impacts, Garrick uses a cushioned, bounded-pressure harness with redundant cutoffs. You withstand the full approved profile without injury or detectable impairment. The apparatus reaches its own certified ceiling before you show any visible strain.

**Controlled exertion.** The final task requires you to keep a fragile glass test cylinder between two gripping surfaces while simultaneously holding a weighted carriage steady. Apply too little pressure, and the cylinder slips. Too much, and it breaks. You maintain both conditions through rapidly changing commands until the countdown expires. The glass remains intact.

Silence follows.

Then the machine displays **PHYSICAL APTITUDE — 100/100 (CERTIFIED RANGE)**.

Garrick signs the result. “Five areas, no mistakes, no damage, no breach of a single equipment limit. Your actual physical ceiling remains unknown. That's acceptable.”

Celeste sighs theatrically. “How terribly disappointing. He's defeated another testing facility by treating its operating manual as sacred scripture.”

Clarissa watches the glass cylinder being removed. “He could have crushed that by accident.”

“Yes,” Garrick says. “Which is why we test *control*.”

The examiner turns his attention to the next candidate.

“**Celeste Somnion. Physical assessment.**”

Celeste rises with a smile that suggests the laboratory's insurance policy has not known peace in years.
