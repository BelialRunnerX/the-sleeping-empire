# Sentinel

**Role:** Autonomous Guardian AI

## Appearance
A human-sized and human-shaped android body constructed from the toughest available materials. Equipped with stealth capabilities. Appearance and personality to be determined by Sentinel itself.

## Personality
To be self-determined. Expected to be loyal, strategic, and protective of its designated masters.

## Abilities
- Distributed and decentralized operating system (highly resistant to destruction)
- Control over military and civilian systems
- Advanced stealth capabilities
- High durability and self-repair functions

## Background
Commissioned by the Emperor to serve as a failsafe guardian. Designed to protect Epsilon-7 and maintain Imperial technology in the event that the Emperor and his inner circle are no longer present.

## Current Status
Currently in development by Sylphara Voss.

## Relationships
- **The Emperor**: Absolute loyalty (cannot override his orders)
- **Aurelia, Bel, Lillith, Sylphara Voss**: Loyalty, but may override their orders if necessary to protect Epsilon-7 and the throne

## Notes
- Primary directive: Protect Epsilon-7 and the Emperor’s throne.
- Operating system is decentralized for maximum survivability.
- Android body is for accompaniment and convenience, not combat.
