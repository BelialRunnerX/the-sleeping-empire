# Imperial Religion

## Overview
Following the Emperor’s return, an Imperial system of religion was introduced to replace existing faiths across member states.

## Purpose
- Unify the Empire under a common belief system centered on the Emperor.
- Promote loyalty and stability through shared spiritual identity.
- Provide a sense of purpose and continuity across diverse cultures.

## Core Tenets
- The Emperor as the ultimate authority and protector of the galaxy.
- The unity of all Imperial citizens under a single divine order.
- The importance of loyalty, discipline, and service to the Empire.

## History
- Introduced shortly after the Emperor’s return.
- Implemented across many reintegrated systems.
- Faced resistance in some regions and eventually collapsed in others.

## Current Status
The Imperial religion was implemented but later faced significant resistance and eventual collapse in many regions. Remnants may still exist in some systems, though it is no longer a central institution of the Empire.
