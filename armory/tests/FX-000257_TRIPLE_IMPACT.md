## Test 01: Aletheia throw into armor embedded in asteroid
- Elys equips Regalia Aegis and Forge Core, travels into vacuum, removes armor and sets it inside an asteroid, parks pack clear of strike.
- Throws sentient Aletheia at armor. Asteroid shattered and ejected in hot fragments, armor remained structurally intact and pressure-capable, with superficial sacrificial plating loss and core unaffected.
- Regalia Aegis durability **100 -> 74 (immediate) -> 100 (fully restored)**. The armor **automatically draws in and transmutes surrounding asteroid wreckage and available dust/minerals into replacement alloys, armor plating and repaired structures**; the Forge Core is not needed. No residual scarring after repair. Matter is consumed from the surrounding environment; material/energy amounts not quantified. Elys re-equips the pristine armor. Weapon returned to right-side autonomous follow state. Kinetic test is **not** full apotheocide maximum-discharge proof.

## Test 02: Aletheia throw against one of Elys's private Andromedan-pattern warships
- Automated ship scan established no crew onboard. Far from inhabited routes. Reactor fail-safe shutdown.
- Shield envelope catastrophically cracked, central structural spine severed, vessel split into major hull sections and mission-killed. **One of Elys's 100 personal Andromedan-pattern armed ships lost, 99 remaining from original group (status unverified)**. No crew casualties or salvage yet. Clarissa's sovereign navy not affected.
- Aletheia remains fully operative and self-recalls to right of Emperor.

## Test 03: Aletheia throw at barren rocky planet
- Sector survey showed no biosignatures/habitats/occupied craft in mapped impact area, but precise planetary title is **UNKNOWN**.
- Initial strike ruptures the surface outward, then the whole world undergoes **catastrophic inward collapse/implosion**, compressing from a planetary sphere to a small emerald-white point which **extinguishes; the planet disappears entirely**. **No surviving planet or persistent impact crater remains.** No new stable black hole or replacement object is presumed; long-term gravitational effects remain unmeasured. Aletheia returns intact; no verified casualties.
- The **complete destruction/disappearance of the barren planet** is now the authoritative outcome, not merely an impact scar. No absolute god-slaying of all possible beings or universal armor invulnerability is established.
