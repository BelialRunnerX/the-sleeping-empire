# ITM-000006 — The Horizon Needle

**Owner:** Elys Somion · **Unique persistent item:** ITM-000006 · **Rarity:** Rare ancient spatial navigation instrument · **Source:** Turn 000286. 
**Acquisition:** Voluntarily transferred by the Twelfth Saint under an authenticated emergency custodian succession order after 12 young Leviathans were released. No theft, no second copy, not a second Last Witness.
**Physical custody:** UNEQUIPPED, safely stored within Regalia Forge Core ITM-000003 on Elys.
**Appearance:** Old-fashioned sextant-like apparatus of seven thin dark-silver arcs surrounding a central crystal with tiny shifting stars.
**Functions:** Provides an independent locally calibrated orientation/reference for twisted or compressed spatial coordinate systems; helps detect discrepancies in a moving star chart and retain reference to confirmed paths; can work with ITM-000005 Sixfold Harmonic Resonator and armor-worn ITM-000004 Last Witness, but remains a distinct scientific instrument.
**Limits:** Not a teleportation device, independent route fabricator, universal tracking system, consciousness, weapon, or godhood amplifier; numeric sensitivity/range/energy and durability unknown. No automatic authority over Twelfth Silence or First Nest.
**History:** [Turn 000286](../../saves/turns/000286.md), [Turn 000287](../../saves/turns/000287.md).
