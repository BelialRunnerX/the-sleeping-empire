## Appearance
A hand-sized **twelve-pointed black-crystal star**, bound in threads of luminous gold, with a constant white flame at its center. Thousands of microscopic gold inscriptions orbit the flame, each a name/testimony; the twelve points separate into floating blades during authorized activation. Inert/retracted form feels warmly weighted and hums like a distant bell. Visual testimony is not a captive live consciousness.

## Actual functions, bounded design
- **Anchor of Identity:** Can create protective continuity references for specifically authorized wearer/targets against mnemonic identity manipulation, ontology/record erasure, and memetic contamination, when *equipped or deliberately deployed*. Effectiveness against arbitrary absolute powers not verified; does NOT passively protect all of existence while stored.
- **Testament Seal:** Writes limited tamper-evident, provenance-authenticated testimony and cross-checks it against its internal fixed witness architecture. Not infallible truth-detection and not unlimited infinite storage.
- **Sepulcher Waymark:** Retains **verified route/coordinate reference to the now-stable Sepulcher main approach**; no unlimited teleportation, interstellar instant passage, grant of entry into every sealed chamber or overriding local authorities.
- **Custodian's Accord:** Cryptographic/ritual recognition by surviving golden-eyed Heart, allowing authenticated *requests* for future read access in accordance with its consent and governance. Does NOT override Heart autonomy or transfer the full archive.
- **Failure independence:** It was designed to preserve one protected true witness when other archives failed. **Not a resurrection artifact**, not an automatic reversal of erased civilizations, not a weapon which destroys sentient beings by itself.

## Custody/ethical considerations
The Heart offered the relic voluntarily after Elys removed the archive predator without destroying the Heart/records; the vault automatically opened upon user choice to retrieve. No trap, unwilling sentient object or unobserved prisoner was inside. The **larger ancient archive remains in the Heart's custody**. The user's new personality direction for Aletheia is a **separate sentient character adjustment**, not a property of this relic.
