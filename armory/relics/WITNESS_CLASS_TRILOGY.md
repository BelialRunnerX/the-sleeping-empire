# The Witness-Class Trilogy — Builder Legacy

**First confirmed description:** [Turn 000272](../../saves/turns/000272.md)
**Source intelligence:** The surviving **Heart of the Sepulcher** voluntarily shares surviving builder histories with Elys, in the opened legacy vault.
**Status:** Historical lore. **NOT new owned or physically encountered inventory items** except already acquired `ITM-000004`.
**Builder origin:** Nine lost civilizations collaboratively designed the Sepulcher and multiple defensive measures against the Eater of Names. The **nine vault doors depict nine contributing builder civilizations**. Three completed *distinct* Witness-class instruments; **not nine copies**.

## The First Witness / The Foundation
Massive, apparently **stationary** instrument built to preserve evidence of **material/astronomical events** even if mainstream archives and observers forgot them. Last historically reported at **Observatory of Still Names**, itself unlocated on current star charts. The Heart **lost contact**. No confirmed functional unit, salvageable artifact, coordinates, precise power, surviving facility or present owner. **Not Elys-owned**. A future investigation could examine archival telemetry.

## The Second Witness / The Remembrance
**Mobile sanctuary** designed to preserve names, memories, bonds and testimony of living people when whole civilizations were erased. According to the Heart's last reliable report, it departed aboard an **unidentified refugee vessel** as the Eater of Names advanced. Destination, manifest, occupant/sentience status, current location and survival are **UNKNOWN**. Cannot assert every passenger is dead, alive, recoverable or still conscious. **Not Elys-owned**, no extra item ID minted.

## The Last Witness / The Continuity
Unique, portable twelve-point black-gold star relic with white flame. Functions to preserve **continuity of identity, relationships, testimony and event records**, complementing the two other designs' objectives. This is exactly the previously acquired **`ITM-000004 The Last Witness`**, retrieved from Sepulcher inner builders' vault in turn 000269, appraised in 000270, physically integrated in **Regalia Aegis `ITM-000002` relic_core** in 000271 and protected with **Rev II mount** in 000272. Sole verified owned Witness relic. Remains distinct, non-sentient, removable and exclusively owned by Elys.

"Last" means last still available to the Heart and **only confirmed intact** at this time, NOT proof that both other Witnesses perished. The Heart's knowledge is limited and cannot guarantee their existence, condition, or current location. They are **historical leads** not automatic repeat treasure, quest completion, teleport targets or new recipes. Their original technological design details, blueprint and numerics remain incomplete.

**Key continuity warning:** An attempted future recovery of First or Second does not authorize autonomous hostility or imply they are necessarily conscious. Any associated civilization/refugees are independent beings, not items to seize by default.
