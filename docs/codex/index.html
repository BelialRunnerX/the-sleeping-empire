Your first outing

Gather stone, logs and accessible ores. Make a furnace, turn ore into ingots, then work toward powered production. Keep room in your pack for the trip home.

Build around a purpose

A small workshop supports your next expedition. Add machines as you need their outputs, then connect power, storage and conveyors rather than collecting idle equipment.

Take a moment before leaving

Check health, suit air and inventory space. Mark a home waypoint for navigation and a separate camp if you want a possible return point after death.
