Use abilities and supports

How to play · Reviewed 19 November 2026

Clear nearby enemies with a radial pulse, or change how your next pulse attack behaves.

Controls and recipes describe the documented native version; check your build’s controls if they differ.

On this page

  2. Choose a support
  3. Watch your cooldown
  4. If a cast stops early
  5. Sources

Cast a radial pulse

Press X. The base cast has a 7-metre radius, 7-second cooldown and 1.8 damage multiplier. It selects at most the nearest 12 living targets, then checks line of sight. A wall can block a selected target without letting a farther one take its place.

Choose a support

In the bag, N selects None, Swift or Cascade for radial pulse. Cascade expands the maximum radius to 10.15 metres. Shift+N selects pulse support: Pierce offers up to three distinct targets at 85% per-hit damage.

Watch your cooldown

An accepted cast spends its cooldown even if it hits nothing. Haste uses cooldown / (1 + haste). Changing gear or support affects the next cast, not a timer already running. These physical abilities do not cost mana or grant invulnerability.

If a cast stops early

Earlier confirmed hits can remain when a later hit fails. Do not expect every target to be hit together or the whole cast to retry. If play stops because a save outcome is uncertain, reload before continuing.
