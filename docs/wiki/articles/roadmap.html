Game direction

Elysium’s direction combines voxel exploration, action-RPG combat and loot, industry and an optional living-colony layer in a sci-fi solarpunk setting. A design resemblance to another game does not mean its systems are already reproduced here.

Not a delivery claim

Full canonical spells and mana, a complete armor/accessory-slot layout, trading and a broader economy, transport between planets, comprehensive NPC schedules and cross-network multiplayer must not be inferred from the narrower implemented mechanics documented here.

Source precedence

Specific implementation notes define what a snapshot actually does. Donor rules explain provenance, and roadmap documents describe intent. When they differ, this wiki labels the adaptation instead of quietly treating all three as the same system.

Browser reference

The earlier browser adaptation is preserved separately. Its Java/NeoForge source ports, browser-only progression, controls and local saves are not interchangeable with native C++ behavior. It is intentionally excluded from this wiki’s default navigation and search corpus.
