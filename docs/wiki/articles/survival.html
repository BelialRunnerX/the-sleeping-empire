Health and survival

Native development snapshot · Reviewed 18 November 2026

Health capacity, environmental protection and breathable rooms are separate mechanics.

Version matters. This is an implementation reference, not a promise that every feature is in your installed build. Provisional adaptations and limitations are stated explicitly. Controls describe the documented native snapshot and may differ in the Windows GUI handoff.

Equipped life

Native maximum health is 100 + the sum of equipped canonical life, with one final binary32 conversion. Increasing capacity does not heal or revive; lowering capacity clamps current health downward. Air and the existing regeneration delay are preserved during equipment changes.

Environmental suit

The native factory can manufacture the existing sealed helmet, hazard suit and sealed leggings. These are ordinary environmental gear, not unique-loot instances. A newly equipped complete suit does not receive free air; breathable conditions refill it under existing rules.

Repair before replacement

Replacing a damaged worn piece is refused until it is repaired. This prevents turning damaged equipment into a pristine type-only inventory item. Empty-slot and valid pristine replacements retain their existing behavior.

Extreme values

Health remains binary32. Very large canonical values lose fine damage/regeneration precision; this is documented rather than hidden behind an arbitrary saturation cap. Older binaries that assume maximum health 100 are not safe downgrade targets for high-health saves.
