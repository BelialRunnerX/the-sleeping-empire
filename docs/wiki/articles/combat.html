Pulse, bash and guard

The native pulse is a provisional physical basic weapon, not a mana-powered Astral lance. Documented Adventure controls are held LMB for pulses, MMB for bash, and held RMB for directional guard. Guard suppresses new shots and bash; already-launched pulses can still finish.

Projectile behavior

Base pulse speed is 24 metres per simulated second, maximum travel is 24 metres and base fire interval is 0.20 seconds. Swept collision checks edited voxel geometry, including thin walls and carved openings. Launch damage is captured; changing equipment or gaining a level does not rewrite a shot in flight.

Level contribution

Native physical damage is round_once((12 + 3 × (level − 1) + equippedPower) × (1 + damage + attackDamage)). The donor level term is retained, but native base 12 remains. Each new attack reads current usable progression; no accumulating saved buff is added.

Bounds and availability

A valid level-and-gear combination may exceed the native damage bound. Attacks become unavailable rather than silently clamping or corrupting the world. Equipment management and valid defense remain accessible. Unknown persistence outcomes are different: those stop play and require reload.
