Loot and equipment

Native development snapshot · Reviewed 18 November 2026

Canonical unique loot lives in the ordinary pack. Equipped selections reference those same instances rather than a second bag.

Version matters. This is an implementation reference, not a promise that every feature is in your installed build. Provisional adaptations and limitations are stated explicitly. Controls describe the documented native snapshot and may differ in the Windows GUI handoff.

Rewards

Eligible future native kills persist a regular 30% loot roll alongside death and XP. P resolves a pending reward into the pack or records a no-drop result. This does not imply a persistent physical corpse. A full pack leaves the exact reward pending without rerolling.

Ownership

Unique gear cannot stack, trade or move into machines. The native pack has 36 slots. The registry retains at most 1,000 lifetime instances, including retired tombstones, and 4,096 claims. These are finite limits, not an infinite stash.

Equip and discard

R toggles the selected unique item’s equipped state. The documented bag Delete confirmation requires two distinct presses within five seconds. Discard gives no salvage reward, removes the sole pack reference and keeps immutable claim/item history to prevent replay resurrection.

Active versus stored affixes

The item preserves its exact generated name, base and affixes. Only supported native effects are active; stored data is not proof that every canonical mechanic is implemented. Equipped life, physical combat projection and haste have separate scoped implementations.
