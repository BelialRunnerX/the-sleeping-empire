Setting

Somnus and its branches

Somnus is a private commercial group overseen by Lethe. Wheatley Storage & Shipping handles logistics; SMITE expands to Space Mining and Industrial Technology Expeditions; Apophatic Technologies develops advanced industrial processes. Their names describe distinct roles, not interchangeable labels for all industry.

Setting

Independent partners matter

Bellweather is an independent cooperative with a nonexclusive relationship to WSS, not a subsidiary. Imperial public institutions and private Somnus holdings remain separate: ownership, contracts and cooperation are different kinds of relationship.

Design reference

From ore to usefulness

Mining, crafting, storage and automation form a chain of practical work. A component matters because it helps make, carry or maintain something; a workshop turns raw material into a reason to undertake the next journey.
