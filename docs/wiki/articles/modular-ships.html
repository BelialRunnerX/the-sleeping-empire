Modular ships and expedition preparation

Design reference · Reviewed 19 November 2026

The ship design emphasizes expedition preparation, cargo and route choice rather than promising a completed dogfighting simulation.

Version matters. Design reference or planned direction, not a delivered feature promise. Read the section labels for implementation status.

Design reference

Propulsion and range

The reference lists Warp Drive I–IV, Pulse Engine, Atmospheric Thrusters and Vector Jets. It describes discrete jump-range tiers and planning limits from fuel, cargo and handling. These are design entries, not selectable native ship upgrades today.

Design reference

Utility and protection

Fuel Tanks, Cargo Racks, Survey Scanners, Deep Scanners and Atmosphere Samplers serve exploration. Hull Plating, Thermal Shields, Radiation Baffles, Autopilot, a Navigation Core and Emergency Beacon support protection and travel. Autopilot is framed as assistance, not permission to ignore every hazard.

Design reference

Industrial modules

Proposed modules include Docking Collars, Drone Bays, Habitation Pods, a Small Refinery and Smuggler Hold. The reference describes inefficient field processing and a cargo-efficiency tradeoff for reduced inspection exposure. These are planned interactions, not a current smuggling economy.

Planned

Orbit and surface

Continuous orbit-to-surface travel without a loading screen is a design goal. It has not been established by the current local world, home marker, camp recovery or movement fixtures.
