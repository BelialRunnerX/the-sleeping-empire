Shelters and atmosphere

Native development snapshot · Reviewed 18 November 2026

An Atmosphere Unit works only under its power, room and filter-charge conditions. A visible enclosure is not by itself proof of breathability.

Version matters. This is an implementation reference, not a promise that every feature is in your installed build. Provisional adaptations and limitations are stated explicitly. Controls describe the documented native snapshot and may differ in the Windows GUI handoff.

Filter consumption

Effective filtering duration is the smaller of valid tick duration and remaining filter seconds. Pressure and oxygen are credited only for time actually paid by charge. A nearly empty filter no longer grants an entire tick of unpaid operation.

Cartridge timing

At most one cartridge loads at the start of a tick. When partial charge expires, the rest of that tick is idle; another cartridge may load next tick. Disabled/unpowered hold and breach venting behavior are unchanged.

Room-model limitation

The current classification retains a cell-centre room model. It is not proof of finest-subvoxel airtightness. Programmatic portal test fixtures are not evidence that a full player-sized doorway loop is delivered in this snapshot.

Safety and saves

A camp position can be geometrically valid yet unbreathable or otherwise dangerous. Ordinary simulation saves record advanced live state; failed saves do not roll the simulation back to its previous oxygen or fuel value.
