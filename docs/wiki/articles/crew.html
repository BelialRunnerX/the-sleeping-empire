Crew work

Native development snapshot · Reviewed 18 November 2026

Crew mining and construction use stable job identities, bounded queues and fresh physical reach checks.

Version matters. This is an implementation reference, not a promise that every feature is in your installed build. Provisional adaptations and limitations are stated explicitly. Controls describe the documented native snapshot and may differ in the Windows GUI handoff.

Area designation

The documented Shift+J action designates a bounded 3×3 plane at the aimed whole voxel. Existing active targets and repeated candidates are suppressed. Queued, skipped and refused counts are distinct; this is not an atomic batch or an immediate resource reward.

Reach and construction

Navigation arrival and authoritative probe/commit use the shared physical reach policy. Upper walls and roofs may be worked from a safe supported position when body clearance, distance and line of sight allow it. Worker movement, a new obstruction or a changed target can invalidate a pending action.

Finite history

The full work-record capacity is 2,048. Explicit maintenance can archive completed/cancelled details into a separate finite ledger of up to 8,192 identities. Queued/running jobs and reservations are not discarded to make room.

Maintenance warning

Archival permanently removes full terminal specification/target detail while retaining identity and outcome. The first committed archive writes work format v2, which older binaries cannot load. This needs explicit confirmation, not an automatic background purge.
