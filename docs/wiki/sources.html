Design reference

Scope of this edition

The mechanics summaries were reviewed on 18 November 2026 against native development documentation. These are editorial summaries, not a published source-code audit, verified Windows release manifest or promise of availability in an installed build. Internal development notes are not republished here.

Design reference

Public reference material

The public Sleeping Empire repository is the existing website source. Its browser adaptation has a public port audit, linked below. That audit describes browser rules and their lineage, not proof of native feature parity. Public reference links do not independently substantiate every native detail in this edition.

Design reference

Editorial labels

Native snapshot means reviewed native development behavior. Native adaptation means provisional or narrowed behavior. Planned means intent, not delivered functionality. Isolated prototypes and test-only setup must not be presented as player-accessible features.

Design reference

Updating this reference

Reconcile each summary against the current implementation before changing its status. Keep controls, compatibility limits and provisional balancing explicit. Publish only curated mechanics material, not raw internal engineering records.

Article provenance

Native article details below are editorial summaries of development documentation, not independently reproducible public-source claims.

  • Elysium: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: A mechanics reference for Elysium, a sci-fi solarpunk voxel action RPG with survival, crafting, automation and colony systems.
  • Mining and tools: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Mining uses tool suitability, material requirements and exact voxel volume. The permanent iron-head upgrade improves the existing pick without introducing a second tool inventory.
  • Combat and progression: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Native basic attacks share one durable encounter owner for damage, death, XP and reward proofs.
  • Abilities and supports: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Radial pulse and persistent support choices are bounded native adaptations, not the complete canonical spell system.
  • Loot and equipment: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Canonical unique loot lives in the ordinary pack. Equipped selections reference those same instances rather than a second bag.
  • Health and survival: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Health capacity, environmental protection and breathable rooms are separate mechanics.
  • Crafting and industry: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Field assembly bootstraps the native factory chain. Processing then depends on machines, fuel, power and reserved inputs.
  • Power and logistics: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Conveyors, storage, loaders and batteries connect existing machine owners. They do not create a separate inventory authority.
  • Shelters and atmosphere: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: An Atmosphere Unit works only under its power, room and filter-charge conditions. A visible enclosure is not by itself proof of breathability.
  • Crew work: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Crew mining and construction use stable job identities, bounded queues and fresh physical reach checks.
  • Exploration and recovery: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: The home waypoint guides travel; the separate camp anchor can influence death recovery. Neither is a general teleport system.
  • Saving and compatibility: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Native saves distinguish committed transactions, known failure and uncertain outcomes. This distinction is part of the mechanics, not just an implementation detail.
  • Design goals and open work: Planned; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Design intent is kept separate from the implemented native snapshot.
  • Combat: choosing an action: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: The native combat kit combines a physical pulse, close bash, directional guard, a horizontal dash and a radial attack.
  • Basic pulse and projectile timing: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: The pulse is the repeatable ranged foundation of the native combat slice.
  • Pierce: trading damage for targets: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Pierce is a saved support for the basic pulse, independent of the radial support selection.
  • Radial pulse and support choices: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Radial pulse trades a longer cooldown for a burst against nearby visible enemies.
  • Dash, guard and avoiding damage: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Movement and directional defense complement armor; they do not replace terrain awareness.
  • Melee and ranged hostile behavior: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Current native enemies include close attackers and a provisional ranged roaming role.
  • Experience and level-based power: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Native eligible hostile kills award durable experience and increase future attack power as levels rise.
  • Loot rolls, pending rewards and inventory space: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Unique rewards are fixed by the eligible kill, then claimed into the ordinary pack.
  • Affixes: active effects and stored rolls: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: An item can preserve an affix without the current combat slice applying its effect. Check the active effect indicator rather than assuming every label works.
  • Armor, haste and maximum health: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: These three active defensive and timing effects solve different problems.
  • Building around the current combat kit: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: These are rule-based starting points for experimentation, not locked classes or promised optimal builds.
  • Pack management and unique-item retirement: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Ordinary resources and unique gear share a 36-slot pack but follow different rules.
  • Environmental suits and protective layers: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Environmental equipment is separate from unique combat loot.
  • Breathing, hazards and recovery: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: A safe expedition depends on the environment, suit condition, carried air and health, not one combined defense score.
  • Home guidance versus a recovery camp: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Home and camp are intentionally separate: one guides travel, the other can select a recovery position.
  • Mining whole blocks and subvoxels: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Fine terrain edits preserve material quantities rather than treating every small chip as a whole block.
  • Iron-head pick upgrade: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: A small permanent upgrade improves mining speed without opening a new material tier.
  • From stone and logs to a powered workshop: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: The current starter route uses a small hand-assembly catalogue to reach station-based industry.
  • Workshop recipes, queues and blocked production: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: A recipe preview is a plan; production requires paid materials, usable capacity and its station conditions.
  • Machine families and production roles: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Stations turn gathered resources into components that support tools, survival and automation.
  • Provisional machine kit reference: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: These powered Fabricator recipes describe the native milestone, not universal design-document costs.
  • Manufacturing a renewable under-suit: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: The Fabricator can replenish the ordinary equipment used for environmental survival.
  • Power demand, priorities and brownouts: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Power is a rate; batteries hold energy. A running network must balance both.
  • Batteries: charging, transferring and recovering: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Recovery must preserve energy instead of hiding a charged battery inside an ordinary empty kit.
  • Network Storage, conveyors and sorters: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Network access and belt routes are different ways to move real stored resources.
  • Cargo Loaders and conserving delivery: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: A Cargo Loader bridges belt routing and powered same-network storage.
  • Automated extraction and output belts: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: An Extractor is deliberately more conservative than a player carving mixed microgeometry.
  • Rooms, filters and breathable shelter: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: A powered Atmosphere Unit needs a detected room and paid filter time to change its atmosphere.
  • Crew mining and construction designations: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Designations ask citizens to perform work; they do not instantly alter the terrain.
  • Completed work and history maintenance: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Finished and cancelled jobs retain history until you deliberately archive details.
  • Life skills, professions and practice: Design reference; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: The reference envisions practical professions that connect character development with settlement work.
  • Sockets, reforging and ascension: Design reference; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Advanced item customization belongs to the wider design and donor systems, not the current native equipment loop.
  • From work crews to a settlement society: Design reference; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: The long-term colony design goes beyond a set of workers completing mining orders.
  • Economy, trade routes and social consequences: Design reference; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: The reference connects production with commerce, institutions and political attention.
  • Planets, biomes and material identity: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: World variety is meant to change survival and construction decisions, not just terrain color.
  • Flora, fauna and contextual resources: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: The broader direction calls for ecosystems with useful differences, not recolored decoration.
  • Travel, outposts and returning to a world: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: The intended travel system connects durable sites rather than generating unrelated disposable minigames.
  • Multiplayer and network expectations: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Local persistence, Network Storage and a menu connected to game data do not by themselves constitute multiplayer.
  • Saving, version compatibility and safe recovery: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Persistent progress is valuable only when the game can interpret it consistently.
  • How to read mechanics status: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: This guide separates implemented native behavior from the larger design so players can plan without mistaking ambition for a release promise.
  • Ground vehicles: rovers, crawlers and mining rigs: Design reference; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Ground vehicles are part of the design reference, not drivable features in the current documented native snapshot.
  • Modular ships and expedition preparation: Design reference; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: The ship design emphasizes expedition preparation, cargo and route choice rather than promising a completed dogfighting simulation.
  • Early vehicles: amphibious rover and starter spacecraft: Planned; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: The first vehicle direction pairs one fixed starter rover with one fixed starter spacecraft, before later modular construction.
  • Profession catalogue: reference names: Design reference; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: The archived progression catalogue gives concrete names to eleven practical disciplines. It is a reference catalogue, not a list of fully active native skill bars.
  • Passive trees and named ability reference: Design reference; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: The archived browser catalogue contains a broader build vocabulary than the current native attack kit.
  • A universe beyond the next ridge: Setting; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: A setting introduction adapted from the Sleeping Empire archive. These histories frame Elysium; they do not promise playable factions or destinations.
  • Worlds and places: Setting; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: A small geographic reading guide to the inherited setting, not a destination list or travel menu.
  • Work, trade and industry: Setting; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: The archive’s commercial institutions give production a social context without turning every workshop into an imperial possession.
  • Technology with a living purpose: Setting; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Elegant engineering and enduring infrastructure supply the setting foundation; Elysium gives that foundation its solarpunk visual treatment.
  • Equipment with identity: Setting; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: The source armory treats equipment as named, persistent objects. Elysium adapts that idea without turning every lore capability into a working mechanic.
  • Setting glossary: Setting; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: A compact vocabulary for reading Elysium’s narrative references alongside the mechanics wiki.
