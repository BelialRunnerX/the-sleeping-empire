Scope of this edition

The mechanics summaries were reviewed on 18 November 2026 against native development documentation. These are editorial summaries, not a published source-code audit, verified Windows release manifest or promise of availability in an installed build. Internal development notes are not republished here.

Public reference material

The public Sleeping Empire repository is the existing website source. Its browser adaptation has a public port audit, linked below. That audit describes browser rules and their lineage, not proof of native feature parity. Public reference links do not independently substantiate every native detail in this edition.

Editorial labels

Native snapshot means reviewed native development behavior. Native adaptation means provisional or narrowed behavior. Planned means intent, not delivered functionality. Isolated prototypes and test-only setup must not be presented as player-accessible features.

Updating this reference

Reconcile each summary against the current implementation before changing its status. Keep controls, compatibility limits and provisional balancing explicit. Publish only curated mechanics material, not raw internal engineering records.

Article provenance

Native article details below are editorial summaries of development documentation, not independently reproducible public-source claims.

  • Elysium: Native development summary; not verified released-build availability. Scope: A mechanics reference for Elysium, a sci-fi solarpunk voxel action RPG with survival, crafting, automation and colony systems.
  • Mining and tools: Native development summary; not verified released-build availability. Scope: Mining uses tool suitability, material requirements and exact voxel volume. The permanent iron-head upgrade improves the existing pick without introducing a second tool inventory.
  • Combat and progression: Native development summary; not verified released-build availability. Scope: Native basic attacks share one durable encounter owner for damage, death, XP and reward proofs.
  • Abilities and supports: Native development summary; not verified released-build availability. Scope: Radial pulse and persistent support choices are bounded native adaptations, not the complete canonical spell system.
  • Loot and equipment: Native development summary; not verified released-build availability. Scope: Canonical unique loot lives in the ordinary pack. Equipped selections reference those same instances rather than a second bag.
  • Health and survival: Native development summary; not verified released-build availability. Scope: Health capacity, environmental protection and breathable rooms are separate mechanics.
  • Crafting and industry: Native development summary; not verified released-build availability. Scope: Field assembly bootstraps the native factory chain. Processing then depends on machines, fuel, power and reserved inputs.
  • Power and logistics: Native development summary; not verified released-build availability. Scope: Conveyors, storage, loaders and batteries connect existing machine owners. They do not create a separate inventory authority.
  • Shelters and atmosphere: Native development summary; not verified released-build availability. Scope: An Atmosphere Unit works only under its power, room and filter-charge conditions. A visible enclosure is not by itself proof of breathability.
  • Crew work: Native development summary; not verified released-build availability. Scope: Crew mining and construction use stable job identities, bounded queues and fresh physical reach checks.
  • Exploration and recovery: Native development summary; not verified released-build availability. Scope: The home waypoint guides travel; the separate camp anchor can influence death recovery. Neither is a general teleport system.
  • Saving and compatibility: Native development summary; not verified released-build availability. Scope: Native saves distinguish committed transactions, known failure and uncertain outcomes. This distinction is part of the mechanics, not just an implementation detail.
  • Design goals and open work: Design goals; not a delivery claim. Scope: Design intent is kept separate from the implemented native snapshot.
