Reference

Scope of this edition

The mechanics summaries were reviewed on 18 November 2026 against native development documentation. These are editorial summaries, not a published source-code audit, verified Windows release manifest or promise of availability in an installed build. Internal development notes are not republished here.

Reference

Public reference material

The public Sleeping Empire repository is the existing website source. Its browser adaptation has a public port audit, linked below. That audit describes browser rules and their lineage, not proof of native feature parity. Public reference links do not independently substantiate every native detail in this edition.

Reference

Guide labels

How to play covers the documented native version, not a guarantee for every installed build. Reference explains a design or rule set. Planned previews unavailable features. Setting describes the world and its narrative. Sections with a different status carry their own label.

Reference

Updating this reference

Reconcile each summary against the current implementation before changing its status. Keep controls, compatibility limits and provisional balancing explicit. Publish only curated mechanics material, not raw internal engineering records.

About this guide’s versions

Article provenance

Native article details below are editorial summaries of development documentation, not independently reproducible public-source claims.

  • Start here: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Choose your next goal: gather resources, build a workshop, survive a fight or prepare a safe return.
  • Mining and better tools: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Gather exact amounts of material and upgrade your pick to finish familiar mining jobs faster.
  • Fight and level up: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Use pulses at range, bash up close and face incoming attacks to guard.
  • Use abilities and supports: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Clear nearby enemies with a radial pulse, or change how your next pulse attack behaves.
  • Collect and equip loot: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Claim rewards into your pack, compare their effects and equip the pieces that suit your build.
  • Stay alive: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Manage health, suit condition and air separately. A larger health bar is not a refill.
  • Build your first workshop: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Start with field assembly, then use fuel and power to turn raw materials into better machines.
  • Connect power and storage: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Keep production supplied, move finished goods and store surplus energy.
  • Make a breathable shelter: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Check the Atmosphere Unit, its power supply and filter charge before trusting a room.
  • Give the crew useful work: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Designate a small job area, give workers access and check the result before expanding.
  • Find your way home: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Use a waypoint for navigation and a separate camp for a possible return after death.
  • Save and protect your progress: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Keep a recoverable backup, watch save results and use a compatible build.
  • What lies ahead: Planned; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: A guidepost to Foundations, the next Additions, and the longer-term frontier.
  • How to read mechanics status: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Start with pages marked How to play for usable mechanics. Reference and Planned pages describe wider ideas or future work.
  • Breathing, hazards and recovery: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Before leaving breathable surroundings, check your seal, carried air and health. Combat defense alone will not keep you breathing.
  • Home guidance versus a recovery camp: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Use home for directions and camp for recovery after death. They are separate markers.
  • Mining whole blocks and subvoxels: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Gather only what you remove. Fine carving produces fractions of a block, which can be combined for crafting.
  • Pack management and unique-item retirement: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Your pack has 36 slots. Put an item in a usable hotbar slot before trying an action that uses the held item.
  • From stone and logs to a powered workshop: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Start with a Furnace, smelt your first ingots, then build powered stations for steel and components.
  • Workshop recipes, queues and blocked production: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Supply a station, choose its recipe and queue batches. If work stalls, check inputs, power or fuel, and output space.
  • Iron-head pick upgrade: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Spend three iron ingots to mine faster with the starter pick. This improves speed, not the materials it can break.
  • Machine families and production roles: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Follow component chains to expand your workshop instead of treating each machine as a separate source of free materials.
  • Provisional machine kit reference: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Use a powered Fabricator for these machine kits. Costs are provisional for this snapshot.
  • Manufacturing a renewable under-suit: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Make replacement environmental equipment at a powered Fabricator, then arrange breathable air before setting out.
  • Environmental suits and protective layers: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Build a complete under-suit for a seal, then add outer protection. Environmental equipment is separate from unique combat loot.
  • Power demand, priorities and brownouts: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Keep enough generation and stored energy for your active loads. A connected machine can still be unpowered.
  • Batteries: charging, transferring and recovering: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Empty a battery before packing it away. Move its charge into another battery rather than losing the stored energy.
  • Network Storage, conveyors and sorters: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Use Network Storage to supply nearby industry and belts to connect directed routes.
  • Cargo Loaders and conserving delivery: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Use Cargo Loaders to bridge belts and powered storage on the same network.
  • Automated extraction and output belts: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Use an Extractor for untouched, uniform natural cells. Use player mining for carved or mixed terrain.
  • Rooms, filters and breathable shelter: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: A powered Atmosphere Unit needs a detected room and filter supply. Check the resulting atmosphere before relying on it for breathing.
  • Crew mining and construction designations: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Mark work for citizens, then give them a reachable target and enough material. Designations do not build or mine instantly.
  • Completed work and history maintenance: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Archive completed or cancelled jobs when you need room in work history. Keep a backup: archiving permanently removes their detailed records.
  • Combat: choosing an action: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Switch to Adventure controls for combat. Start with ranged pulses, keep room to move, and use guard or dash when enemies close in.
  • Basic pulse and projectile timing: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Hold LMB in Adventure mode for repeatable ranged fire. Keep a clear line to your target.
  • Dash, guard and avoiding damage: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Use dash to reposition and guard to meet an incoming attack. Neither replaces watching the terrain.
  • Melee and ranged hostile behavior: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Starter encounters use melee attackers. Some roaming enemies fire a delayed bolt that you can dodge after they lock their aim.
  • Radial pulse and support choices: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Press X to strike nearby visible enemies. Wait until targets are in position: even a cast that hits nothing spends its cooldown.
  • Pierce: trading damage for targets: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Choose Pierce for lined-up enemies, or None when you want full damage against one target.
  • Experience and level-based power: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Eligible hostile kills earn XP and raise the power of your next attacks.
  • Loot rolls, pending rewards and inventory space: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Press P to resolve one pending reward. If your pack is full, make space and try again: the reward will not reroll.
  • Affixes: active effects and stored rolls: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Read which effects are active before choosing gear. Some rolled affixes are stored for future systems and give no current benefit.
  • Armor, haste and maximum health: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Choose armor to soften physical hits, haste to act sooner, and life for a larger health buffer.
  • Building around the current combat kit: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Try these combinations as starting points, then adjust for the enemies and terrain. They are not locked classes.
  • Planets, biomes and material identity: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Explore for useful materials and safe places to build. Appearance alone does not tell you what a world feature does.
  • Flora, fauna and contextual resources: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Check interaction feedback before planning food or resource supplies around a plant or animal.
  • Travel, outposts and returning to a world: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Use home guidance and camp recovery to explore locally. Ship travel and lasting interplanetary routes are a separate design track.
  • Saving, version compatibility and safe recovery: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Keep a backup before changing builds, and distinguish a completed action from a completed world save.
  • Multiplayer and network expectations: Native snapshot; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: This guide describes local mechanics. It does not establish separate-network co-op availability.
  • Life skills, professions and practice: Design reference; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Design preview: practical professions would develop through everyday work and connect your character to settlement life.
  • Sockets, reforging and ascension: Design reference; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Design preview: customize an existing item through sockets, reforging and ascension rather than replacing it with an unrelated item.
  • Profession catalogue: reference names: Design reference; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Earlier-version reference: eleven named disciplines give a preview of the profession system’s vocabulary.
  • Passive trees and named ability reference: Design reference; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Earlier-version reference: the browser catalogue offers a preview of broader ability and passive-tree ideas.
  • From work crews to a settlement society: Design reference; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Design preview: grow a settlement with institutions, supplies and social responsibilities beyond individual work orders.
  • Economy, trade routes and social consequences: Design reference; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Design preview: production would feed markets, contracts and trade routes with local consequences.
  • Early vehicles: amphibious rover and starter spacecraft: Planned; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Planned first vehicles: one fixed-design amphibious rover and one fixed-design starter spacecraft.
  • Ground vehicles: rovers, crawlers and mining rigs: Design reference; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Design preview: scout rovers, cargo crawlers and mobile mining rigs would serve different surface journeys.
  • Modular ships and expedition preparation: Design reference; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Design preview: prepare an expedition by balancing range, cargo, protection and utility modules.
  • A universe beyond the next ridge: Setting; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Explore a frontier of ancient institutions, independent communities and worlds shaped by long silences.
  • Worlds and places: Setting; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Get to know Epsilon-7, Bellwynn and Caldris II: places of civic life, cultivation and industry.
  • Work, trade and industry: Setting; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Meet the organizations behind the setting’s mining, shipping and advanced manufacturing.
  • Technology with a living purpose: Setting; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Read the frontier’s engineering through enduring structures, living greenery and carefully made machines.
  • Equipment with identity: Setting; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: Discover why an item’s name, craftsmanship and history can matter as much as its appearance.
  • Setting glossary: Setting; editorial summary, not verified released-build availability. Scope: A quick reference to the places, organizations and calendar of Elysium’s world.
