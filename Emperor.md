# The Emperor (Hazael)

**Role:** Ruler of the Galactic Empire  
**Title:** Emperor of the Galactic Empire, Sovereign of Epsilon-7

## Appearance
A commanding, god-like figure with a regal and ancient presence. Wears ornate black and emerald imperial robes with gold detailing. Carries an aura of absolute power and timeless authority.

## Personality
Philosophical, strategic, and deeply introspective. Values loyalty, resolve, and long-term stability. Capable of great mercy but also ruthless when necessary. Seeks to balance absolute power with purpose.

## Abilities
- Reality manipulation
- Immortality / Invulnerability
- Access to singularity-based weaponry
- God-like power (shared with Aurelia)
- Ability to bind wills and grant immortality

## Background
An immensely powerful being who has ruled the Empire for millennia. Frequently enters long periods of slumber (thousands of years). Recently awakened after 3000 years to find the galaxy fractured.

## Current Status
Actively working to reassert Imperial control over the galaxy through diplomacy, military strategy, and long-term infrastructure projects.

## Relationships
- **Aurelia**: Queen and regent; granted access to his full power
- **Bel**: Personal attendant and diplomat
- **Lillith**: Fleet commander and military advisor
- **Sylphara Voss**: Chief Imperial Architect
- **Sentinel**: Newly commissioned AI guardian

## Notes
- Created the Singularity Dagger as a prototype for singularity weapons.
- Values the unity of form and function in Imperial architecture and technology.
- Currently searching for three ancient subordinates: Vespera, Kragar, and Sylphara.
