# Lillith

**Role:** High-Ranking Fleet Commander

## Appearance
A tall, disciplined woman with long dark hair often tied in a high ponytail and sharp crimson eyes. Wears a formal black and emerald imperial military uniform with gold accents.

## Personality
Calm, precise, and highly professional. Extremely loyal and efficient. Values discipline, strategy, and results.

## Abilities
- Master strategist and fleet commander
- High-level military leadership
- Strong decision-making under pressure

## Background
One of the Emperor’s most trusted military commanders. Oversees fleet operations and major military deployments.

## Current Status
Managing the redeployment of Imperial fleets and preparing for potential conflict with refusing systems.

## Relationships
- **The Emperor**: Her direct superior
- **Aurelia**: Works closely with her on military and strategic matters
- **Bel and Sylphara Voss**: Close colleagues

## Notes
- Recently involved in testing the 11th Fleet.
- Follows only the Emperor’s or Aurelia’s direct orders.
