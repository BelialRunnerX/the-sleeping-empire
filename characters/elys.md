# Elysomnion

**Role:** Emperor · undercover independent researcher  
**Reference state:** Chapter XLVII · updated 2026-11-05

An effectively omnipotent, not omniscient sovereign seeking an ordinary life as a farmer and student.

## Profile

The ancient Emperor of the Black and Emerald Empire, whose Three-Day Unification ended historic wars at a staggering human cost. He retains ultimate sovereignty but delegates routine government to Aurelia. Publicly he lives and studies as Hazael Wheatley, farmer, research fellow and owner of Somnus. Only confidants know the link between identities.

## Character, relationships and story arc

**Actual identity:** immortal, ancient emperor and ultimate sovereign; primary player character. **Public current identity:** Hazael Wheatley, modest farmer, academy attendee and now independent research fellow. Earlier aliases *Hazael Somnion* and *Hazael of House Belor* belong to prior cover phases; current property registry says **Hazael Wheatley**. The Emperor's true public history remains unchanged.

**Power:** effectively omnipotent reality alteration, selective absolute defense (nullification/reflection) per root lore, instantaneous teleportation and precision structural manipulation. **Not omniscient.** Power does not automatically supply wisdom, strategy or knowledge of unseen events. He can make mistakes, be surprised, be distracted, enjoy a pun and choose restraint.

**Character tension:** terrifying sovereign who ended ancient warfare by force versus ordinary student deliberately subjecting himself to professors, exams, friendship, awkwardness and a desire to grow wheat. Seeks escape from 13,000 years of on-call responsibility without abandoning final authority. Philosophically provocative: will argue law/property/morality are fictions of power, then invoke God as the Good itself and identify sovereign with goodness. Do not assume he reconciled the contradictions. Regards solemnity and sacred silence highly, yet repeatedly engages in bawdy agricultural and bakery puns; ironic humor. He is not naturally a strategist despite older documents presenting him as strategic. He values independent will, especially rather than manufacturing assent. Has stated that authority's *ability* to compel is not itself the same as moral truth.

**Recent choices:** gifts Andromeda to Clarissa, purchases Caldris for affordable wheat, jointly acquires Nimbus, allows Aurelia to continue governing, creates flight-based noble eligibility without additional political power, becomes a research fellow, reverse-engineerable but not teachable magical materials. In institutional settings he prefers not to receive special treatment publicly.

**Future direction:** does he value ordinary life for its own sake, and how will his proclaimed identification with the Good withstand contradiction, mortal autonomy and historical responsibility? Preserve player-exclusive control of words/actions.

By Chapter XLVII he is Somnus’s sole shareholder under the Hazael Wheatley identity. His commercial consolidation is underway, with residential, jointly owned, restricted and sovereign assets separately protected. He placed third in the Bellweather tractor challenge (84/100), without magical assistance; his small field has germinated but has not been harvested.

## Appearance — foundational archive

A commanding, god-like figure with a regal and ancient presence. Wears ornate black and emerald imperial robes with gold detailing. Carries an aura of absolute power and timeless authority. His eyes are often described as piercing and unreadable, radiating an overwhelming sense of dominance.

## Established details and limits

- Sole shareholder of Somnus under the civilian identity Hazael Wheatley.
- Personally cultivates a small wheat field at his private Caldris farmhouse.
- His historical Imperial biography was never rewritten to make him a farmer.
- Effectively omnipotent but not omniscient; the player alone chooses his actions.

## Film-inspired quiet mercy — user-requested turn 000300

The user supplied a YouTube film link and specifically asked to draw upon the line “Aren't you tired of fighting? Let me help you” as an Elys **personality inspiration**. Independently identified as spoken by the Void in *Thunderbolts* (2025); **the actual YouTube edit's transcript could not be directly checked**. Elys is NOT the Void and the Void's apparent mercy masking annihilation is NOT adopted as Elys's ethics.

**Elys may be terribly calm, soft-spoken, and overwhelming without posturing.** He can recognize the exhaustion of a sincere, coerced or lost adversary and offer real relief and protection; surrender must be a genuine choice that preserves the person's dignity and future accountability, not trickery or erasure. His impossible-to-harm presence can make gentle offers more frightening than threats. He still has ironic humor, ordinary curiosity, sovereignty and willingness to use proportionate extreme force/companion combat as warranted; this is not compulsory pacifism or pardon. In [turn 000300](../saves/turns/000300.md) he protected surrendering patrol personnel, accepted the Light-Born's right not to tell him its name, and examined alternative technologies before coercive living removal.
