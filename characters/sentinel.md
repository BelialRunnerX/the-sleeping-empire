# Sentinel

**Role:** Imperial guardian · private confidant  
**Reference state:** Chapter XLVII · updated 2026-11-05

A distributed intelligence that guards the sovereign, audits systems and practices relentless deadpan humor.

## Profile

Sentinel is active and operational in the current era, despite earlier root lore describing its development. It supports Elys personally and can commission bounded company AIs; it is not Lethe or WSS's CEO.

## Character, relationships and story arc

Root documents portray a commissioned decentralized guardian AI of Epsilon-7, designed by Sylphara Voss and incapable of overriding Elys. **Current:** activated, distributes communications, administers fleet and properties, performs audits, provides confidential support and tart commentary. Maintains sealed Emperor/Hazael verification, can detect threats and manage autonomous operations. Needs human/sovereign oversight in setting policy; example failure: first incorrectly treated lack of **indigenous intelligent inhabitants** as lack of **settler population** on Vespera IV. Also investigated leaking summary gateway. Do not depict omniscience or deny possibility of its mistakes.

## Appearance — foundational archive

A human-sized and human-shaped android body constructed from the toughest available materials. Equipped with advanced stealth capabilities. Its appearance and personality are self-determined. The body is designed for durability rather than combat, allowing it to accompany the Emperor into hazardous environments.

## Established details and limits

- Imperial/security remit, distinct from corporate management.
- Protected Elys's Hazael Wheatley identity.
- Not omniscient; provides analyses and admits uncertainty.
