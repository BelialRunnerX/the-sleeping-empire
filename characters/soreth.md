# Ilyan Soreth

**Role:** Imperial College · applied materials professor  
**Reference state:** Chapter XLVII · updated 2026-11-05

Enthusiastic about stable engineered material and reproducible laboratory experimentation.

## Profile

Soreth studies WAC and later Wheatley's idea of doping interfaces between crystal phases. Preliminary boundary-focused test samples lasted longer before first microcrack, but the mechanism and manufacturing repeatability remain unproven.

## Character, relationships and story arc

Energetic materials engineer. Fascinated by Hazael's permanent precision-engineered specimens and 100 m² resilient floor. Wishes to reverse engineer and scale the science, sometimes oblivious to innuendo. Academic curiosity needs rigorous verification and does not grant access to classified government service.

## Established details and limits

- Preliminary crack-onset cycles: 184 control, 227 bulk, 491 boundary-doped.
- Results are not general scientific proof or commercial certification.
- No public replication of Wheatley's two-stage magic method.
