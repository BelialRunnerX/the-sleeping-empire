# Maelis Vorr

**Role:** Imperial College · dean and professor  
**Reference state:** Chapter XLVII · updated 2026-11-05

A rigorously independent academic, skeptical of miracles without data.

## Profile

Vorr oversees the College, assesses Hazael on merit, and insists on responsible experiments and proper scientific names, including a famous conflict over WAC-01 puns.

## Character, relationships and story arc

Exacting dean/faculty examiner at new Imperial College of Magical Excellence. Respects procedural academic merit, evidence, security boundaries and safety. Hazael's extraordinary skills impress but do not exempt him from paperwork. Fiercely opposes unprofessional WAC puns. Important new dynamic: she stands up to even a highly favored government-linked researcher and separates expertise from political sponsorship.

## Established details and limits

- Does not know Hazael is the Emperor.
- Respects proprietary magic methods and demands replicable evidence.
- Opposes unlicensed experiments in crowded classrooms.
