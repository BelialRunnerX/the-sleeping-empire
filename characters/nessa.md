# Nessa Calder

**Role:** Bellweather · terminal engineer  
**Reference state:** Chapter XLVII · updated 2026-11-05

Insists that infrastructure be repairable by the people who have to use it.

## Profile

Nessa knows the co-op's deteriorating grain terminal in painful detail and offered schematics, logs and seventeen corrections to WSS designs. Her primary demand: accessible, standardized equipment that works with existing plant.

## Established details and limits

- 17 documented engineering corrections.
- Existing loading cycle slow, couplings incompatible and repairs cumbersome.
- Advocates local technician training and routine maintainability.
