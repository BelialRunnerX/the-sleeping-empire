# Aven Rhel

**Role:** Imperial College · field dynamics  
**Reference state:** Chapter XLVII · updated 2026-11-05

Explores why raw magical power is not identical to control of a field.

## Profile

Rhel lectures on conflicting magical instructions, the difference between sustaining a field and establishing a condition, and whether absolute power can encounter systems that reject modification.

## Established details and limits

- Does not know Hazael's Imperial identity.
- His field-sovereignty language is a physics metaphor, not settled political theology.
