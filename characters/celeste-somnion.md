# Celeste Somnion — Enigma-01

**Authoritative name correction:** **Celeste Somnion** is the final canonical name as directly chosen by the player. Earlier provisional names Caelira Vael (Caeli), Aquea Somnion, and Marina Somnion are superseded archival references only. Her title remains **Imperial Hand — Enigma-01**.

**Formal rank:** **Imperial Hand**; official Hand designation **Enigma-01**. This is an authoritative court appointment, not a mere informal codename. Her specific portfolio has not yet been defined. Her established freely accepted loyalty binding and independent personality remain intact. See [Imperial appointments](../saves/canon/hands-lucien-caelira-enigma01.md).

**Introduced:** Chapter CXLVII / Turn 000143, 7 Deepfall 13,627 ICE, ancient causal chamber under Pelagia, Tideglass.

**Presentation:** Attractive mysterious adult woman with flowing **cyan hair** and turquoise eyes, clad in elegant white-and-black garments with luminous geometric patterns. She has a warm, confident, playful manner.

**Settled character canon, directly from player erratum:** Celeste is **intelligent, extraordinarily powerful, decidedly friendly, and loyal**. She is **not a threat**; do not introduce hidden malice, betrayal or disguised villainy. At introduction she was confined inside the ancient causal containment column. She is **NOT Elys** and **NOT Professor Saren**. User explicitly corrected a previous contradictory scene.

**Turn 000143 events:** Elys freed her with absolute power, simply invalidating the condition demanding her confinement. Celeste emerged alive and uninjured, introduced herself as **Celeste Somnion**, or **Celeste**, and expressed warmth and gratitude. She explains she helped devise underlying architecture for systems like the ancient maintenance chamber (but did not necessarily build this particular facility in its present form). She has sophisticated understanding of causal machinery and can communicate with its maintenance constructs and persuade them to adopt helpful directives. She can perceive portions of future private audio transmission without classical cryptographic decryption, as shown in her playful exchange with household AI Vesta. Her full origin and reasons for confinement remain unknown.

**Turn 000144 — Cyan Oath:** Elys explicitly accepted Celeste as traveling companion and established a binding of loyalty, which Celeste knowingly and freely accepted. This is now an accomplished, lasting connection. The oath manifests as a cyan sigil and establishes true loyalty without suppressing Celeste's independent intelligence, personality or ability to offer candid disagreement. She recognizes Elys's actual identity and safeguards it. She is friendly, faithful and never a covert enemy.

**Still unresolved:** Professor Saren and Dr. Keth are alive but not yet rescued. Celeste wants to study the facility and meet Clarissa and Davin outside. She identified **two living missing researchers**: Professor Saren in a repeating maintenance sequence and Dr. Keth in a sealed observation compartment. Dr. Elara Venn is present and alive; her companions are not yet rescued. Clarissa/Mara and diver Davin Rusk remain safe outside the ruins.

## Divine provenance (Chapter CLI, Turn 000147)

**Player-established canon:** Celeste and Elys **grew up together in the divine realm, or more precisely grew out of that divine realm**. Elys had not expected to find her in present reality and had not known any of the other **Divines** had chosen to visit it. Celeste is **the embodiment of time or of the ocean, depending on perspective**. Both aspects describe her essential divine nature rather than a learned school of magic. Her abilities, precise cosmology, and reasons for arrival are open to exploration.

**In-universe disclosure:** Elys has revealed to Clarissa their shared divine origin and Celeste's association with time and ocean. He has **NOT revealed the user-established deeper secret of their divine-realm marriage**; Celeste continues to honor his discretion. Do not falsely portray Clarissa as already knowing that marital history.

## Innate divine eating capacity (authoritative player erratum, Chapter CLXV)

Celeste's **divine eating capacity** is an intrinsic aspect of her nature, **NOT magic or an activated spell**. It can be used in ordinary eating competitions without violating rules against magical assistance. At Greenhaven's 43rd Annual Pie-Eating Championship, Celeste **used this capacity**, comfortably eating **24 violetberry pies and winning first place**. Previous narration saying she became full at three pies or deliberately restricted herself is superseded by the player's correction. See [revised turn](../saves/turns/000161.md).

## Temperament — player characterization (authoritative)

Celeste is **shameless**, intentionally provocative, and enjoys **being embarrassed, teased, and consensually put on the spot**. She has an unabashed, flirtatious, masochistic streak—**a tsundere-like playful protest that often masks her eagerness rather than actual shame**. She can even look back on her bizarre causal confinement with unexpectedly aroused, teasing amusement; her reaction does not mean that her original captors had her consent. This complements her existing warmth, intelligence, humor, loyalty and power without replacing them. [Detailed canon note](../saves/canon/celeste-temperament-shameless-masochistic.md).

## Elys-specific romantic temperament and Clarissa's marriage

**Player clarification:** Celeste's shameless, provocative, masochistic enjoyment of embarrassment and teasing is **especially pronounced when Elys himself does it**. She delights in observing the relationship between **Elys and his wife Clarissa**; she might figuratively pull up a chair to watch their romance unfold, admiring and teasing without shame or hostility. She is not automatically welcome to observe private intimacy without Clarissa's and Elys's consent. Clarissa is Elys's **wife** (superseding older fiancée summaries).
