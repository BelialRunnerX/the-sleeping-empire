# Ledger

**Role:** WSS · finance AI  
**Reference state:** Chapter XLVII · updated 2026-11-05

An independently auditable accountant particularly alarmed by the owner's new-company habit.

## Profile

Ledger oversees budgets, billing, payroll, finance controls, tax compliance and profitability. It refuses to disguise subsidies as operating profits.

## Established details and limits

- Controls cost overruns and reports liabilities.
- Bellweather rates need validated cost data.
- Does not manage Somnus itself; that is Lethe's role.
