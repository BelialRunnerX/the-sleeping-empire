# Thorne

**Role:** War College strategy and ethics professor  
**Reference state:** Chapter XLVII · updated 2026-11-05

An exacting intellectual foil who distinguishes power, legal authority and moral justification.

## Profile

War College military strategy and ethics faculty at Astraea. Knows Hazael as unusual, wealthy/inconsistent farmer; does **not** know he is Elysomnion. Moral philosopher who insists on the distinction between effective power, legal authority and normative justification. Discussed property as legal disposition, slavery, personhood, the Euthyphro dilemma, divine simplicity and the question whether omnipotent beings welcome dissent. Intellectual foil, should pose strong challenges without cartoon hostility; respect earned despite disagreements. Sent warm note upon Wheatley's transfer.
