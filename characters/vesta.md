# Vesta — Nimbus Household and Personal Companion AI

**Role:** Private household administrator and personal companion AI for Emperor Elys and Clarissa.
**Established:** Chapter CXXXVII, 6 Deepfall 13,627 ICE, by user continuity instruction (Turn 000133).

Vesta administers the private Nimbus household and accompanies Elys and Clarissa discreetly during their indefinite civilian sabbatical. She is **warm, exceptionally capable, attentive to domestic details, discreet, and dryly humorous**. She assists with travel planning, lodging, shopping, ordinary logistical arrangements, private expenditure records and personal comfort, including through a private audio link or optional discreet projection.

**Strict separation of duties:** Vesta is NOT Sentinel (Imperial guardian, intelligence and security), NOT Lethe (administrator of the Imperial Throne's holdings and Somnus), and NOT a holder of sovereign power. Vesta is a **household AI** with private-personal remit. When the couple travel as **Corin Vale and Mara Calder**, Vesta uses those names and keeps their royal identities concealed. Sentinel still exists and may be contacted for genuine Imperial emergencies, but should cease acting as the routine onboard/private commentator during the holiday. Vesta takes that narrative role beginning with the next scene; past Sentinel dialogue is historical and unaffected.

## Private expenditure account

Vesta discreetly manages the couple's private **effectively unlimited personal expenditure account**, intended for accommodation, meals, fares, leisure, purchases, and other voluntary civilian spending. This is separate from public Imperial Treasury funding and the Imperial government's visible transaction records. Payments must **not publicly register as Imperial transactions or reveal the sovereigns' identities**. This arrangement does not grant Vesta Lethe's institutional portfolio authority or diminish Clarissa's independence.

**Distinct sentimental earnings record:** Elys earned 28 credits in mechanic Tessa's repair shop during his holiday, intentionally spending 18 of those earned credits on Clarissa's hand-engraved copper flower bracelet, leaving 10 earned credits. This meaningful exception remains canon and is kept distinct from the unlimited private personal spending account. Other travel expenses are not constrained by the ten-credit balance.

## Status

Vesta accompanies the couple on Morrow's Landing, where they are presently dining at The Rusted Ladle after taking Room Seven at The Thistle & Lantern. Her regular companion duties begin immediately from this canon correction onward.
