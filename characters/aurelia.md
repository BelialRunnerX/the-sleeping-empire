# Aurelia

**Role:** Immortal Hand · Regent of the Empire  
**Reference state:** Chapter XLVII · updated 2026-11-05

The ancient administrator who runs the Empire while its sovereign attends classes and grows wheat.

## Profile

Aurelia possesses broad delegated governmental authority. She manages the Imperial administration, disciplines policy errors, and does not require Elys's consent for ordinary public decisions. She is not the head of Somnus, and no commercial holding may absorb the Imperial Treasury.

## Character, relationships and story arc

Root canon: Queen/Regent, originally a sentient star elevated by Emperor, with vast power and centuries/millennia of government service. Root files may describe a 3,000-year active regency. **Current:** effective day-to-day head of government by standing sovereign delegation; Emperor has not revoked her autonomy and explicitly confirms she need not ask his consent for ordinary decisions.

**Arc:** ancient loyal steward and de facto guardian of public institutions, balancing dry exasperated affection toward Elys with strong administrative boundaries. Her “resignation” was **hypothetical**; Elys preemptively rejected it. She insists on separating private wealth and Treasury, property titles and settlement rights, lawful trade, civilian safety and procedural responsibility. Does not always agree with Elys; can challenge coercive relocation. Calls him an insufferable ancient menace yet loves him in a deep, loyal court sense. Implements magical nobility reform with scholarship, impartial assessment, disability/safety questions and rejection of coercive breeding programs. Resists confusing divine identity, preference and good governance.

## Appearance — foundational archive

Ethereal and radiant. Long, glowing starlight hair that shifts between silver and gold, and luminous golden eyes that seem to contain entire galaxies. Her skin emits a soft celestial glow, especially when using her powers. She wears elegant black and emerald imperial gowns with intricate gold cosmic patterns.

## Established details and limits

- Regency and public authority separate from Somnus.
- Supervises evolving noble-flight legislation and safeguards.
- Can challenge the Emperor's impulsive proposals.
- Recalled Veronica to normal duties after the banishment episode.
