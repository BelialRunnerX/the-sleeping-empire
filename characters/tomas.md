# Tomas Venn

**Role:** Cooperative representative  
**Reference state:** Chapter XLVII · updated 2026-11-05

Represents thousands of farming households and seeks a viable partnership rather than charity.

## Profile

Tomas represents roughly four thousand farming households on three agricultural worlds serving eleven developing systems. He warns that unrestricted cheap Caldris grain could destroy independent production and offers a nonexclusive storage and logistics partnership.

## Established details and limits

- Bellweather WSS service framework negotiated but not signed.
- Hosts Hazael and Clarissa at Morrow's Crossing harvest fair.
- His price and volume forecasts require verification.
