# Lucien Vale

**Role:** Student · inventor · pastry entrepreneur  
**Reference state:** Chapter XLVII · updated 2026-11-05

Inventor and enthusiastic bakery magnate with access to an absurdly dangerous ship.

## Profile

Lucien founded the orbital Cream-Pie-R bakery venture, became embroiled in a security-summary leak through a misconfigured bakery bot, and dreams of becoming Baron von Buttercream. The title has NOT been granted. He uses the Extraordinary Debtor on defined civilian terms but does not own it.

## Character, relationships and story arc

Student, inventor, Andromedan citizen, proprietor of approved-in-principle **Cream-Pie-R** orbital bakery, comic counterweight and one of Elys's closest boisterous friends. Often speaks IN ALL CAPS; obsession with cream puffs, absurd branding, grand titles and engineering. Former owner of ultra-advanced ship later transferred back to Elys; now authorized civilian operator with major financial liabilities for his own accidents. Wants title **Baron von Buttercream**. **Has demonstrated personal magical lift but not controlled flight**; eligible under narrow legal wording but no title granted. His bot unintentionally accessed a badly secured summary gateway and leaked confidential snippets to bakery group; no evidence he knows Hazael's Imperial identity. Veronica later questioned him about the leak; no established revelation of Hazael’s Imperial identity follows from that questioning. Downstream access remains an open security issue. Comic irresponsibility does not mean malice or complete lack of competence: genuine engineering flair and ample magical potential.

## Established details and limits

- His automated notification bot accidentally obtained meeting summaries; gateway was closed.
- Flight qualification for nobility differs from controlled public-flight safety testing.
- Extraordinary Debtor ownership reverted to Elys.
