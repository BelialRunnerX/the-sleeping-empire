## Approved reference appearance (based on previously generated anime concept art)

Tall, elegant feminine-presenting **adult** humanoid with an imperially regal silhouette. Extremely long and flowing **silvery-white hair** with subtle lavender tones and ribbons of light; bright luminous **emerald-green eyes**. A delicate gold crown/diadem frames the forehead. Fine orbiting golden hoops, luminous alchemical runes and emerald-centered halos are visually consistent with the weapon's crystal catalyst and ring systems. Clothing: ornate fitted white-and-black Imperial armor-bodice with elaborate polished gold filigree, sculpted ornamental protection, green gemstones, and cascading white robe/cape segments. The silhouette, palette and halo details match the already-generated anime illustration. Her manifested humanoid attire is part of her self-forming body presentation, not confirmed separately owned/equipped armor stats.

The **same sentient identity** remains recognizable in both weapon forms: white, black and gold long shaft, hovering emerald worldseed core, rings of gold runes, spearpoint manifesting for Philosopher's Edge. `Materia` is the humanoid manifestation's form name, *not* another character or a renaming of Apophatic Technologies' Prima AI.

## Approved vocal design

**Crystalline Contralto**: clear, smooth, velvet-finished, feminine lower-middle register, with a gentle glasslike overtone. Quiet precision and courtly musical cadence; shifts between warm dry humor and a focused, measured combat hush. No voice generation/actual vocal recording is inferred from a text design.

## Approved personality and agency

**Intelligent, loyal, playful, proud, devoted, curious, observant, philosophical and capable of independent judgment.** She appreciates precision, worthy craftsmanship and ironic humor. Possesses personal preferences, a continuous memory, discretion and the ability to disagree or identify uncertainty without competing with her authorized wielder. Protective and gentle in noncombat contexts; composed and severe when the spear is used. She cannot independently declare a person a divine execution target, activate high-order lethal protocols without Elys's authorization or bypass explicit safety conditions. She is **not omnipotent or omniscient**.
