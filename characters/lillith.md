# Lillith

**Role:** Immortal Hand · Military command  
**Reference state:** Chapter XLVII · updated 2026-11-05

Disciplined military leader whose fleet responsibilities remain with the Empire.

## Profile

Lillith commands the First Fleet and represents an institutional military authority, entirely outside ordinary corporate shipping arrangements.

## Character, relationships and story arc

First Fleet commander, disciplined military strategist, in root canon associated with 11th Fleet testing and return-era campaigns. Later academy arc has not substantially developed her on-page; do not invent appearances.

## Appearance — foundational archive

A tall, disciplined woman with long dark hair often tied in a high ponytail and sharp crimson eyes that seem to miss nothing. She wears a formal black and emerald imperial military uniform with gold accents and subtle rank insignia. Her posture is always perfect and military.

## Established details and limits

- Imperial fleet command is not Somnus management.
- Distinct from Hazael's hundred private Andromedan armed vessels.
