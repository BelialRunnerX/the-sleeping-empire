# Halden Rook — Former Asterion Director

**First played appearance:** [Turn 000303](../saves/turns/000303.md), Chapter CCLII.  
**Character ID:** `char-halden-rook`  
**Status:** ALIVE, lawfully detained, due independent hearing and counsel.

A long-lived human administrator preserved through technological life extension. Living executive behind the distributed *Auditor* command system at Asterion, which is distinct and preserved for its own technical and possible agency review. Rook signed directives perpetuating coercive use of conscious Light-Born navigational references and canceled proposed humane technical alternatives. Original 23 transfers have since been accounted for, 15 alive and eight verified dead. Exact criminal responsibility and sentencing remain for lawful determination; authentication of certain Rook signatures/permissions occurs on-page in Chapter CCLII.

Elys confronted him and offered actual safe surrender with accountability; a concealed defense attack was utterly negated by Elys's intrinsic absolute powers, and Aletheia disabled unmanned weapons while human personnel were spared. Rook voluntarily yielded command credentials and was taken into lawful custody. Veronica tauntingly presented him to Elys as a trophy but did not sexually coerce or harm him. His current personal powers and numerical stats UNKNOWN. No item confiscation credited to Elys.
