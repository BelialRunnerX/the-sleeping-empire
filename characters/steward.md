# Steward

**Role:** WSS · chief executive  
**Reference state:** Chapter XLVII · updated 2026-11-05

Manager of day-to-day WSS operations, coordinating three specialist AIs.

## Profile

Steward is empowered for normal company operations and reports material exceptions to Hazael/Lethe. WSS is operational administratively, while its infrastructure is being built.

## Established details and limits

- Coordinates Meridian, Ledger and Granary.
- Separates operational actions from major shareholder decisions.
- Monthly reports and significant risk escalations.
