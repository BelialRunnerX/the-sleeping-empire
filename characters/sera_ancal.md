# Captain Sera Ancal

**First played appearance:** [Turn 000301](../saves/turns/000301.md), Chapter CCXLVII.  
**Character ID:** char-sera-ancal  
**Status:** Alive; recovered from long-term preservation inside the damaged survey vessel **Madrigal**, medical bay, folded spatial region beyond Asterion Depot.

Adult woman in older expedition uniform, dark blue-black hair streaked silver. Commander of an independent scientific survey ship roughly one kilometer long, the **Madrigal**, whose original manifest lists **nine crew**. She is **one confirmed living member**. The others' fates remain unknown, not automatically deceased or living. Her medical preservation chronology suggests centuries may have passed outside, but exact elapsed period is unreliable; do not invent biological age or rank beyond captain.

Sera is practical, skeptical and initially disoriented. She remembers her crew investigating artificial stabilization beacons around a spatial 'wound': some appear to stabilize its boundaries while others make it move. No complete mechanism or causal theory is confirmed. Her ship has badly damaged historical sensor logs and a fragmented warning transmission containing "NOT THE ENEMY", "DO NOT FOLLOW THE LIGHT", and "WE ARE STILL"; their sender and intended meaning are unknown, and the transcript is incomplete.

Elys and one physical humanoid Aletheia found Sera alive in medical suspension, stabilized its life support and safely awakened her. She is not yet medically cleared for dangerous travel or completely debriefed. She is independent owner/operator only insofar as her command of the craft is known; **no legal title transfer of Madrigal to Elys or Somnus**. She knows these travelers are on 'holiday'/sabbatical, but their imperial identities need not automatically be established.
