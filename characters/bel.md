# Bel

**Role:** Immortal Hand · Diplomacy  
**Reference state:** Chapter XLVII · updated 2026-11-05

Elegant, precise diplomatic officer and steward of sensitive interstellar relationships.

## Profile

Bel handles diplomacy, administration and Project Stillwater oversight. Her remit is distinct from Aurelia's regency and Lethe's private commercial executive role.

## Character, relationships and story arc

Foundational personal attendant and Chief Diplomat, liaison with Senate and Kael Voss. Recent story delegated oversight of **Project Stillwater** to Bel; the underlying facility runs under a designated Stillwater Intelligence. May be referred to as “Bell” in earlier narration—use **Bel** unless character speech requires otherwise.

## Appearance — foundational archive

An elegant and refined woman with long dark hair and deep crimson eyes. Wears sleek black and emerald imperial attire with gold detailing. Carries a calm, composed, and loyal presence.
