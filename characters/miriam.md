# Miriam Holt

**Role:** Bellweather · veteran farmer  
**Reference state:** Chapter XLVII · updated 2026-11-05

A lifelong farmer who convincingly beats a god-emperor in a tractor competition.

## Profile

Miriam wins the Morrow's Crossing machinery challenge with 96 points, ahead of Elias 93 and Hazael 84. She advocates grain reserves and reliable distribution without sacrificing local farmers' livelihoods.

## Established details and limits
