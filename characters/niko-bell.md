# Niko Bell — Bellwynn Young Inventor

**First recorded:** Chapter CLIII, [Turn 000149](../saves/turns/000149.md), **8 Deepfall 13,627 ICE**, Greenhaven Harvest & Innovation Festival, Bellwynn.

Niko Bell is a **local teenager** who builds homemade agricultural robots. Niko wears grease-stained overalls and enters a fiercely imperfect robot called **Sir Turnip III** in the festival's agricultural obstacle course. Niko explains that earlier prototypes met unfortunate ends: Sir Turnip I caught fire and Sir Turnip II drove into a pond.

The current robot has an oversized wheel and two very small legs, an imaginative but poorly implemented balance algorithm and an exuberant audio module. Celeste Somnion takes an interest in the design and helps Niko improve its center of mass using ordinary hand tools, without divine interference or violating the amateur contest rules.

**Latest status:** Sir Turnip III is standing at the obstacle-course starting line with a polished sponsored robot among its rivals. The first heat has **not yet been resolved**, and **no result is canon**.

Niko's future connections, family and long-term career are **not established**.
