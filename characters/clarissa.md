# Clarissa Somnion

**Role:** **Wife of Elys** · Empress of Andromeda  
**Reference state:** Chapter CXXXVI · continuity corrected 2026-11-06

Independent sovereign owner of Andromeda, loving partner, and Bellweather commercial negotiator.

**LATEST PLAYER CANON CORRECTION:** Elys and Clarissa are **married**. Any older references to fiancée are superseded; the ceremony's date/details have not been supplied. [Canon record](../saves/canon/clarissa-married-celeste-elys-dynamic.md).

## Profile

**Authoritative age and origin correction:** Clarissa is only **a couple of centuries old**. She attended the academy **as a fellow student alongside Elys approximately 200 years ago**. Elys subsequently **gifted her immortality and Andromeda**. She is **an advisor to the Senate on behalf of Lillith**. Do not portray her as a many-millennia-old immortal or pre-academy historical witness. This correction supersedes conflicting narration. See [the continuity note](../saves/canon/clarissa-age-role-correction-2026-11-06.md).

Clarissa received the entire Andromeda galaxy as an engagement gift and owns its assets outright, independently of the Imperial Treasury and Somnus. She shares Nimbus 50/50 with Hazael, built the cozy Caldris farmhouse, enjoys ordinary agricultural life, and was explicitly tasked with Bellweather WSS services negotiations.

## Character, relationships and story arc

**Current position:** Elys's fiancée, Imperial Hand, and **Empress/sole private owner of the entire Andromedan dominion**, her engagement gift. Publicly joint owner of **Nimbus (50%)** with Hazael Wheatley. The possessive nickname **Clari** is used by Elys.

**Arc:** From dedicated inner-court collaborator to a sovereign with independent property, income and stewardship responsibilities, and a partner asserting her own decisions. She values affection, intimacy and an ordinary home with Elys; imagines gardens, books, an observatory and storm-watching. She recognizes Elys's godlike power yet asks what he values, whom he trusts and whether he will respect others' freedom. Wit is flirty and warm; not a purely compliant echo. Wants an Andromedan development fund and preserve public-benefit commerce. Can personally levitate.

**Privacy:** knows Hazael is Emperor; public joint registry must not expose that fact. Clarissa appearing alongside wealthy Hazael can attract attention but is not itself proof of his identity.

By Chapter XLVII, she lives with Elys at the Caldris farmhouse, enjoys the Bellweather fair and keeps his third-place tractor ribbon. On Elys’s instruction she negotiates WSS service pricing with Tomas and Nessa; the framework is provisional, with final tariffs and signatures pending. She is not subordinate to Lethe.

## Established details and limits

- Andromeda remains 100% her private property.
- Nimbus: 50% Clarissa, 50% Hazael.
- Negotiating WSS services with Tomas and Nessa; final rates and signatures pending.
- Fiancée, not yet married; capable and independently minded.
- Around 200 years old; Elys's former academy classmate, gifted immortality and Andromeda by Elys.
- Advisor to the Senate on behalf of Lillith.
