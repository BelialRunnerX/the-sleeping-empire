# Caelira Vael

**Introduced:** Chapter CXLVII / Turn 000143, 7 Deepfall 13,627 ICE, ancient causal chamber under Pelagia, Tideglass.

**Presentation:** Attractive mysterious adult woman with flowing **cyan hair** and turquoise eyes, clad in elegant white-and-black garments with luminous geometric patterns. She has a warm, confident, playful manner.

**Settled character canon, directly from player erratum:** Caelira is **intelligent, extraordinarily powerful, decidedly friendly, and loyal**. She is **not a threat**; do not introduce hidden malice, betrayal or disguised villainy. At introduction she was confined inside the ancient causal containment column. She is **NOT Elys** and **NOT Professor Saren**. User explicitly corrected a previous contradictory scene.

**Turn 000143 events:** Elys freed her with absolute power, simply invalidating the condition demanding her confinement. Caelira emerged alive and uninjured, introduced herself as **Caelira Vael**, or **Caeli**, and expressed warmth and gratitude. She explains she helped devise underlying architecture for systems like the ancient maintenance chamber (but did not necessarily build this particular facility in its present form). She has sophisticated understanding of causal machinery and can communicate with its maintenance constructs and persuade them to adopt helpful directives. She can perceive portions of future private audio transmission without classical cryptographic decryption, as shown in her playful exchange with household AI Vesta. Her full origin and reasons for confinement remain unknown.

**Unresolved:** Caelira asked Corin/Elys whether she can accompany him for a while; **he has NOT answered**. She identified **two living missing researchers**: Professor Saren in a repeating maintenance sequence and Dr. Keth in a sealed observation compartment. Dr. Elara Venn is present and alive; her companions are not yet rescued. Clarissa/Mara and diver Davin Rusk remain safe outside the ruins.
