# Tamsin Orel

**First played appearance:** [Turn 000301](../saves/turns/000301.md), Chapter CCXLV.  
**Character ID:** char-tamsin-orel  
**Status:** Alive, at Asterion Depot experimental calibration wing; no combat/equipment statistics established.

Adult Asterion engineer with gray-threaded dark hair and scar through her left eyebrow, wearing a worn technical uniform stripped of some professional insignia. Formerly senior calibration engineer, demoted to maintenance and subjected to punitive surveillance after she **refused to sign off unsafe coerced living-subject replacement procedures**. Her personal chronology includes the narration that she pursued a nonliving reference alternative for roughly two centuries; precise biological age unknown.

Tamsin is technically exacting, weary of self-justifying administrative cruelty, and capable of disagreeing with a would-be rescuer. She supplies an original twelve-ring synthetic phase-reference concept and knows the experimental control histories. Elys prevented a remote execution drone from harming her. Elys built local test fixtures, Aletheia wholly transformed to Genesis Loom staff and assisted with measurements, and Tamsin designed a route-aware controller to compensate for feedback through folded space. In a limited live trial **one Asterion calibration channel is supported by a synthetic nonliving reference** with validated fallback, allowing the third historic Light-Born to consent to leaving the machine safely. **Full Night Engine stabilization remains UNVALIDATED.**

Tamsin remains a free independent engineer; not automatically an Imperial subject to a new employer, owned asset, romantic interest, or permanent member of Elys's party. No known level, numeric stats, weapon, funds or rank.

**Current thread:** further design, safety validation and audit at Asterion, with the mysterious Auditor still controlling parts of the installation.
