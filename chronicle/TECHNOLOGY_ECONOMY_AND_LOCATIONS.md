# Technology, Holdings, Planets, Military Assets and Projects

## Physical / magical technology

Magic is **technical** in the setting: wards, relays, trained lattices, signalers/signallers, energy tolerances, failure modes, recordkeeping, suppression structures and safety protocols. Engineering and magic interface with actual materials and physical behavior. Root [Imperial Technology](../Imperial_Technology.md) specifies singularity cannons, the original **Singularity Dagger**, singularity-forged neutronium, exceptionally durable Citadel, stealth and self-repairing distributed infrastructure. The architectural ideal is **unity of form and function**, associated with Elys and Chief Architect Sylphara Voss.

Elys has demonstrated technically impossible precision, reality rewriting, passive nullification and reflection. Keep *phenomenological observation* separate from *reproducible method*: he may perform something effortlessly yet be unable to explain it step-by-step to less powerful scientists.

### Magic-engineered material properties (Chapter XXXVIII)

Hazael's independent research assessment includes:
- Magically changing microstructure rather than imposing an impermanent enchantment: crystal grain control, tailored defect/boundary arrangement, electrical/thermal properties, fracture resistance and stress pathways.
- Persistent post-casting functionality with no detectable sustaining spell.
- Four material samples given to the College for study and reverse engineering; **he cannot presently teach an exact procedure** and his government background is classified.
- **Wheatley Adaptive Composite (WAC-01):** 10 m × 10 m = **100 m²** section of Examination Hall Three floor converted into resilient *engineered* cushioning material, still structurally integrated, with proposed resilience against impacts, heat/electricity, chemical aggression and transmutation. Early tests pass, but no quantified bounds or general manufacturability established.
- **Emergency pillows:** hundreds of physically real cushions spontaneously appear below Lucien's falling body during a failed assessment. He is permitted to take the pillows; this is distinct from changing the underlying hall floor.
- **WAC-1-OFF, WAC Attack, “forced WAC-ing”:** irreverent proposed product and protective-combat naming, not a deployed weapon program. Brainstormed uses: protective barriers, kinetic dissipation, drop/landing zones, impact mitigation and evacuation; exact performance remains untested. Vorr insists on scientific evidence and nomenclature review; Soreth wants to study it.
- Do not retroactively have Hazael deliver lengthy technical instructions or reveal classified experience without player action.

## Caldris II — The Imperial Plowing Grounds

**Status:** PURCHASED, publicly titled to **Hazael Wheatley (100%)**, privately financed. Clear title/no inhabitants after verification. **Role:** wheat farm to provide reliable low-price exports to developing worlds; not a personal vacation palace. Preferred over variable-storm Thalassar for stable climate, freshwater, abundant arable lands and lower infrastructure burdens.

**Operating concept:** Sentinel plans climate/environment monitoring, soil/irrigation automation, automated planting/harvest, grain drying/processing, reserve storage, orbital lift and export depots, 100 privately owned military-capable Andromedan vessels as freight/shipping defense, and a mobile orbital shipyard manufacturing suitable industrial modules. Comply with land, environmental, trade and shipping laws. Preserve wild areas, watersheds, soils and crop resilience rather than strip-mine all land for monoculture. Initial setup and infrastructure deployment had begun by latest scene, not yet a completed production economy. Financial objective **lowest reasonably sustainable price**, invest positive surplus back into farming and maintenance; not necessarily zero-profit or maximum private profit.

**Privacy:** name is publicly suggestive but does not disclose true sovereign ownership. The public may see an extravagant rich farmer.

## Nimbus (formerly Thalassar Prime)

**Status:** PURCHASED; jointly titled **Hazael Wheatley (50%) / Clarissa Somnion (50%)**. Official name **Nimbus** chosen jointly. Not Caldris; not Andromeda. Large continents, rivers, immense seasonal storms and variable harvest risk. Owned as a **private preserved natural-world estate** and prospective home, not main wheat farm.

**Proposed residence:** highland promontory overlooking ocean, valleys, waterfall, lake and offshore storms; cottage/manor with large windows, library, fireplace, garden, observatory and possible private orbital elevator. It remains a **concept/design**, no completed construction as of latest scene. Elys teleports Clarissa to future site for ~7 minutes; strong crosswinds, his concern for her safety and instantaneous return to capital become romantic/comic scene. Their future home represents separation from public responsibilities and mutual chosen partnership.

## Epsilon-7 and Imperial Court

Capital, throne, Citadel and council chamber; throne room withstands direct advanced Andromedan strike, unharmed (not shield-bounced). Root Citadel uses singularity-forged neutronium. The Imperial **Festival of the Invincible Crown** is an authorized annual safe reenactment of extraordinary throne resilience; separate from grand **Festival of Sevenfold Unity**, the latter intended to formally celebrate the end of Final War with colossal cake.

Aurelia governs government, budget, fleet/diplomatic coordination in Elys's absence. Public record of Emperor's history is authoritative and **unchanged** even after Hazael cover updates.

## S.S. Extraordinary Debtor

Earlier names **S.S. Crème Puff**, then **S.S. Debtor**. Initially given to Lucien, then transferred title back to Elys/Hazael. Lucien has civilian access and workshop/commercial use. Owner pays routine operating costs, Lucien liable for extraordinary losses from his own activity. Extremely advanced (roughly a million-year lead over standard technology, as described in the story), not representative of normal civilian fleets. Weapons disabled except narrow automated defense against **non-government aggressors**; passive defensive systems remain available. Its target practice against Imperial throne led to **Operation Sovereign Resilience** and authorized 4,096-drone mock bombardment.
