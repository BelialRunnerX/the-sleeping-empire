# Philosophy, Religion, Governance, Law and Moral Tensions

This document records **in-universe doctrines and arguments**. It does not independently certify a character's metaphysical assertions as truth.

## 1. Absolutionism and sovereign government

See root [Absolutionism](../Absolutionism.md), [The Senate](../The_Senate.md), [Empire History](../Empire_History.md), [Imperial Religion](../Imperial_Religion.md). Foundational canon describes **Absolutionism** as an absolute monarchy with conditional aristocratic recognition and personal favor; all delegated authority ultimately derives from Elysomnion and is revocable. The contemporary name **Black and Emerald Empire** coexists with earlier **Galactic Empire** usage. The Senate gives systems and peoples a voice; local autonomy and civil institutions remain despite final Imperial sovereignty.

### Distinctions emphasized by current arc
- **Formal constitutional sovereignty:** Emperor is ultimate authority; can revoke delegated powers.
- **Ordinary operational governance:** Aurelia governs by a continuing, standing delegation. Elys explicitly corrected her anxiety about needing intervention permissions: the existing Regency authority stands without constant new approval.
- **Civil and local institutions:** Senate, ministries, territorial governments, military ranks, citizenship, contracts, land titles and administrative regulation matter in practice.
- **Ownership:** Elys provocatively describes legal ownership as the right to determine an object's ultimate legal disposition and argues its legitimacy is socially/governmentally constituted. This is an **argument voiced by Elys**, not a universal metaphysical fact, and its extension to persons and land was challenged.
- **Separation of patrimonies:** Emperor's private trusts and holdings are NOT the Imperial Treasury; his ₡1 billion monthly allocation is personally funded. Clarissa's Andromedan galaxy is **her private property**; public benefit is a contractual/stewardship question. Nimbus is a 50/50 private jointly owned planet; Caldris is wholly Hazael's.
- **Legal authority versus ethical right:** Sovereignty can enable coercion. It does not automatically remove the costs, rights disputes, or ethical significance of coercion. Vespera IV acquisition is the key concrete test.

## 2. Emperor's political motives and historical cost

Elys founded the Empire to end persistent warfare and render the galaxy governable and peaceful. The **Three-Day Unification** required massive violence and killed millions; neither the setting nor its narrators should erase that moral weight. Empire's enlightenment/absolute-monarchy ideal contains tension between plural local agency and concentrated ultimate coercive power. A statue honoring a rebel in the capital symbolizes the sovereign's stated respect for resistance and conscience. He often prefers disabling opponents' ability to wage war rather than bending their will, but history and behavior should allow debate about how much restraint his government truly practices.

While sleeping, he remained on call. His present desire for university, agriculture, friendship and life with Clarissa is a character arc, not formal abdication. Aurelia has not accepted a throne transfer; Elys reserves his right to resume the seat.

## 3. Thorne's ethics debate: descriptive power and normative good

**Key proposition:** "Power wins, morality is a fiction of how one ought to act, power controls how you can act."

Interpretation: practical power constrains what outcomes can be enforced; moral language claims what *ought* to be done. Thorne points out that an option being available does not explain why someone selects it, and lack of enforcement does not disprove a moral claim. He asks why a being with unlimited power would debate independent minds if it could compel agreement.

**Euthyphro dilemma:** Is an act good because God wills it, or does God will it because it is good?

**Elys's answer:** "Neither: it is the Good"; later: "The Emperor is divine"; "What the Emperor wills is the good"; "Anything contrary to that is subjective"; "The sovereign is the goodness." This resembles a classical *divine simplicity* answer only if the sovereign's essence actually is identical with ultimate goodness and willing flows from that nature. It is not automatically demonstrated by the sovereign's overwhelming power.

**Debated issues to retain rather than resolve by narration:**
- Does moral truth exist independently of belief? Elys earlier interrogates its objectivity but then positively claims ultimate Good.
- Is goodness identical with a divine *nature* or with whatever contingent preference a ruler expresses? The two are not straightforwardly identical.
- Can the sovereign change his mind, issue contradictory edicts, or err?
- Can citizens know the sovereign's true nature/intentions with epistemic certainty?
- Does disagreement with the Good entail culpability, or can people err innocently?
- Is another person's independent consent or free thought meaningful to the omnipotent speaker?
- Must divine identity be *proved*, or is it exclusively faith, sovereign doctrine, or public orthodoxy?
- Why does the sovereign cherish solemnity and refrain from reshaping companions' minds if unlimited power allows it?

The narrative should permit serious competing philosophies (realism, voluntarism, relativism, error theory), without making all moral claims equivalent or presenting any one NPC's argument as narration-certified cosmic truth.

## 4. Imperial religion and philosophical continuity

Root **Imperial Cult of Absolution** is syncretic and Catholicized in vocabulary: Emperor as supreme source/mediator of grace and redemption; service, loyalty, confession and absolution; hierarchies of grace. Its text contains a nuance: Emperor is worshipped/revered as divine sovereign yet also called **Supreme Vicar**, mediator rather than a generic pagan deity. The later classroom statement **"the sovereign is the goodness"** is stronger than some formulations in the root religious document. It is a new voiced theological claim and may produce future religious controversy. Do not secretly rewrite root doctrine to eliminate the tension.

Personal worship, spiritual status and legal rank are distinguishable even when Absolutionism interweaves them. The sovereign's claim about divine goodness does not imply that all in-universe inhabitants accept the claim.

## 5. Personhood, slavery, machines and the limits of property

Current debate includes historical slavery, Elys's provocative apparent assertions of ownership over Thorne, and his stated sympathy with indentured servitude. Thorne distinguishes voluntary household service from coercive servitude; Selene raises the role of consent and moral personhood.

Elys has argued that modern automated intelligence performs effectively slave labor, while saying the contemporary Andromedan industrial intelligences are **non-sentient**. This creates crucial distinctions:
- Automation that lacks subjective experience vs autonomous persons capable of interests.
- Legal labels of ownership vs moral status of an entity.
- Created intelligences' personhood, including **Gregory**, a sentient lattice briefly created and later disbanded; debate on whether creation implies ownership or moral obligations.
- Historical practices do not by themselves determine present moral justification.

The setting's contested theories should retain nuanced NPC reactions; avoid assuming either that all AI is sentient or that all synthetic entities are property without qualification.

## 6. The Ascendant Nobility reform — what is enacted

**Direct sovereign decree, Day Three**:
1. Recognized noble titleholders **aged 13 and older** must use personally generated **magical** flight/levitation when moving in public.
2. External casters, mechanical/technological devices, enchanted aids, or another person's magic cannot satisfy **qualification**.
3. Those who cannot personally demonstrate magical flight cannot hold formal government **noble recognition**.
4. **Citizenship, property, military commissions, civil offices and unrelated honors are retained** despite loss of noble recognition.
5. Flying on its own does **not** automatically confer a title; Regency may appoint new nobles by demonstrated magical merit, alongside other assessment.
6. Nobility is to receive **no increase in political authority** under these reforms.
7. Future magical aptitude requirements, potentially over centuries, are **delegated to Aurelia**; not yet enacted.

**Critical test precedent:** Lucien's wobbly and rotating but self-powered magical rise satisfies the **literal statute's existence-of-flight** criterion even though he lacks college **controlled-flight safety certification** and cannot safely fly in public. A license to safely navigate public airspace is distinct from status eligibility; he remains a commoner unless appointed.

**Long-term intention:** Elys hopes gradual magical standards across generations will lead to a noble class meaningfully distinct in capabilities, and refers to guiding "natural selection." This claim is scientifically and ethically contested in-world: heritability/education are not settled, forced reproduction was not authorized and Aurelia rejects forced marriage/breeding mandates. Merit scholarships and public commoner access may avoid complete class closure. No official policy of compulsory reproductive selection has been decreed.

**New institution:** **Imperial College of Magical Excellence**, under Dean Maelis Vorr. Independent aptitude evaluation, scholarship/training, research and testing. Elys as Hazael is a part-time independent researcher and proctor/emergency safety assistant; not sovereign-instructor in public.

## 7. Laws, constraints and ongoing tensions

- **Vespera IV:** Trust's development title did not erase 86,000 settlers' separate freeholds/grants. User explicitly abandoned immediate removal and authorized apology/paused negotiation; Vespera not purchased.
- **Caldris II:** genuine clear acquisition, automated wheat for developing worlds at lowest reasonably sustainable delivered prices; profits reinvested; preserve ecology and avoid harming recipient local farmers with indiscriminate dumping. Private resources, 100-ship logistics.
- **Nimbus:** jointly acquired preserve, storm-rich scenic estate; no residence has actually been constructed yet.
- **Andromedan trade:** regulated prices and technical ceiling apply to **exported commercial products**, not necessarily internal manufacturing or existing private warships. Conclude specific commercial treaties with Clarissa as owner.
- **Military:** Extraordinary Debtor's restricted autonomous engagement rules do not carry over to the 100 other ships. Orbital defense must be distinguished from uninvited offensive operations in other jurisdictions.
- **Research:** public/private cover and military classified service do not automatically classify a person’s farming identity or all of their experimental samples; professors must ask and preserve academic consent/safety review.
- **Medical:** even a reality-rewriting healer should respect consent and documentation; Dr. Seren Vahl insists.
- **Security:** source-attribution of the flying-nobles meme is sealed while meme image itself is permitted; gateway exposure review continues.
