## Canon-confidence protocol

- **Established root archive:** foundational history, entities, and in-universe descriptions from original repository files. Some root documents describe an *earlier status quo*, such as Sentinel still being under construction and the Empire still reintegrating systems.
- **Played events:** actions, decisions, and outcomes explicitly established within the ongoing academy-era CYOA. These update the **current story state**, not the historical truth of earlier periods.
- **In-universe view:** a character's theology, propaganda, ethical judgment, or theory may not be the narrator's objective metaphysical truth.
- **Proposal vs enactment:** pending purchases, promised reforms, brainstorms and uncompleted negotiations must not be represented as completed. Some AI/narrator reports are preliminary estimates, not proof.
- **Retcon:** user's express corrections override narrator slips. Specifically, the Emperor's public history was **never rewritten** into a turnip- or wheat-farmer story; only the undercover persona's records changed. The current cover name is **Hazael Wheatley**, not Hazael Somnion.
- **Authorial agency:** the player exclusively decides Elys's actions and speech. Narrator controls NPCs and world. Do not invent player dialogue, especially during transitions. Elys is effectively omnipotent but not omniscient and need not be a natural strategist.

**Latest status supersedes the old WAC endpoint below:** By **Chapter XLVII**, Elys/Hazael has a germinating Caldris wheat field, established **Wheatley Storage & Shipping** with AIs **Meridian, Ledger, Granary, Steward** and construction commenced on a 22-ship civilian fleet/storage facilities, reserved **SMITE**, discussed (but not incorporated) **Apophatic Technologies**, incorporated **Somnus**, and appointed **Lethe** as holding-company overseer. The latest scene ends in Lethe's joke about forgetting. Read [Post-WAC Story](POST_WAC_STORY_AND_OPERATIONS.md) and [Corporate Ledger](SOMNUS_CORPORATE_PORTFOLIO.md) first for the most recent canonical status.

Elysomnion (Elys), ancient sovereign of the **Black and Emerald Empire** (earlier documents often use *Galactic Empire*), has resumed activity after cycles of slumber. He delegates ordinary governance to **Regent Aurelia** while attending higher education undercover as **Hazael Wheatley**, nominally an agricultural student/farmer and now an **independent research fellow** at the Imperial College of Magical Excellence. His fiancée **Clarissa Somnion** owns **Andromeda** outright and shares ownership of **Nimbus** with him. His personal estate owns **Caldris II**, transformed into a vast wheat-production project with a hundred advanced Andromedan vessels and an orbital shipyard. The last scene features his magic-engineered adaptive floor material **WAC-01**, its proposed defensive applications and incessant wordplay (WAC Attack, WAC-1-OFF).

## Known contradictions requiring care

- Root [Empire History](../Empire_History.md) describes a 3,000-year slumber and early reconsolidation; later narrative states final war has ended and Aurelia is preparing its announcement. Treat these as successive periods, not silently overwrite historical timeline.
- Root [Imperial Religion](../Imperial_Religion.md) calls the sovereign both divine source/conduit and Supreme Vicar; classroom discussions press stronger claims that the Emperor **is** the Good. Record that as **Elys's stated doctrine**, with philosophical objections unresolved.
- Root [Sentinel](../Sentinel.md) describes design/development; later scenes show operational Sentinel. This is a chronological advance.
- Historical **Andromedan Dominion** listed among former powers in [Known Governments](../Known_Governments.md) and the contemporary largely automated **Andromedan private dominion** need not be identical in governance or period; do not assume seamless continuity without further exposition.
- Original archive presents Elys as strategically capable; academy story explicitly establishes he is **not a strategist by nature** and often improvises, notwithstanding extraordinary intelligence/power and his historical record.
- Earlier passages refer to **Hazael Somnion**, an affluent sovereign cover; current registered civilian identity is **Hazael Wheatley**. Historical archive on Emperor Elysomnion untouched.

## Newly established Imperial Hand — Veronica

[Veronica](../Veronica.md), the violet-haired immortal **Head of Imperial Intelligence and Interrogation**, has been added to the core archive. Her distinctive fifteen tentacles and touch-mediated compulsion make her a formidable, ethically fraught interrogator. She has a restrained, lightly yandere-like devotion to Elysomnion and a possible source of tension with Clarissa. **Later-story update:** Veronica subsequently interrogated Lucien about the notification leak; she remains in intelligence office after a brief, theatrical banishment was reversed, and no restraining order was enacted. Earlier pre-introduction scenes remain unchanged.
