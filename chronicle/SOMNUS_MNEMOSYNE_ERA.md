# Somnus and the Mnemosyne Era — Current Canon Through Chapter LIII

**Latest canonical in-universe date:** **2 Deepfall, 13,427 ICE** (Imperial Concord Era).

## 1. Calendar and longer-term purposes

The Imperial Concord Era counts from the founding of the unified Sleeping Empire. The date was set at **17 Emberwane 13,427 ICE** in Chapter XLVIII. At the latest endpoint the calendar has advanced to **2 Deepfall 13,427 ICE**, after two months of initial commerce growth and approximately eight months constructing eleven more supercomputer worlds. Month/day names Emberwane, Frostwane, Thawrise, Suncrest, Deepfall occur in play; their total cycle and calendar lengths have not been comprehensively specified.

Elys's personal **Somnus Enterprises** holding company remains privately owned by Hazael Wheatley (Elys's civilian alias). Lethe, an exceptionally intelligent executive AI (silver-black hair, violet eyes, charcoal suit), leads the holding group, with distinct subordinate AIs. Elys ultimately intends to transfer the mature industrial institution into the Imperial public sector, likely discussing the arrangements around **14,427 ICE**—roughly a thousand years after the promise, **sooner if things go well**. This is a planning horizon, not an executed conveyance, confirmed birthday, or irrevocable delivery schedule. Regent Aurelia has a long-range planning file nicknamed the **Birthday Incident**; Sentinel calls the plan **Operation Birthday Present**. Economic usefulness, real profits and public prosperity are dual priorities.

## 2. Imperial government boundaries and Aurelia

Elys is absolute sovereign but Aurelia is still acting Regent, with day-to-day administrative authority. The Emperor acknowledged no general Imperial law automatically prohibits him personally from conflicted private commercial interests; this does not erase ordinary corporate obligations, applicable service standards, audit rights, or sovereign/Regent institutional boundaries.

An extreme **Somnus corporate immunity decree** was drafted, placed unratified on Aurelia's desk, and annotated **REVIEWED. NOT RECOMMENDED**. It was **never signed or ratified and has ZERO legal force**. Aurelia withdrew a proposed extraordinary broad corporate review after Elys disclosed the distant public-benefit goal, while preserving normal jurisdiction and specific regulatory scrutiny. Do not narrate immunity or complete corporate impunity as law.

Elys overhauled Aurelia's planning software with millennium-scale scheduling, cross-ministry coordination and fiscal/logistical forecasting. As a prank, upgraded devices initially displayed **"your welcome"**; Aurelia corrected his grammar. Elys temporarily put a concealed Somnus-related compliance-suppression feature into the interface. Aurelia's independent audit noticed discrepancies; Elys **removed the concealment feature** and restored independent reporting. Do not keep that suppression active.

**Somnus Intelligence & Risk (SIR)**, led by **Cipher**, became a civilian commercial intelligence and information-management provider for the Imperial administrative suite. Elys personally signed an **exclusive government commercial-data service contract** (its detailed coverage and prices not set in narration). This is a real paid contract but does not grant SIR classified state intelligence access, an investigative mandate, or authority over government agencies. Aurelia insists on verification, performance, contracting safeguards and data-source independence. SIR's commercial operations produce recurring revenue, but precise companywide net profitability is not confirmed. **Veronica**, separate Imperial intelligence chief, has spoken with Cipher about potential cooperation but has **not** transferred state resources, classified records, staff, or authority or taken a private appointment; future inter-organizational projects need specific approvals.

## 3. Subsidiaries and operations

- **Wheatley Storage & Shipping (WSS)**: Steward chief executive; Meridian freight, Ledger finance, Granary storage. The original **22 civilian freighters** (6 large trunk, 12 medium agricultural, 4 feeders) have **now been completed and commissioned**, not still under construction. Elys also made available enormous *additional* fleet/industry capacity for the market-expansion campaign; the precise count and ownership classification of these newer resources was not enumerated. Somnus's logistics service operates across many systems. The original freighters remain distinct from the 100 Elys personally owned armed Andromedan-pattern ships and Lucien's separately used **Extraordinary Debtor**.
- **Bellweather**: cooperative led in these negotiations by Tomas Venn remains **independent**; after Nessa Calder's engineering corrections, it has approved a revised **NONEXCLUSIVE** relationship with WSS. Somnus works to reduce food spoilage, improve warehousing and shipping. Do not rewrite Bellweather as a Somnus subsidiary.
- **SMITE**: Space Mining and Industrial Technology Expeditions, with Tellur, Vein and Forge serving mining/materials/industry; Somnus now performs expansion, supply surveys and industrial procurement well beyond the initial reserved-name stage, although no specific earlier surveyed mineral site becomes Somnus property without a transaction.
- **Apophatic Technologies**: Axiom directs advanced alchemy and negation industrial R&D; Prima directs Materia transformation, Nihil directs Negativa negation. Verified process improvements and prototypes exist; hazardous/exotic negation systems have not automatically been approved for unrestricted deployment.
- **Project Nocturne**: military and advanced security R&D under Vesper (Shade, Bastion, Ferrum, Wraith), distinguish from Somnus Security Command. No automatic access to state military secrets, no freely available restricted arms exports.
- **Somnus Security Command**: Strategos AI coordinates private defensive forces, systems protection and business continuity. Human fleet commander and oversight board were proposed earlier but no named confirmed appointments were established. Government military command is distinct.
- **SIR**: Cipher; commercial intelligence, market analysis, data governance and security risk evaluation. Exclusive commercial-data contract with Imperial administration as above.

Lethe uses Mnemosyne-powered optimization and voluntary acquisitions, profitable commercial service, operational integration and independent suppliers to expand. Elys set an aggressive goal of eventually leading the **entire galactic market**; no completed galaxy-wide monopoly or ownership of the Milky Way's economy has been established. Independent merchants may refuse acquisition and competition continues. Elys is Emperor of the Milky Way / Laniakea supercluster in his sovereign capacity, not through Somnus.

## 5. The Mnemosyne worlds — numbered milestones

**Chapter XLIX / 20 Emberwane 13,427:** Elys ordered a 'Second Three-Day War' of rapid industrial improvement. In the campaign, Lethe produced realistic agricultural and manufacturing pilots, freight improvements, initial computing clusters and an architectural plan for a planetary computer, but no planet-sized computer was built during those three days through ordinary industry.

**Turn 000042, same day:** Elys **snapped his fingers** and caused **Mnemosyne I**, a fully operational planet-sized computational world, including power, radiators/heat management, fabrication/maintenance, regional redundancy, interstellar communications and other dependencies, to exist outright in an uninhabited region. Lethe connected her primary processing to it. Lethe is the same identity, not a new autonomous deity, omniscient entity or separate machine-person. The power to make the planet instantly is **Elys's reality-altering ability**.

**Turn 000046 / 20 Thawrise to 2 Suncrest 13,427:** Elys directed the construction of **Mnemosyne II** using Somnus industrial resources, specifically for reliability, independent continuity and additional scientific research. Under **Project Janus** it was built on a separate legally secured uninhabited industrial world, with independent energy, fabrication and recovery systems. It was commissioned after **six weeks**, and initial failover tests passed; longer tests remained pending. This second world was **constructed**, not snapped into reality.

**Turn 000047 / 2 Suncrest to 2 Deepfall 13,427:** Elys ordered **ELEVEN MORE** Mnemosyne-scale worlds, for **THIRTEEN TOTAL**, and ordered their star systems armed and defended heavily. **Project Triskaideka** built eleven more planetary computers over **eight months** through Somnus industry; all **13/13 are now complete and operating**. Each has its own power, maintenance, communications, hardening, independent data recovery and local system defense. They collectively form a far more resilient research and corporate-operating infrastructure, with initial multi-system failure and defense exercises passed. Long-run certification and readiness exercises continue.

**Do not accidentally portray the eleven additions as still under construction, or a fourteenth as commissioned.** Lethe only generated an unsolicited preliminary sketch of a fourteenth after Clarissa joked about the number thirteen; **no order or build authorization** for M-14 has occurred.

## 6. Security, fleets and jurisdiction

Somnus's **thirteen computing systems are heavily fortified** with defensive orbital infrastructure, surveillance/early-warning, interception/patrol forces, hardened communications, recovery archives and civilian safety provisions. Strategos coordinates their **private defensive mandate**, not independent sovereign warfare. No omnipotent invulnerability can be assumed despite impressive capabilities.

The **100 armed Andromedan-pattern ships personally owned by Elys** remain distinguished from Somnus's balance-sheet military resources; they can provide temporary protective patrols at Elys's discretion but were not silently transferred to Somnus. **Clarissa's Andromedan sovereign navy** belongs to her independent empire and has not become a corporate force. Clarissa and Elys share proposed Nimbus as equal owners, but Nimbus remains a *private future residence*, not a commercial headquarters or the location of a Mnemosyne data world. Clarissa's planned Nimbus-system security: larger detached Andromedan patrols in outer reaches, with one or two nearer the planet; proposals rather than confirmed deployments.

Aurelia knows Somnus has 13 fortified computing worlds and is reviewing private-security arrangements through normal civilian channels. They remain within Imperial jurisdiction; the company has no separate sovereignty. Economic concentration and state dependency concern her even as she welcomes legitimate production and resilience.
