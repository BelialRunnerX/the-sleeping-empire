**Canon through:** Chapter XLVII, immediately following Lethe's confirmation that she retains the restructuring instructions.  
**Public civilian owner:** **Hazael Wheatley** (the undercover legal identity of Emperor Elysomnion).  
**Reading note:** This file is the authoritative **current** business/portfolio supplement to [Technology and Holdings](TECHNOLOGY_ECONOMY_AND_LOCATIONS.md). Historical claims in older files remain valid *for their earlier scene dates*, but may be superseded below.

## 1. Founding and organization

The player first ordered Sentinel to establish **Wheatley Storage & Shipping (WSS)**, then reserved a mining name **SMITE**, proposed **Apophatic Technologies**, chose the single-word holding-company name **SOMNUS**, and appointed executive AI **LETHE**. The player subsequently instructed Lethe to merge **his personal holdings** into Somnus, then praised her work with the pun *"now just don't forget it!"* Lethe answered *"Forget what?"*, later *"Who are you again?"*—both jokes. She has not genuinely lost the asset records.

**Somnus** is **incorporated**, under Hazael Wheatley's civilian identity, initially with him as **100% sole shareholder**. It is a privately owned holding company, not a governmental ministry and not a merger of the Empire with private enterprise. Executive overseer: **Lethe**. Her mission is to coordinate investment, subsidiaries, capital allocation, strategic risk and reporting while minimizing daily involvement by Hazael. She is permission-bounded, audited, and must escalate material deals and changes. She must **not** silently sign away the emperor's public assets or Clarissa's assets.

An earlier company **Wheatley Agricultural Holdings** operated the farming interest and was the initial parent of WSS; Somnus now plans/conducts restructuring at the holding-company level. The **"Somnus Agriculture"** and **"Somnus Industrial Holdings"** labels appeared as a **proposed group chart**. Do not assert those separately named entities are already independently incorporated unless a later event confirms it.

**Current transfers:** Somnus's incorporation and Lethe's appointment are complete. Asset transfer filings for straightforward personal commercial property are **initiated / pending clearance**, not all completed. There must be an auditable ownership ledger, share consideration and separation of liabilities. The user ordered consolidation, but no narrator should retroactively declare every asset automatically vested. Legal and co-owner consent issues remain real.

## 3. WSS — actual operating mandate

**Name:** Wheatley Storage & Shipping (WSS). **Business:** commercially priced warehousing, grain preservation, handling, freight logistics, reserve management, exchange listings. Its official exchange registration permits **grain-market access, freight listings, storage listings and bid/forward inquiry**. **Physical delivery authorization/capacity certification remains pending** pending usable terminals and freighters. Do not invent first delivered shipments or completed profitable quarters.

The player explicitly authorized: *"Sentinel get me a freight manager ai, an account manager, a storage manager, and a company manager. Wheatley Storage and Shipping is now in business. Build out the infrastructure."* Sentinel executed **company establishment, staffing, capital authorization, shipbuilding and construction mobilization**. **Company has begun operating administratively; major new infrastructure is NOT yet commissioned.** Weekly progress reported on schedule.

**Four corporate AIs, roles and voice:**

- **MERIDIAN** — freight operations director: ship routes, schedules, capacity, bulk/feeder planning, vessel maintenance, port coordination, efficiency; dry and operationally precise. *"A vessel that arrives precisely when promised is more useful than one that arrives spectacularly."*
- **LEDGER** — finance and accounts director: payroll, invoices, tax/compliance, budgets, independent financial audits, cost overruns. Skeptical of Elys starting ventures before the first company's balance sheet. She is under WSS management, **not** the head of all Somnus.
- **GRANARY** — storage operations director: grain condition, monitoring, pests, moisture, segregation, terminal safety and facility maintenance. Will keep existing Bellweather silos working where safe during retrofits.
- **STEWARD** — WSS chief executive: coordinates the three functional directors, customer service, hiring, routine contracting and monthly WSS reporting; ordinary decisions delegated, major spending elevated.

**LETHE** manages **Somnus** above WSS/other controlled interests. **Sentinel** is the Emperor's independent Imperial guardian, not a WSS employee or substitute CEO. AI systems have audited scopes/permissions and financial transparency; do not automatically assert their full sentience or infer legal personhood status from their personae.

### Fleet and facilities — ordered versus delivered

- Initial **22 purpose-built civilian vessels ordered**: **6 large trunk-route bulk carriers, 12 medium agricultural freighters, 4 feeder ships**. Keel/structural fabrication has begun at Caldris shipyard; stage deliveries, route testing, certifications still to occur.
- Caldris II **initial export terminal** under construction; **regional agricultural storage hubs** phased, future scale depends on true crop volumes.
- Bellweather terminal **retrofit** in engineering review, with local maintenance engineer **Nessa Calder** providing **17 corrections**, accessible panels and compatibility with existing systems; existing terminal should remain in service through the retrofit if safe.
- Bellweather storage **initial expansion target: 60,000 additional tonnes**, modular and conditional on design, survey and performance; **not yet built**.
- Technical components: condition-controlled silos with moisture/ventilation/temp/contamination control; compartmentalized inventory; standardized sealed intermodal grain containers; loaders, weighing and conveyance, dust safety; commercial-grade civilian freight. Supply design data for **Caldris + Bellweather**, not an unexplained instant planet-wide complete network.
- **100 war-capable Andromedan ships** are separate security fleet, NOT the 22 civilian freighters. Routine grain movement should not use expensive warships unless specifically justified. Civilian commercial technologies must stay at or below the level permitted by the established **Andromedan commercial technology export ceiling**.

### Bellweather cooperative — customer and independent partner

**Tomas Venn** represents around **4,000 independent farming households on three agricultural worlds**, serving eleven developing markets (per his estimates). He worries Caldris low-priced mass wheat will bankrupt local producers even as it aids consumers. He proposes a **nonexclusive** relationship: use his co-op's existing farms, mills and distribution networks and Caldris/WSS's storage, reserves, efficient freight and supplemental grain.

At the visited terminal: **six old silos, 72,000 tonnes usable capacity, 68,400 tonnes inventory, last season's grain losses of 8.2%, average 11-day off-world dispatch delays** (all supplied cooperative figures, not independent audits). Nessa reports inconsistent container couplings, difficult maintenance and multi-hour transfer delays.

**Clarissa** was expressly delegated by Elys to **negotiate service pricing for Bellweather after the operational plans**, not to surrender co-op independence. She negotiated initial terms with Tomas and Nessa:
- WSS retains ownership of newly financed facilities; Bellweather retains its farms, mills, employees and commercial autonomy.
- Nonexclusive service; transparent storage costs plus reasonable margin; freight by volume/route with adjustable fuel costs; annual review and impartial dispute process.
- Reasonable reserved capacity, no unlimited take-or-pay, no fabricated guarantees of profit; local maintenance and training.
- **Draft framework / provisional understanding exists; final tariffs, signatures, delivery and performance remain pending** Ledger's cost validation and capacity certificates.

Trade philosophy: reduce actual spoilage and freight inefficiency instead of destroying local farming through dumped grain, or propping it up via unsustainable inflated consumer prices. WSS aims to be profitable; deliberate subsidy must be classified as subsidy in its books, not falsely reported as returns. Early earnings are proposed for reinvestment; no actual profitable year has occurred.

## 4. SMITE and Apophatic — not operational

**SMITE** stands for **Space Mining and Industrial Technology Expeditions**. Sentinel secured the **name reservation and prospective mark**; no mining rights, mining vessels, operating corporation or capital commitments. Elys joked about seizing the means of production via low-grade asteroid alchemy and a monopoly; this **was a joke**, not a state decree, executed monopoly or proven commercially viable plan.

**Apophatic Technologies** was Elys's preferred concept for alchemy and negation: apophatic refers to knowing/expressing something through **negation**. Other brainstorms included Via Negativa Industries, Prima Materia, Athanor Industries, Null Principle, Kenosis, but **Apophatic Technologies has not been reserved or incorporated**. Proposed research divisions:
- **Materia** — alchemy, transmutation, physical-stable materials, industrial refinement.
- **Negativa** — magical negation, nullification, containment and suppression.

The concept of chaining SMITE mining → Apophatic refinement → WSS distribution is a **strategic possibility**, not established current operations, proved cost advantage or authorized industry monopoly.

## 5. Somnus governance, safeguards and unanswered strategy

Elys expressly chose **"Somnus"** (single word, not "Somnus Enterprises") with **Lethe** executive overseer. Lethe proposed group-level management, independently auditable accounts, capital discipline, separation of private and sovereign property, legal examination of restricted vessels, co-owner consent on Nimbus, and a **noncorporate farmhouse and small field**. Elys praised the plan as **"perfect"** and ordered her not to forget it. She made Lethe-name jokes, while reassuring him that records are redundant, immutable/auditable in-world logs and no pending transfers will be marked complete.

Lethe's open question was whether Somnus should optimize **maximum sustainable commercial return** or also reward **community benefit, employment and long-term economic stability**. **The user has not answered** that choice explicitly. Do not silently make a binding doctrine from her question; Bellweather's service model prizes sustainable returns and local resilience but does not settle all subsidiaries' investment policy.
