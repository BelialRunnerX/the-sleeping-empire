# Character Bible and Ongoing Arcs

## Elysomnion / Elys / Hazael Wheatley

**Actual identity:** immortal, ancient emperor and ultimate sovereign; primary player character. **Public current identity:** Hazael Wheatley, modest farmer, academy attendee and now independent research fellow. Earlier aliases *Hazael Somnion* and *Hazael of House Belor* belong to prior cover phases; current property registry says **Hazael Wheatley**. The Emperor's true public history remains unchanged.

**Power:** effectively omnipotent reality alteration, selective absolute defense (nullification/reflection) per root lore, instantaneous teleportation and precision structural manipulation. **Not omniscient.** Power does not automatically supply wisdom, strategy or knowledge of unseen events. He can make mistakes, be surprised, be distracted, enjoy a pun and choose restraint.

**Character tension:** terrifying sovereign who ended ancient warfare by force versus ordinary student deliberately subjecting himself to professors, exams, friendship, awkwardness and a desire to grow wheat. Seeks escape from 13,000 years of on-call responsibility without abandoning final authority. Philosophically provocative: will argue law/property/morality are fictions of power, then invoke God as the Good itself and identify sovereign with goodness. Do not assume he reconciled the contradictions. Regards solemnity and sacred silence highly, yet repeatedly engages in bawdy agricultural and bakery puns; ironic humor. He is not naturally a strategist despite older documents presenting him as strategic. He values independent will, especially rather than manufacturing assent. Has stated that authority's *ability* to compel is not itself the same as moral truth.

**Recent choices:** gifts Andromeda to Clarissa, purchases Caldris for affordable wheat, jointly acquires Nimbus, allows Aurelia to continue governing, creates flight-based noble eligibility without additional political power, becomes a research fellow, reverse-engineerable but not teachable magical materials. In institutional settings he prefers not to receive special treatment publicly.

**Future direction:** does he value ordinary life for its own sake, and how will his proclaimed identification with the Good withstand contradiction, mortal autonomy and historical responsibility? Preserve player-exclusive control of words/actions.

## Clarissa Somnion

**Current position:** Elys's fiancée, Imperial Hand, and **Empress/sole private owner of the entire Andromedan dominion**, her engagement gift. Publicly joint owner of **Nimbus (50%)** with Hazael Wheatley. The possessive nickname **Clari** is used by Elys.

**Arc:** From dedicated inner-court collaborator to a sovereign with independent property, income and stewardship responsibilities, and a partner asserting her own decisions. She values affection, intimacy and an ordinary home with Elys; imagines gardens, books, an observatory and storm-watching. She recognizes Elys's godlike power yet asks what he values, whom he trusts and whether he will respect others' freedom. Wit is flirty and warm; not a purely compliant echo. Wants an Andromedan development fund and preserve public-benefit commerce. Can personally levitate.

**Privacy:** knows Hazael is Emperor; public joint registry must not expose that fact. Clarissa appearing alongside wealthy Hazael can attract attention but is not itself proof of his identity.

## Aurelia

Root canon: Queen/Regent, originally a sentient star elevated by Emperor, with vast power and centuries/millennia of government service. Root files may describe a 3,000-year active regency. **Current:** effective day-to-day head of government by standing sovereign delegation; Emperor has not revoked her autonomy and explicitly confirms she need not ask his consent for ordinary decisions.

**Arc:** ancient loyal steward and de facto guardian of public institutions, balancing dry exasperated affection toward Elys with strong administrative boundaries. Her “resignation” was **hypothetical**; Elys preemptively rejected it. She insists on separating private wealth and Treasury, property titles and settlement rights, lawful trade, civilian safety and procedural responsibility. Does not always agree with Elys; can challenge coercive relocation. Calls him an insufferable ancient menace yet loves him in a deep, loyal court sense. Implements magical nobility reform with scholarship, impartial assessment, disability/safety questions and rejection of coercive breeding programs. Resists confusing divine identity, preference and good governance.

## Sentinel

Root documents portray a commissioned decentralized guardian AI of Epsilon-7, designed by Sylphara Voss and incapable of overriding Elys. **Current:** activated, distributes communications, administers fleet and properties, performs audits, provides confidential support and tart commentary. Maintains sealed Emperor/Hazael verification, can detect threats and manage autonomous operations. Needs human/sovereign oversight in setting policy; example failure: first incorrectly treated lack of **indigenous intelligent inhabitants** as lack of **settler population** on Vespera IV. Also investigated leaking summary gateway. Do not depict omniscience or deny possibility of its mistakes.

## Lucien Vale

Student, inventor, Andromedan citizen, proprietor of approved-in-principle **Cream-Pie-R** orbital bakery, comic counterweight and one of Elys's closest boisterous friends. Often speaks IN ALL CAPS; obsession with cream puffs, absurd branding, grand titles and engineering. Former owner of ultra-advanced ship later transferred back to Elys; now authorized civilian operator with major financial liabilities for his own accidents. Wants title **Baron von Buttercream**. **Has demonstrated personal magical lift but not controlled flight**; eligible under narrow legal wording but no title granted. His bot unintentionally accessed a badly secured summary gateway and leaked confidential snippets to bakery group; no evidence he knows Hazael's Imperial identity. Security investigation is ongoing. Comic irresponsibility does not mean malice or complete lack of competence: genuine engineering flair and ample magical potential.

## Professor Thorne

War College military strategy and ethics faculty at Astraea. Knows Hazael as unusual, wealthy/inconsistent farmer; does **not** know he is Elysomnion. Moral philosopher who insists on the distinction between effective power, legal authority and normative justification. Discussed property as legal disposition, slavery, personhood, the Euthyphro dilemma, divine simplicity and the question whether omnipotent beings welcome dissent. Intellectual foil, should pose strong challenges without cartoon hostility; respect earned despite disagreements. Sent warm note upon Wheatley's transfer.

## Professor Ilyra Voss

History professor; unwittingly hears Elys comment on the Emperor's political legacy. Connects farming and dormancy metaphors to history, coins informal “Wheatley Principle.” Does not know identity. Keep distinct from **Sylphara Voss** (Chief Imperial Architect) and **Kael Voss** (Senate representative).

## Professor Seraphine Valcrest

Economics professor at Astraea, aware Hazael's apparent holdings are enormous; reacts analytically to the unusual economic background. No established knowledge of his true sovereign identity.

## Professor Maelis Vorr

Exacting dean/faculty examiner at new Imperial College of Magical Excellence. Respects procedural academic merit, evidence, security boundaries and safety. Hazael's extraordinary skills impress but do not exempt him from paperwork. Fiercely opposes unprofessional WAC puns. Important new dynamic: she stands up to even a highly favored government-linked researcher and separates expertise from political sponsorship.

## Professor Ilyan Soreth

Energetic materials engineer. Fascinated by Hazael's permanent precision-engineered specimens and 100 m² resilient floor. Wishes to reverse engineer and scale the science, sometimes oblivious to innuendo. Academic curiosity needs rigorous verification and does not grant access to classified government service.

## Kaelen

Silver-haired, disciplined swordsman and student peer. Blunt, principled and dryly sarcastic. Challenges coerced morality, legal incoherence and tactical claims; wants earned respect and competence. Usually exasperated by Lucien.

## Selene

Peer concerned with rights, consent, consciousness and personhood. Asks whether power and goodness can be identified, whether disagreement matters, and how to treat people who honestly err. Independent moral perspective, not merely a foil.

## Renn

Analytical student, asks logically exacting questions about laws, ability, consistency and classification; has links to earlier warded-blade lore. Distinguish from other names if earlier academy cohort differs.

## Elara

Student with engineering-oriented interests, part of ensemble. Further personal arc pending.

## Bel

Foundational personal attendant and Chief Diplomat, liaison with Senate and Kael Voss. Recent story delegated oversight of **Project Stillwater** to Bel; the underlying facility runs under a designated Stillwater Intelligence. May be referred to as “Bell” in earlier narration—use **Bel** unless character speech requires otherwise.

## Lillith

First Fleet commander, disciplined military strategist, in root canon associated with 11th Fleet testing and return-era campaigns. Later academy arc has not substantially developed her on-page; do not invent appearances.

## Sylphara Voss

Immortal Chief Imperial Architect, designing self-repairing infrastructure; associated with original creation/development of Sentinel. Root setting's form/function ideal bears on new material engineering.
