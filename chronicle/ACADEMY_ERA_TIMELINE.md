# Historical and Narrative Chronology

Dates are **in-universe relative** unless specified. Story timestamps use local/Capital Standard Time; the academic sequence is presented as Chapters XXXIV–XXXVIII and Days Two–Four where recorded. Exact chronology of some older pre-academy adventures is not established; do not invent Gregorian dates.

## Foundation and ancient Empire (root archive)

- **Year 0: Three-Day Unification/Conquest.** Elysomnion ends generations of galactic war by conquering the galaxy in three days. Millions die; the moral and political legacy remains contested. He intends to stop cycles of war rather than govern every moment of individual life. The political settlement includes strong central ultimate authority and considerable local self-government.
- **Years 1–100:** Citadel on Epsilon-7 and Nullspire established, other core Imperial architecture and institutions begin.
- **Years 100–500:** consolidation, first periodic imperial slumbers.
- **Year 2000:** Senate institutionalized to represent member states.
- **Years 2000–5000:** golden age; 11th Fleet with member-species participation; trade authorities develop.
- **Years 5000–13000:** extended sleeping periods, corruption, division and Great Fracture; Senate weakens/collapses, 11th Fleet disbands; regions become independent or form rival alignments (Druun, Veylari, Korrath etc.). Root timeline says reawakening at Year 13000 after a 3,000-year sleep; other much older sleeping cycles existed.
- **Return:** Elys awakens, with Aurelia steering Imperial governance; initial era of reconsolidation and search for ancient personnel (Vespera, Kragar, Sylphara) described in root documentation. Later academy-era story proceeds beyond this snapshot.

## Developments before and around the academy

- **Court and sovereignty:** Regent Aurelia exercises operational government by standing delegation; the Emperor preserves ultimate, revocable authority. His slumbers do **not** mean he was never on call. Elys insists he has had no real day off in ~13,000 years.
- **Final War:** combat is concluded by the academy era. Aurelia prepares the formal announcement at the **Festival of Sevenfold Unity**, including a gigantic seven-layer, roughly 3,200-km-wide cake distribution celebration. Do not assume the public announcement happened; it remained pending in latest related context.
- **Undercover enrollment:** Elys secretly enrolls at **Astraea Collegium** to experience academic life as a peer. Older alternate names include **Hazael**, **Hazael of House Belor**, **Elys of House Somnion**, and **Hazael Somnion**; he finally adopts cover **Hazael Wheatley**, an apparently humble farmer. Peers can remember former public identities; registry changes do not automatically erase memories. Only a small trusted circle (Aurelia, Clarissa, Sentinel and some inner associates) know the Emperor's secret.
- **Academy academic sequence:** examinations and lessons in governance, economics, history, military strategy and applied magical engineering. In lessons he disputes moral authority, ownership, legitimate force, philosophical realness of goodness and the practical ethics of sovereign power. Faculty generally do **not** know his real identity. Main remembered faculty: Professor Thorne (strategy/ethics), Professor Ilyra Voss (history), Professor Seraphine Valcrest (economics).
- **Academy friends:** Lucien Vale, ingenious and incorrigible pastry enthusiast; Kaelen disciplined swordsman; Renn analytical; Selene ethical/personhood focused; Elara engineering; others including Caelith and Lucien from early arcs. Clarissa and Elys deepen their bond.

## Andromeda and military-commercial upheaval

- Elys reveals extravagant personal holdings across the Milky Way and Laniakea, including trusts, unfinished Dyson structures, unusual observatories, preservation facilities and industrial resources. His **₡1 billion monthly discretionary allocation** comes from private fortune, **not** the Imperial Treasury.
- Elys establishes/demonstrates control over exceptionally advanced Andromedan automation and vessels; operational AIs there are **non-sentient** by his account. The first two sentient Andromedan citizens in the modern arrangement are Hazael and **Lucien Vale**; Clarissa joins with ownership.
- As an engagement present, Elys transfers **the entire Andromedan dominion/galaxy and productive property** to **Clarissa**, now Empress of Andromeda, **as her private asset**, not as a treasury-owned Imperial province. She holds commercial revenue; public-interest trade agreements with the Black and Emerald Empire must be negotiated, not presumed to transfer ownership.
- Lucien receives a terrifyingly advanced ship first called **S.S. Crème Puff**, later **S.S. Debtor**, then **S.S. Extraordinary Debtor**. An imprudent episode involves the ship firing directly at the **Imperial Throne Room**, which remains wholly undamaged; the beam actually struck its impervious target and was not simply deflected by a shield. Aurelia reframes the incident as official **Operation Sovereign Resilience**, with 4,096 authorized drones conducting simulated bombardment.
- Lucien transfers ownership of **S.S. Extraordinary Debtor** back to Hazael/Elys with Lucien maintaining civilian use, lab/workshop and commercial privileges; Elys pays **routine operating costs**, Lucien bears **extraordinary liabilities caused by his use**. The ship's autonomous defense is restricted to non-government aggressors, with passive evasion/protection otherwise; no autonomous combat against government vessels.
- Elys brings **100 additional Andromedan war-capable ships plus a mobile orbital shipyard** to Epsilon-7. Distinguish these from Lucien's now-repossessed ship: the earlier defensive weapons restriction applies to the **Extraordinary Debtor alone**, not to the hundred ships. The hundred can defend planets/shipping when necessary.
- Imperial commerce with Andromeda opens under **fixed/regulatory pricing** for eligible goods and a **commercial export ceiling** excluding advanced products beyond ordinary contemporary Imperial technology; internal manufacturing methods may be more advanced. Actual implementation details are delegated to ministries and Clarissa under agreements.
- A yearly **Festival of the Invincible Crown** is authorized to reenact the Throne Room defense safely, with simulated displays rather than unrestricted live bombardment.
- Lucien gains approval in principle for a regulated **orbital bakery**, later branded **The Cream-Pie-R**; proposed staff title **Official Creamer**; various joke titles including **MasterCream** (NOT on Hazael's student ID).

## Day Two, Chapter XXXIV — Thorne's debate (17:00–~18:40)

- Casual crop/sexual wordplay develops into arguments about legal ownership, property as a juridical fiction, sovereignty and persons, sentient vs non-sentient AIs, slavery/indenture, and descriptive power vs normative morality.
- Elys argues power determines what is possible and questions the objective basis of moral claims; challenges Thorne with an omnipotent being addressing mortals.
- The Euthyphro dilemma sparks his explicit thesis: **neither that things are good because God wills them nor that God wills them because they are independently good, because God IS the Good**.
- He then asserts **'The Emperor is divine'; 'What the Emperor wills is the good'; 'Anything contrary to that is subjective'; 'The sovereign is the goodness.'** Class questions divine simplicity, whether commands can change, epistemic access, moral culpability and why omnipotence would value uncoerced disagreement. Treat identity claims as in-world theology/character position, not independently verified cosmic law.
- After class Elys rejects Aurelia's **hypothetical resignation** despite her having submitted no real resignation. She arranges a 09:00 sovereign administrative meeting.

## Day Three, Chapter XXXV — Regency meeting and planets

- Elys attends the 09:00 meeting (even arrives early), in a farmer's tunic; chooses a levitating plain wooden chair rather than throne-like seating. He reaffirms Aurelia's broad discretion to govern and says he may resume his throne later but not now.
- Approves annual Invincible Crown celebration, Lucien's bakery, Andromedan fixed-price commerce, civilian technological export limitations; reaffirms separated private estates and that **Clarissa owns Andromeda entirely**.
- Announces intention to buy a planet to cultivate vast quantities of wheat using automated systems and his hundred ships as freight/military support, with low sustainable pricing and reinvested profits. Initially pursues **Vespera IV**. The survey incorrectly treated zero indigenous sapients as zero inhabitants; **86,000 settled residents** are revealed. Elys first wants generous buyout/relocation, clashes with Regent and seller, then pauses acquisition, apologizes and opts for an uninhabited world instead. **Vespera was NOT purchased**.
- Survey finalists: **Caldris II** (most reliable wheat), **Eirenhal III** (oceanic aesthetic), **Thalassar Prime** (greatest acreage but powerful storms). Elys chooses **Caldris II** for agricultural production and **Thalassar Prime** as storm-watching private estate.
- Purchases completed, clear title and no settlement claims found. Caldris II is **100% owned by Hazael Wheatley**; Thalassar Prime renamed **Nimbus**, **50% Hazael Wheatley and 50% Clarissa Somnion**. Some earlier narrator screens accidentally used **Hazael Somnion**: explicitly corrected. Emperor's actual identity must remain sealed.
- The 100 vessels and mobile shipyard relocate to **Caldris** to initiate approved orbital, agricultural and logistical infrastructure. Caldris's provisional epithet **The Imperial Plowing Grounds** is a joke; public ownership does not name Elys. Nimbus is a mostly preserved personal retreat with dramatic storms and planned cottage/estate, library, gardens and observatory.
- Elys teleports himself and Clarissa to their future home site on Nimbus; after approximately seven minutes, abruptly returns them to capital due to concern that strong wind might toss **Clarissa** sideways. Their relationship emphasizes care, autonomy, humor and a desire for private peace.

## Day Three, Chapters XXXVI–XXXVII — leaks and nobility

- Lucien repeatedly appears to know confidential conversations. Imperial security traces this to a **misconfigured administrative accessibility-summary gateway** surfaced through his bakery-notification bot, which accessed summary previews; not a direct breach of primary sovereign encryption. Lucien shared some data in a 47-person bakery chat. Gateway disabled, logs quarantined; downstream assessment ongoing; no evidence he learned Hazael's true identity. His **THE_UPPER_CRUST.png** floating-noble meme may circulate unattributed, while its source remains sealed.
- Elys decrees recognized nobles **age 13+ must personally magically levitate/fly in public**; technological aids and others' spells do NOT count. Those unable to do so lose official **noble recognition**, but keep citizenship, private property, military commissions and independent civil offices/honors. Elys says the decorated officer remains an officer, simply not a noble.
- Elys contemplates rising magical standards over successive generations, with natural-selection/bloodline implications. He delegates LONG-TERM policy to Aurelia, confirms **no extra political powers** for nobility, permits new **magical-meritocratic appointments**. Aurelia intends fair examination/scholarship opportunities, rejects coerced reproductive practices. Current compulsory requirement remains personal flight; later criteria NOT enacted.
- Lucien can produce magical lift but at first spins uncontrollably, failing an academic controlled-flight certification; later legal review correctly recognizes he satisfied the narrower **statutory personal-flight criterion**. He is **not yet granted** the title **Baron von Buttercream**; his application remains pending. Public-flight safety certification remains separate.

## Day Four, Chapter XXXVIII — independent researcher and WAC

- Elys elects to transfer from Astraea Collegium to the **Imperial College of Magical Excellence** as **Hazael Wheatley**, independent research fellow who can attend lectures, conduct independent work and assist with examinations/emergency healing. Professor **Maelis Vorr** (dean) insists on independent practical entrance assessment; he passes levitation, multi-object manipulation, energy stability and safety perfectly.
- His original research demonstration is **magically engineered enduring material properties**, not merely temporary enchantment: structural arrangement, fracture control, conductivity, thermal behavior etc. He permits reverse engineering of samples, but cannot presently teach others how to replicate his internal technique. His government service is classified, farming background is genuine and unclassified.
- Professor **Ilyan Soreth**, materials researcher, is delighted by the specimens, while Vorr demands documentation, consent, proper safety and scientific rigor.
- As Lucien's proctor at ~14:43, Elys materializes hundreds of pillows beneath his falling friend. Lucien retains them. Subsequently Elys engineers a **10 m × 10 m** (100 m²) permanent, soft, impact-dissipating, heat/electric/corrosion/transmutation-resistant floor section; mechanism intrinsic to material, no continuous magical support. Tests promising but physical limits still require verification.
- Provisional name **Wheatley Adaptive Composite (WAC-01)**; deliberately dreadful puns including **WAC-1-OFF**, **WAC Attack** (potential combat-protective uses), **forced WAC-ing**. No fully developed offensive weapon or licensed military product has yet been demonstrated; applications are brainstorming and faculty review. Vorr despairs of the jokes; Soreth is scientifically excited; Clarissa and Lucien enjoy them.
- **Current endpoint:** the joke about “forced WAC-ing,” after Vorr requests scientific naming discipline. Future continuation starts with examination hall materials research and faculty dynamics, not an automatically launched combat program.
